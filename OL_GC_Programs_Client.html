Warm-Up
⌄
Hip 90/90 mobility
8 each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Sit on the floor with both legs bent at 90° — one leg in front of you, one out to the side. Sit as tall as you can, hands lightly resting on the floor behind you for support.

Movement: Slowly rotate your hips and allow both legs to swing across to the opposite 90/90 position — the leg that was in front moves to the side, and the leg that was to the side comes in front. Think of it as winding and unwinding your hips. Pause briefly at each end position before rotating back. Keep the movement smooth and deliberate throughout.

Focus: Keep your back straight and hips level throughout. This is a slow, controlled movement — don't rush it or use momentum. The goal is to explore hip range, not just get through the reps.

⌄
Squat matrix
5 eachBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with good posture, feet together.

Movement: Squat with feet narrow, then shoulder-width, then wide. Then stagger your stance — one foot slightly ahead of the other — squat, then switch which foot is forward and repeat. Each stance offers a different feel and range.

Focus: Explore range in each stance. Tall spine, knees track over toes. Slow, controlled tempo.

10Light DBLight

Movement: Raise both arms into a Y shape, lifting a few centimetres. Hold briefly, lower with control.

Focus: Keep the movement small — quality over height. Lower back stays relaxed.

Main Circuit
How to run it
Circuit: do one set of each exercise in turn with minimal rest, then repeat the whole round 3 times. Aim for 10 to 12 reps on each. Your first move, the goblet squat, is the main strength lift: go a little heavier and stop around 8 to 10 reps.

Goblet squat
3 x 8-10KB/DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Movement: Press both dumbbells upward to full arm extension, allowing them to naturally travel slightly inward at the top. Lower under control.

KB swings

Movement: Hinge at the hips and hike the kettlebell back between your legs. Explosively snap your hips forward to drive the kettlebell up to shoulder height. Let it fall back and repeat.

Focus: Power comes entirely from your hip snap — not your arms. Think explosive hip extension, not shoulder raise.

Piriformis stretch
Hold 30s each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Cross one ankle over the opposite knee so your leg forms a figure-4 shape.

Movement: Gently press the top knee away from you until you feel a deep stretch in your buttock. Hold for the required time, then switch sides.

Focus: Keep your lower back flat on the floor throughout. Breathe steadily and let the stretch develop gradually — don't force it.

⌄
Cross-body shoulder stretch
Hold 30s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand or sit tall with good posture. Bring one arm straight across your chest.

Movement: Use your opposite hand to gently hold your elbow and pull your arm a bit closer to your body. You should feel a stretch in the back of your shoulder. Breathe steadily and hold for the prescribed time, then switch arms.

Focus: Keep both shoulders down and relaxed — don't let your shoulders hunch up toward your ears.
