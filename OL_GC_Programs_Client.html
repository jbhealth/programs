⌄
Hip 90/90 mobility
8 each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Sit on the floor with both legs bent at 90° — one leg in front of you, one out to the side. Sit as tall as you can, hands lightly resting on the floor behind you for support.

Focus: Keep your back straight and hips level throughout. This is a slow, controlled movement — don't rush it or use momentum. The goal is to explore hip range, not just get through the reps.

Movement: Slowly rotate your hips and allow both legs to swing across to the opposite 90/90 position — the leg that was in front moves to the side, and the leg that was to the side comes in front. Think of it as winding and unwinding your hips. Pause briefly at each end position before rotating back. Keep the movement smooth and deliberate throughout.

Squat matrix
5 eachBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with good posture, feet together.

Focus: Explore range in each stance. Tall spine, knees track over toes. Slow, controlled tempo.

Movement: Squat with feet narrow, then shoulder-width, then wide. Then stagger your stance — one foot slightly ahead of the other — squat, then switch which foot is forward and repeat. Each stance offers a different feel and range.

Movement: Gently press the top knee away from you until you feel a deep stretch in your buttock. Hold for the required time, then switch sides.
