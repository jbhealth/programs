How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move on to the next. Three times a week, on days apart. Your physio exercises come first and this sits alongside them. Box squat: sit to a high chair, with a cushion on the seat if the knee needs it. Good mornings: hug a cushion to your chest for now, no weight. Kickbacks: hold the worktop, and start without the band. Do the last two on the bed if getting down to the floor is hard right now. Bend the knee only as far as it is happy. Effort or a stretch is fine, but sharp pain means stop that exercise.

Box squat

Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box or chair, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Box height is set to suit, so work to the height given in the notes.

Movement: Push the hips back and sit down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall by driving through the heels. Control the descent all the way down.

Focus: Keep the knees pushed out in line with the middle toes and the chest up as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping onto it, and drive up through the heels.

Good mornings – hugged
2 sets10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, knees soft, hugging a weight to your chest with both arms, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and hinge forward with a flat back until the hamstrings load, then drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: The weight sits high on the chest, so the back works hard to stay flat. Hinge at the hips, not the waist, and only go as far as that flat back allows.

Glute kickback – band
2 sets10 each sideBand/AnchorLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Loop a band around your ankles or hold it under the standing foot. Stand tall, hands on a support for balance, slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Push one leg back against the band using the glute, keeping it fairly straight, then return with control.

Focus: Squeeze the glute at the back and keep the movement at the hip. Don't arch the lower back or swing the leg to cheat the range.

Hip abduction – side-lying
2 sets10 each sideMatLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, legs straight and stacked, hips square, head supported, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Raise the top leg up to the side, keeping it straight and in line with the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the leg in line with the body and the hips stacked, not rolling back. Lead with the heel, keep it slow, and feel the outer hip.

Glute bridge – bilateral
3 sets10 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart, close enough that your fingertips brush your heels.

Movement: Drive through your heels and push your hips towards the ceiling until knees, hips and shoulders form a straight line. Squeeze the glutes, hold two seconds, then lower slowly.

Focus: Lift with the glutes, not by arching the lower back. Keep the ribs down and the core braced. Stop at a straight line, don't push on into the back.

How to
