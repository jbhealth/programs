Massage gun release – lower back and glutes
3 sets30 secMassage gunBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.

Goblet squat and reach
3 sets10-12 repsAny kit2.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat and, at the bottom, reach the weight forward to help you sit upright, then draw it back to the chest and stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Use the reach to stay upright rather than tipping forward, and squat only as deep as the knees allow.

Romanian deadlift – BB
3 sets8-10 repsBarbell5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

Leg press – single leg
3 sets8-10 repsLeg press machine8.25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit in the leg press with one foot centred on the platform, the other resting aside, hips and back settled, core braced.

Movement: Lower the platform under control on the one leg to a comfortable bend, then press back up without locking the knee hard.

Focus: Keep the working knee tracking in line with the toes, never caving in, and the hip flat on the pad. Control the depth to what the knee allows and keep the safety catches set.

Step-ups – supported
3 sets8-10 each sideStep boxBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand facing a step or box, feet hip-width, with one hand on a wall, rail or sturdy support beside you.

Movement: Place one foot fully on the box, drive up through that heel to stand tall on top, then step back down with control. Complete the reps, then swap sides.

Focus: Use the support for balance only, not to pull yourself up. Keep the stepping knee tracking over the foot and drive through the heel, then lower with control rather than dropping, keeping the hips level.

ISO single leg deadlift – supported
3 sets30s each sideDBModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Hip abduction – cable, side step
3 sets10-12 repsCable machineModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a cuff or band low around one ankle attached to a side cable, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step the working leg out to the side against the resistance, then step it back under control.

Focus: Keep the torso tall and the hips level, stepping from the outer hip, not leaning. Keep the steps controlled, and the working knee soft.

Hip adduction machine – slow eccentric
3 sets10-12 repsHip adduction machineModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the machine, inner thighs against the pads, legs apart, back supported.

Movement: Squeeze the thighs together, then return slowly over three to four seconds under control.

Focus: Squeeze together smoothly and control the slow return, going only to a comfortable stretch. Keep the back settled, and don't let the pads pull the legs wide fast.

Dorsiflexion – banded eccentric
3 sets10-12 repsBand/AnchorLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit with the leg out in front and a band around the forefoot, anchored ahead of you. Use a heavier band than usual.

Movement: Pull the foot up towards the shin, using the other hand or foot to help if needed, then lower slowly against the band.

Focus: The lowering is the work. Take three or four seconds and resist the whole way. Because you help the foot up, the band can be heavier than the shin could lift alone.
