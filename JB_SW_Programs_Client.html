Goblet squat
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Goblet squat and reach
3 sets10 repsAny kit5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat and, at the bottom, reach the weight forward to help you sit upright, then draw it back to the chest and stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Use the reach to stay upright rather than tipping forward, and squat only as deep as the knees allow.

Romanian deadlift – staggered stance
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Reminder to squeeze your shoulder blades back throughout.

Start: One foot flat, the other half a step back on its toes. Weight in front of the thighs, soft bend in the front knee.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front leg until the hamstring loads, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. Swap legs halfway.

Focus: Most of the weight sits on the front leg, the back foot is only there for balance. Back flat, hips square, not twisting open.

Seated cable row – close neutral grip
3 sets10 repsCable row machine19.25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Remember the 3 stage movement - shoulder blades first

Start: Sit at the low cable, feet braced, a neutral close handle, arms extended, chest up, back tall.

Movement: Pull the handle to the lower abdomen, elbows driving back close to the body, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Drive the elbows back and squeeze the shoulder blades, keeping the back tall and still. Keep the shoulders down, and control the return without rocking.

Knee extension – cable
3 sets10 each sideCable machine1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

Pallof press
3 sets8 each sideCable machine/Band7kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

Hip abduction – clamshell
3 sets10 each sideBand/MatLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, hips and knees bent, feet together, hips stacked, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Keeping the feet together, open the top knee up like a clam, then lower under control.

Focus: Open only as far as the hips stay stacked, not rolling the top hip back. Keep the movement slow, and feel the outer hip and glute work.

Glute bridge – single leg
3 sets10 repsMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with the foot flat, the other leg extended straight out and held off the floor.

Movement: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to push your hips up until the body is level. Hold two seconds, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Push with the glute, not the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill

Start: Half kneel or stand with one foot forward, flat on the floor, toes pointing straight ahead.

Movement: Gently drive the knee forward, in line with the middle toes, keeping the heel down, to a comfortable stretch at the front of the ankle, then return.

Focus: Keep the heel firmly down and the knee in line with the middle toes, moving forward only to a comfortable range. Move slowly, keep the foot pointing straight, and don't force the stretch.

Calf raises – eccentric heel drop

Start: Balls of both feet on the floor or the edge of a step, holding a support.

Movement: Rise onto the toes on both feet, lift one off, then lower the other heel slowly over three to four seconds. Foot down, rise on both again. One side at a time.

Focus: The slow lowering does the work, so give it the full count and do not let the heel drop away. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out.

2 x 8 each side · Bodyweight
Calf raises – single leg

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on the floor or a step edge, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Balance – single leg free

Start: Stand on one leg without support, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to help balance.

Movement: Hold the single-leg balance freely for the set time, then swap.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, fixing the eyes on a point ahead. Make small ankle adjustments to stay steady, and keep the core engaged.

Single leg deadlift – bodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Single leg deadlift – supported

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready, with light fingertip support on a wall, chair back or rail beside you.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return. Use light fingertip support only - enough to steady you, not to hold you up. Take the hand away when you feel steady, not before.

Step-down – single leg

Start: Stand on top of a low step on one leg, the other foot hovering off the edge, arms out for balance.

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, lightly tap, then drive back up to stand tall on the one leg.

Focus: Keep the standing knee tracking over the foot, never caving in, and the hips level throughout. Control the lower down slowly, and keep the movement in the standing leg, not by reaching with the free foot.

ISO split squat – bodyweight

Start: Stand in a split stance, one foot forward and one back, weight balanced, torso tall, hands at the chest.

Movement: Lower straight down until both knees are bent towards right angles and hold that bottom position still for the set time.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the torso upright through the hold. Keep the weight balanced between both legs, and hold only at a depth the knees tolerate.

Hip abduction – standing band

Start: Stand with a band around the ankles or lower legs, holding a support, torso tall.

Movement: Take the working leg out to the side against the band, keeping it straight, then return under control.

Focus: Keep the leg straight and the torso tall, not leaning to lift, and the hips level. Lead with the heel, keep it slow, and feel the outer hip.

Curtsey lunge – bodyweight

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, hands at the chest for balance.

Movement: Step one foot back and across behind the other into a curtsey, lowering under control, then drive back up to standing. Keep the hips fairly square.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving inward, and lower only as far as control and knee comfort allow. Keep the chest up and the hips square rather than twisting.

Side plank – feet stacked

Start: Lie on your side, forearm down with the elbow under the shoulder, legs straight and feet stacked.

Movement: Lift the hips so the body forms a straight line from head to heels and hold for the set time.

Focus: Keep the hips high and the body in one straight line, resisting the sag towards the floor. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow and the neck long, and stop when the line drops.

Squat – TRX

Start: Face the anchor holding the handles lightly at chest height, feet hip to shoulder-width, arms fairly straight.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, using the straps only for light balance, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Use the straps for balance, not to pull yourself up, and go only as deep as your knees stay comfortable.

Single leg squat – TRX

Start: Hold the TRX handles, stand on one leg with the other lifted in front, standing foot planted, light tension on the straps.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down on the one leg, using the straps for balance and assistance, then drive up through the heel to stand.

Focus: Keep the working knee in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the hips level. Use the straps to share the load, keep the heel down, and control the depth.
