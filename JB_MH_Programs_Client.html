Squat & Push
Back squat – high bar
3 × 5 · heavyBarbell85kg

Start: Place the barbell high on your traps close to the base of your neck, feet shoulder-width.

Focus: High bar position requires a more upright torso than low bar. Keep your chest tall and knees tracking over toes.

Movement: Squat to parallel or below with an upright torso. Drive through your heels to stand.

Barbell bench press
3 × 6-8Barbell52.5kg

Start: Lie flat on a bench with eyes under the bar. Grip the bar just wider than shoulder-width, feet flat on the floor. Retract and depress your shoulder blades into the bench.

Focus: Keep your shoulder blades pinched together throughout. Lower with control — don't let the bar bounce off your chest. Maintain a slight arch in your lower back.

Movement: Unrack the bar and lower it in a slight arc to your lower chest. Touch lightly, then press back up and slightly back toward the rack. Lock out at the top without losing tension.

Bulgarian split squat
3 × 8 eachBench10kg x 2 Plates

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Dips – slow negative
3 × 8Dip barsBanded

Start: Grip the dip bars with arms straight. Use assistance for the concentric (up) phase.

Focus: The slow negative builds eccentric strength — the most effective stimulus for dip strength development.

Movement: Lower yourself over 4–5 seconds to the bottom position. Use assistance on the way back up if needed.

Deadlift & Pull
Barbell deadlift
4 × 4 · heavyBarbellweight

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell over mid-foot. Hinge at the hips and grip the bar just outside your legs with a neutral or double overhand grip.

Focus: Keep your back flat and chest up throughout. Drive your feet through the floor — this is a push, not a pull. Bar stays close to your body the entire way.

Movement: Take a deep breath and brace your core. Drive through your heels, extending hips and knees simultaneously until you're standing tall. Lower with control by hinging at the hips first, then bending the knees as the bar passes them.

Romanian deadlift to shrug
3 × 8 · moderateBarbellweight

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Focus: The shrug at the top should be explosive. Feel your traps and posterior chain working together.

Movement: Perform a Romanian deadlift, then at the top add an explosive shoulder shrug before lowering back down.

Pull-ups
3 × maxPull-up barweight

Start: Hang from a pull-up bar with hands just wider than shoulder-width, palms facing away. Arms fully extended, shoulders engaged — don't just hang passively.

Focus: Initiate with your shoulder blades — pull them down and back before bending your elbows. Think about driving your elbows toward your hips.

Movement: Pull your chest toward the bar by driving elbows down. Chin clears the bar at the top. Lower with full control back to a dead hang. Avoid swinging or kipping.

DB single-arm press
3 × 8 eachDumbbellweight

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Reverse lunge – BB
3 × 8 eachBarbellweight

Start: Place the barbell across your upper traps with feet hip-width apart.

Focus: Front knee tracks over your ankle. Reverse lunge places less stress on the knee than forward lunge.

Movement: Step back into a lunge, lowering the rear knee toward the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

ISO Copenhagen plank
3 × 20-30s eachBenchweight

Start: Lie on your side with your top foot or shin resting on a bench. Set up a side plank with your bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Build time progressively — start with shin on bench, progress to ankle. Keep your body in a straight line throughout.

Movement: Hold the position for the prescribed time with no hip drop. This isometric exercise loads both the adductors and lateral core.
