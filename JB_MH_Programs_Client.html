Anderson squat
3 x 5Barbell/Rack50kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the safety pins in the rack so the bar rests at a squat depth. Get under the bar on the pins, brace hard, chest up.

Movement: From a dead stop at the bottom, drive up through the heels to stand tall, then lower back to rest on the pins and reset before each rep.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the chest up from the very first drive out of the bottom. Brace hard before each rep so the back stays flat, and don't bounce off the pins.

Single arm overhead press
3 x 8 eachDB/KB10kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall, a weight at one shoulder, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced against the offset load.

Movement: Press the weight overhead until the arm is straight, then lower back to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist leaning away from the weight, keeping the torso upright. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and press within a comfortable range.

Start: Take a wide stance, toes out, bar over mid-foot. Sit the hips back and down to grip inside the legs, arms straight, chest up, flat back, shoulders over the bar.

Wall squat – single leg
3 x 30s eachWallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your back flat against a wall, feet a little way out from it. Take one foot off the floor and hold it there, standing on the other leg.

Movement: Slide down the wall by bending the standing knee to about 45 degrees, hold for a slow count of five, then push back up the wall to standing.

Focus: The wall takes the balance out of it so all the attention goes on the knee. Keep the knee tracking over the second toe on the way down and the way up, and stop short of any depth where it drifts inwards. Work in a range you own rather than chasing depth.
