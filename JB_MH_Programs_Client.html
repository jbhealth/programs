Back squat – tempo
3 x 5BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Bar on the upper back, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, braced hard, standing tall after unracking.

Movement: Lower slowly over three to four seconds to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand. Keep tension the whole way down.

Focus: Hold the knees tracking over the toes and the chest up through the slow descent, where control matters most. Keep the heels planted and the back flat, and don't let the knees cave as you fatigue.

⌄
Bench press – paused
3 x 5Barbell/Bench~50kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on the bench, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, grip a little wider than shoulders, unrack over the chest.

Movement: Lower the bar to the mid-chest and pause for one to two seconds, still, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: Hold the pause on the chest with the shoulder blades set and the elbows tucked, not flared. Keep the feet planted and the wrists stacked, and press up without bouncing off the pause.

⌄
Barbell seal row
3 x 8-10Barbell/Elevated benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie chest-down on a raised bench, a barbell hanging at arms' length below, chest supported, core relaxed.

Movement: Pull the bar up towards the bench, driving the elbows back, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbows and squeeze the shoulder blades, letting the bench keep the back still and supported. Keep the shoulders down, and control the lower without swinging.

⌄
Band face pull – seated
3 x 15BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit tall, a band anchored in front at head height, a handle in each hand, arms extended forward, ribs down.

Movement: Pull the band towards the face, drawing the hands to the sides of the head with the elbows high, then return under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbows high and squeeze the shoulder blades, not shrugging up. Keep the ribs down and the neck relaxed, and control the return.

Suitcase carry
3 x 20m eachDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall with a weight in one hand at the side like a suitcase, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso upright and level.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the weighted side, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the ribs down. Take steady steps, brace hard, and don't let the weight swing.

⌄
Band monster walk – diagonal
3 x 10 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and outward alternately, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees driving out, not caving. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips working each step.

⌄
Copenhagen plank – short lever
3 x 20s eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your side with the top leg's inner knee resting on a bench, forearm down, elbow under the shoulder.

Movement: Lift the hips and the bottom leg so the body forms a straight line, supported by the knee on the bench, and hold.

Focus: Keep the hips lifted and level, not sagging, and the body in one line. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow, and hold only as long as the line holds.

Main Circuit
⌄
Deadlift – BB
3 x 5Barbell~135kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with feet hip-width, the bar over mid-foot. Hinge down to grip just outside your legs, shins to the bar, chest up, back flat, shoulders slightly ahead of the bar, core braced hard.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, keeping the bar dragging close to your legs, hips and shoulders rising together. Finish standing tall with the glutes, then hinge the hips back and lower the bar under control down the same line.

Focus: The back stays flat from floor to lockout, never rounded. Take the load through the legs and hips and keep the bar close to the body throughout. Don't jerk the bar or lean back at the top, just stand tall.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat – BB
3 x 8 eachBarbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Barbell across the upper back, stand a stride in front of a bench with the back foot resting on it, torso tall, core braced.

Movement: Lower by bending the front knee until the thigh is towards parallel, then drive up through the front heel to the start.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the weight through the front leg with the core braced. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the knee stays comfortable.

⌄
Barbell Z-press
3 x 6-8BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on the floor, legs straight out in front, a barbell at the shoulders, sitting tall, ribs down, core braced hard.

Movement: Press the bar overhead until the arms are straight, then lower to the shoulders under control while staying upright.

Focus: Keep sitting tall with the ribs down, not leaning back or arching, which the seated position exposes. Keep the shoulders down, and press within a comfortable overhead range.

⌄
Bent-over row – BB underhand
3 x 8-10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back holding a barbell underhand at arms' length, chest up, hips back, core braced.

Movement: Pull the bar towards the lower abdomen, driving the elbows back close to the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hinge steady, not rounding as you row. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and keep the wrists straight.

⌄
Band hamstring curl – standing
3 x 12 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand holding a support, a band around one ankle anchored in front, the working leg straight back, torso tall.

Movement: Curl the heel towards the buttock by bending the knee against the band, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the thigh fairly still and move at the knee, curling the heel up. Keep the torso tall and the lower back neutral, and control the lower.

⌄
Balance – single leg eyes closed
3 x 30s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg near a support, torso tall, other foot lifted, then close the eyes.

Movement: Hold the single-leg balance with the eyes closed for the set time, then swap.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, making constant small ankle adjustments without sight. Keep the core engaged, breathe, and keep support within reach.

⌄
Ab rollout – partial
3 x 8Ab wheel/BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Kneel with a wheel or barbell in front of you, hands on it, core braced, back neutral.

Movement: Roll the wheel forward a short way, extending the body, then pull back to the start using the core. Keep the range partial.

Focus: Keep the back flat and neutral, never letting it sag as you roll out, this is where the back is at risk. Brace hard, keep the range short, and only extend as far as you can hold the neutral spine.
