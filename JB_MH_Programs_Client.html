Rack pull
3 sets6-10 repsBarbell/Rack90kg

Start: Set the bar on rack pins at about knee height. Feet hip-width, grip just outside the legs, chest up, flat back, shoulders over the bar, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the legs and hips to stand the bar up tall, keeping it close to the body, then lower it back to the pins under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the bar close throughout. Finish by standing tall with the glutes, don't lean back or over-arch at the top. Take the load through the hips and legs.

2 sets8-10 each sideAny kit2.5kg

Movement: Sit the hips down keeping the weight stacked overhead and the arm locked, to a comfortable depth, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the weight over the mid-foot with a locked active arm, the chest up, and the knees in line with the middle toes. Keep the hips and shoulders level, and reduce depth if the torso tips or the arm drifts.

Hip thrust – single leg
3 sets8-12 each sideBenchBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute doing the work, and keep the ribs down so the lower back stays out of it.

Deficit deadlift
3 sets8-10 repsPlate/Barbell90kg

Focus: The extra depth makes a flat back harder to hold, so keep the chest up and don't let the back round at the bottom. Take the load through the legs and hips and keep the bar close throughout.

Focus: Keep the back flat and parallel throughout, never rising or rounding as you pull. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and reset the bar on the floor between reps.
