Back squat – tempo
3 x 5BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Barbell across your upper traps, feet shoulder-width apart.

Movement: Perform the squat using the prescribed tempo, typically 3 seconds down, pause at the bottom, 3 seconds up.

Focus: The prescribed tempo removes momentum and forces perfect technique throughout. Control every centimetre.

⌄
Bench press – paused
3 x 5Barbell/BenchHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on bench, feet flat. Retract shoulder blades, grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.

Movement: Lower to mid-chest, pause 2–3 seconds with full tension. Drive explosively back to extension.

Focus: Pause eliminates stretch reflex, every rep starts from zero. Full tension throughout pause.

⌄
Barbell seal row
3 x 8-10Barbell/Elevated benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie prone on an elevated bench with the barbell hanging below. Grip with a shoulder-width overhand grip, arms straight.

Movement: Pull the bar to the bench by driving your elbows back. Squeeze your shoulder blades at the top. Lower under full control.

Focus: Keep your chest on the bench throughout, no body swing. This is a strict row with zero lower back involvement.

Band face pull – seated
3 x 15BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on the floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Movement: Pull band toward your face. Seated position prevents cheating with momentum.

Focus: Drive elbows wide and back. Squeeze with high elbows at the end.

Suitcase carry
3 x 20m eachDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Movement: Step diagonally forward and to the side, alternating directions.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

Main Circuit
⌄
Deadlift – BB
3 x 5BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand over the bar with feet hip-width apart and shins close to it. Hinge down, grip the bar, flat back and core braced.

Movement: Push the floor away to stand, locking out fully at the top with hips driven through.

Focus: Drive the floor away with your heels. Hips and bar rise together, don't let your hips shoot up early.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat – BB
3 x 8 eachBarbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place the barbell on your upper traps with your rear foot elevated on a bench.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Focus: Front shin vertical and torso upright throughout. This is demanding single leg strength work under a significant load.

⌄
Barbell Z-press
3 x 6-8BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit cross-legged on the floor holding the barbell at shoulder height.

Movement: Press the barbell straight overhead to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Focus: Core braced throughout. Don't arch your lower back as you press, the seated position removes all leg drive.

⌄
Bent-over row – BB underhand
3 x 8-10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge forward with a flat back and underhand grip on the barbell.

Movement: Pull the bar toward your lower abdomen with elbows close. Squeeze at the top. Return under control.

Focus: Keep your elbows closer to your body than the overhand version. Feel your biceps engaged throughout.

⌄
Band hamstring curl – standing
3 x 12 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Anchor the band low in front of you and loop the other end around one ankle. Face the anchor, step back until the band is taut, and hold a wall or chair for balance.

Movement: Curl your heel up toward your glute against the band, then lower back down under control.

Focus: Keep your hip still and torso upright. Feel constant tension in the back of the thigh through the whole rep.

⌄
Balance – single leg eyes closed
3 x 30s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Establish a controlled single leg stance with eyes open first. Soft knee bend, tall spine.

Movement: Once you have solid eyes-open control, close your eyes and hold balance. Open if needed to reset. Build duration progressively.

Focus: Closing your eyes dramatically increases proprioceptive demand by removing the visual cue. Master eyes-open first.

⌄
Ab rollout – partial
3 x 8Ab wheel/BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Kneel on floor and grip an ab wheel or barbell with both hands.

Movement: Roll wheel forward to comfortable partial range, hips don't drop. Roll back under control.

Focus: Keep your lower back flat throughout. Feel your core bracing hard.
