Goblet squat and reach
2 sets10-12 repsAny kit5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat and, at the bottom, reach the weight forward to help you sit upright, then draw it back to the chest and stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Use the reach to stay upright rather than tipping forward, and squat only as deep as the knees allow.

Deadlift – trap bar, staggered stance
2 sets8-10 each sideTrap bar30kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in the trap bar with the working foot flat under you and the other foot a half step back, up on its toes as a kickstand. Hinge down, grip the handles in the middle, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Push through the front foot and stand tall, then sit the hips back and lower the bar under control. Complete the set, then swap sides.

Focus: Most of the weight stays on the front heel, the back foot only balances. Keep the hips level and square, not twisting towards the back leg, and the back flat throughout.

Hip thrust – single leg
2 sets8-10 each sideBenchBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, one foot flat on the floor and roughly under the knee, the other leg lifted and held. Chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute doing the work, and keep the ribs down so the lower back stays out of it.

Single leg deadlift – with rotation
3 sets10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight, soft standing knee, the other leg ready to reach behind, core braced.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight while adding a small controlled rotation of the torso, then return to standing tall and square.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hips level, and let the rotation come from the upper body, small and controlled. Move slowly, balance and control matter more than depth.

Chest press – Swiss ball
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit/Swiss ball2 x 6kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie with the upper back on a Swiss ball, hips up in a bridge, a weight in each hand at chest height, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the hips up and the core braced to stay stable on the ball, and the shoulder blades set. Keep the elbows at about 45 degrees, and control the lower.

Cuban press
2 sets8-10 repsDB/Band2 x 3kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a light weight in each hand in front of the thighs, palms facing you.

Movement: Lift the elbows out to the sides to shoulder height, then turn the forearms up so the hands are above the elbows. Press the weights overhead, then reverse each stage slowly back down.

Focus: Use a light weight and move slowly through each stage, keeping the elbows at shoulder height before you turn. Stop short of any pinching at the top of the shoulder.

Straight leg raises
2 sets10-12 repsMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your back, legs straight, hands under the lower back or by the sides, ribs down.

Movement: Raise both straight legs towards the ceiling, then lower them slowly without letting the lower back arch.

Focus: Keep the lower back gently pressed down throughout, and stop lowering the moment it wants to arch. Move slowly, and bend the knees a little if the back lifts.

Hockey ball release – glute
How to
3 mins

Start: Sit on the floor with a hockey ball under one buttock, hands behind you for support. Cross that ankle over the opposite knee to open the hip, and lower your weight on gradually.

Movement: Lean into a tender spot and hold until you feel it release, usually thirty to sixty seconds. When it lets go, shift and find another. Two or three spots per side.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, not pain. Hold still rather than rolling about, and take weight back through your hands if it is too much. If you get anything shooting, electric or numb down the leg you are on the nerve, so move off that spot straight away.
