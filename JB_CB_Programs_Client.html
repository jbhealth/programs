Program 1 — Strength & Wrist Rehab
WC 1st June 2026
Barbell deadlift
2 × 8Barbell

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell over mid-foot. Hinge at the hips and grip the bar just outside your legs with a neutral or double overhand grip.

Focus: Keep your back flat and chest up throughout. Drive your feet through the floor — this is a push, not a pull. Bar stays close to your body the entire way.

Movement: Take a deep breath and brace your core. Drive through your heels, extending hips and knees simultaneously until you're standing tall. Lower with control by hinging at the hips first, then bending the knees as the bar passes them.

Dumbbell squat
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

Focus: Keep chest tall and torso upright. Knees track over toes.

Movement: Squat to depth under control. Drive through heels to stand.

Single arm DB row – incline
2 × 10 each sideDumbbell

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Seated row – machine
2 × 10Machine

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

Lateral raises – DB
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides with a slight bend in elbows.

Focus: Keep shoulders pressed down — don't shrug. Lead with elbows.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lower slowly under control.

Back extension machine – bilateral
2 × 10Machine

Start: Set up on the back extension machine with hips on the pad.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Keep your lower back neutral throughout.

Movement: Lower your torso, extend back to neutral.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance – single leg free
2 × 30 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Wrist rotations with light DB (Pronation/Supination)
2 × 12 each directionDumbbell

Start: Sit or stand with your elbow bent at 90° and tucked into your side. Hold a very light dumbbell in one hand with your palm facing up.

Focus: Keep your elbow still and close to your body throughout — all movement comes from the forearm rotating at the wrist. Use a weight that allows full, pain-free range.

Movement: Slowly rotate your forearm to turn your palm face down (pronation), then back to face up (supination). Move through the full comfortable range. Complete all reps then switch hands.

Reverse crunches
2 × 12Mat

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.

Bird-dog
2 × 10 each sideMat

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

ISO side plank
2 × 20-30 sec eachMat

Start: Set up in a full side plank position.

Focus: Keep your body in straight line head to heels. Don't let hips rotate back.

Movement: Hold for prescribed time. Build progressively: 30s, then 45s, then 60s.
