Pull-ups – band assisted
3 x 8Pull-up bar/Band40kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up with assistance from a band or partner, hands wider than shoulders on the bar, arms extended, chest up.

Movement: Pull yourself up until the chin nears the bar, driving the elbows down, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbows and draw the shoulder blades down, not shrugging. Control the lower fully, and use enough assistance to keep the movement clean.

Back squat – BB
2 x 10Barbell/Rack25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the bar across the upper back on the traps, hands gripping evenly, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Brace the core hard and unrack, stepping back to a stable stance.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down, bending the knees, until the thighs reach a comfortable depth, keeping the chest up and the bar over mid-foot. Drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking out over the toes, never caving in, and the heels planted throughout. Keep the chest up and the core braced so the back stays flat, and take depth only to where you keep good form and comfortable knees.

⌄
Romanian deadlift – BB
3 x 10Barbell25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

DB single-arm press
2 x 10 eachDumbbell7kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand or sit tall, a dumbbell at one shoulder, ribs down, core braced against the offset load.

Movement: Press the dumbbell overhead until the arm is straight, then lower back to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist leaning away from the weight, staying upright. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and press within a comfortable range.

⌄
Bent-over row – BB underhand
3 x 10Barbell15kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back holding a barbell underhand at arms' length, chest up, hips back, core braced.

Movement: Pull the bar towards the lower abdomen, driving the elbows back close to the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hinge steady, not rounding as you row. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and keep the wrists straight.

Hip abduction machine
3 x 12Hip abduction machine35.75kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit at the machine, the outer thighs against the pads, back supported, feet on the rests.

Movement: Push the thighs apart against the pads, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Push apart smoothly and control the return, not letting the pads snap the legs together. Keep the back settled and the movement steady, feeling the outer hips.

⌄
Pallof press – standing lunge
3 x 10 each sideCable/BandRed band
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height in a split stance, one foot forward in a shallow lunge, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to twist you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square over the split stance, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable. The lunge stance narrows your base, so the anti-rotation job gets harder. Keep the ribs down and press smoothly.

Hip thrust – BB

Start: Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench, a padded barbell across the front of your hips. Feet flat, hip-width, shins vertical when the hips are up. Tuck the chin slightly and brace the core.

Movement: Drive through your heels and push the hips up until the thighs are level and the body makes a straight line from knees to shoulders. Squeeze the glutes hard at the top, hold, then lower with control.

Focus: Finish the lift with the glutes at a straight line, don't over-arch the lower back to gain height. Keep the ribs down and the chin tucked so the movement stays in the hips, not the back. Shins stay roughly vertical at the top.

Farmer's carries

Start: Stand tall holding a heavy weight in each hand at the sides, feet hip-width, ribs down, shoulders back.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and steady.

Focus: Stand tall with the ribs down and shoulders back, not letting the weights pull you into a slump. Take steady steps, brace the core, and keep the weights from swinging.

Wall push-up

Start: Stand facing a wall, hands flat on it at shoulder height and width, arms straight, body in a line leaning in slightly.

Movement: Bend the elbows to bring the chest towards the wall, then press back to straight arms, keeping the body in a line.
