Barbell deadlift
2 × 8Barbell45kg

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell over mid-foot. Hinge at the hips and grip the bar just outside your legs with a neutral or double overhand grip.

Focus: Keep your back flat and chest up throughout. Drive your feet through the floor — this is a push, not a pull. Bar stays close to your body the entire way.

Movement: Take a deep breath and brace your core. Drive through your heels, extending hips and knees simultaneously until you're standing tall. Lower with control by hinging at the hips first, then bending the knees as the bar passes them.

Goblet squat
2 × 10Kettlebell12kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Romanian deadlift – BB
2 × 8Barbell25kg

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Seated row – machine
2 × 10Seated row machine24.75kg

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

Bulgarian split squat
2 × 8 eachBenchBodyweight

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Calf raises – bilateral
2 × 12Stepweight

Start: Stand tall, feet flat on the floor or edge of a step.

Focus: Go through the full range — let heels dip below the step on the way down.

Movement: Rise up onto the balls of your feet as high as possible. Lower slowly through full range.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCableweight

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Bird-dog
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.
