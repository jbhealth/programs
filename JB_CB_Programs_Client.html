Romanian deadlift – BB
3 x 8Barbell30kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

⌄
Overhead squat – single arm DB
2 x 10 eachDumbbell2kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a single dumbbell locked out overhead in one hand, arm fully extended, feet shoulder-width apart.

Movement: Squat to depth while keeping the dumbbell directly over the shoulder. Drive through the heels to return to standing.

Focus: Keep the arm vertical and the chest up throughout. Brace the core to resist leaning toward the loaded side.

⌄
Glute bridges – single leg
10 eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with foot flat, other leg extended.

Movement: Drive through the bent knee heel to push hips up. Hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

Focus: Keep hips level, don't let one side drop.

⌄
DB single-arm press
3 x 10 eachDumbbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

⌄
ViPR – log roll or carries
3 x ~20mViPR4kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold the ViPR horizontally in both hands with a braced core and tall posture.

Movement: Carry the ViPR for prescribed distance or time. Vary the carry style — at waist height, on the shoulder, or overhead. Each position creates a different stability challenge.

Focus: Keep your ribs down and spine tall throughout. Feel the offset weight challenging your core stability.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 eachDumbbell4kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
Farmer's carries
3 x 30sKB/DB16kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding a heavy kettlebell or dumbbell in each hand at your sides.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance or time. Keep everything tight — this is a full body stability and strength exercise.

Focus: Torso perfectly upright, shoulders back, core braced. Take deliberate, controlled steps.

⌄
Wall push-up
3 x 10BodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand facing a wall with arms extended, hands on the wall at shoulder height and width.

Movement: Bend the elbows to bring the chest toward the wall, then press back to the start.

Focus: Keep a straight line from head to heels with the core gently braced. A wrist-friendly way to build pressing strength.
