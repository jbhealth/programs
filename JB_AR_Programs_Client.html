Zercher squat

Start: Hold the bar in the crooks of your elbows against the body, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced hard, chest tall.

Movement: Sit the hips down with an upright torso to a comfortable depth, keeping the bar hugged in, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the elbows and chest up so the torso stays tall, and the knees in line with the middle toes. Keep the core braced hard to protect the back, and control depth to protect the knees.

Good mornings – BB
2 sets8-10 repsBarbell/Rack10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Rest the bar across the upper back as for a squat, feet hip-width, soft bend in the knees, core braced hard.

Movement: Push the hips back and hinge the torso forward with a flat back until you feel the hamstrings, then drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, this is the whole point of the lift. Hinge at the hips, not the waist, and only go as far as the flat back allows. Keep a soft knee and don't round the lower back.

Incline bench press – BB
2 sets8-10 repsBarbell/Incline bench/Rack10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on an incline bench, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, grip a little wider than shoulders, unrack over the upper chest.

Movement: Lower the bar under control to the upper chest, elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set back and down and the elbows tucked, not flared, to protect the shoulders. Keep the feet planted, lower under control, and don't bounce the bar.

Front squat

Start: Rest the bar across the front of the shoulders, elbows lifted high to make a shelf, hands lightly guiding the bar, feet shoulder-width, core braced hard.

Movement: Sit the hips down with an upright torso, elbows kept high, to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the elbows up so the chest stays tall and the bar secure, and the knees tracking over the toes. Keep the heels down and the core braced, and control depth to protect the knees and back.
