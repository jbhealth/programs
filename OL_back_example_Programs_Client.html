Goblet squat
3 sets8 repsAny kit14kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Romanian deadlift
3 sets8 repsAny kit12kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Stop when the left hamstring catches, not when the weight touches the floor.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, a dumbbell in each hand resting in front of your thighs, knees softly bent, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the dumbbells down the front of your legs, keeping them close, until you feel the hamstrings stretch. Drive the hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the dumbbells close throughout. Hinge at the hips, don't squat or round the back. Go only as low as your flat back allows.

Pallof press – band, half kneeling
3 sets10 each sideBandLight band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Half kneel side-on to a band at chest height, inside knee down, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the band straight out, resisting the rotational pull, hold briefly, then return to the chest.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square and the torso tall, resisting any twist towards the band. Keep the core braced and the back neutral, not arching, and press with control.

Suitcase carry
3 sets25m each sideAny kit14kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Don't hitch the hip.

Start: Stand tall with a weight in one hand at the side like a suitcase, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso upright and level.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the weighted side, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the ribs down. Take steady steps, brace hard, and don't let the weight swing.

Glute bridges – single leg
3 sets8 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Squeeze before you lift.

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with the foot flat, the other leg extended straight out and held off the floor.

Movement: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to push your hips up until the body is level. Hold two seconds, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Push with the glute, not the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

Dead bug – arms only
2 sets10 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent up over the hips, arms reaching towards the ceiling, lower back gently pressed into the floor.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead towards the floor, keeping the back flat, then return and swap arms.

Focus: Keep the lower back pressed to the floor throughout, this is the whole point. Move slowly, and if the back starts to arch, reduce the reach.

Main Circuit
Step-ups – lateral
3 sets8 each sideStep boxBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a medium step, the near foot ready to step onto it.

Movement: Place the near foot on the step, drive up through that heel to stand tall, then lower back down with control. Complete the reps, then swap sides.

Focus: Keep the stepping knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the hips level. Drive through the heel and control the lower rather than pushing off the trailing foot.

Single-leg deadlift – weighted
3 sets6 each sideAny kit6kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the dumbbell lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Crab walks – band
3 sets12 each wayBandMedium band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Toes forward.

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, in a slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and out one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, then continue for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees pushing out, not caving in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips and glutes work.

Rack carry
3 sets25m each sideAny kit10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Don't hitch your shoulder.

Start: Stand tall with a kettlebell racked at one shoulder, elbow tucked, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and steady.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist leaning away from the weight, staying square. Keep the shoulder down and the elbow tucked, take steady steps, and brace the core.

Bird-dog
2 sets10 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

McGill curl-up – basic
2 sets8 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with the foot flat, the other leg straight, hands under the lower back, elbows down.

Movement: Lift the head and shoulders just slightly off the floor, keeping the neck neutral, hold briefly, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the lower back in its natural arch and lift only slightly, bracing the core rather than crunching. Keep the neck neutral and don't pull the chin to the chest.
