How to run it
One round: do each exercise once, in order, and you're done. Rest as you need between them.
Warm-Up
Ankle dorsiflexion drill
8 each sideWallBodyweight

Start: Half kneel or stand with one foot forward, flat on the floor, toes pointing straight ahead.

Movement: Gently drive the knee forward, in line with the middle toes, keeping the heel down, to a comfortable stretch at the front of the ankle, then return.

Focus: Keep the heel firmly down and the knee in line with the middle toes, moving forward only to a comfortable range. Move slowly, keep the foot pointing straight, and don't force the stretch.

Knee-to-chest stretch
Hold 20s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back, legs extended, relaxed.

Movement: Draw one knee up towards the chest, hold it gently with the hands, feel the stretch, then release and swap.

Focus: Draw the knee in only to a comfortable stretch, never forcing. Keep the other leg and the lower back relaxed, breathe, and ease off if it pinches.

Main Circuit
Goblet squat
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit~20kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Romanian deadlift
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit~24kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, holding a weight in front of your thighs, or hands empty, with a soft bend in the knees. Set the shoulders back and brace the core.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front of your legs, keeping it close, until you feel a stretch in the hamstrings. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, never let it round. The movement is a hip hinge, not a squat, and the weight stays close to your legs. Only go as low as you can keep the back flat.

Step-down – single leg, supported
2 sets6 each sideStep boxBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on a low step on one leg, the other foot hovering off the edge, fingertips on a chair back beside you.

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, tap lightly, then drive back up to stand tall.

Focus: Standing knee over the foot, never caving in, hips level. Keep the movement in the standing leg. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Calf raises – single leg
2 sets8 each sideStepBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on the floor or a step edge, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Hip abduction – clamshell
2 sets10 each sideBand/MatLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, hips and knees bent, feet together, hips stacked, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Keeping the feet together, open the top knee up like a clam, then lower under control.

Focus: Open only as far as the hips stay stacked, not rolling the top hip back. Keep the movement slow, and feel the outer hip and glute work.

Bird-dog
2 sets6 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

Cool-Down
Piriformis stretch
Hold 30s each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back, one ankle crossed over the opposite thigh, hands holding the supporting leg.

Movement: Gently draw the supporting leg towards the chest until you feel a stretch deep in the crossed hip, then hold.

Focus: Draw the leg in only to a comfortable stretch, keeping the crossed knee open. Keep the head and shoulders relaxed on the floor, breathe, and ease off if it pinches.

Supine hamstring stretch
Hold 30s each sideTowel/BandLight

Start: Lie on your back, one leg extended, the other lifted towards the ceiling, hands or a strap behind the thigh.

Movement: Gently draw the lifted leg towards you, keeping it fairly straight, until you feel a hamstring stretch, then hold.

Focus: Draw the leg in only to a comfortable stretch, keeping a slight knee bend if needed. Keep the lower back relaxed on the floor, breathe, and don't force the range.

Massage gun release – foot

Start: Sit with the foot crossed over the other knee, or resting on a low step. The foot has to be relaxed.

Movement: Work the gun slowly along the arch from the heel towards the ball of the foot, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Use the lowest setting and a soft head. Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the heel bone and the tops of the toes. Anything sharp or numb, come off that spot straight away.

Start: Sit on the floor with a hockey ball under one buttock, hands behind you. Cross that ankle over the opposite knee and lower your weight on gradually.

Movement: Lean into a tender spot and hold until it releases, thirty to sixty seconds. Two or three spots per side.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, not pain. Hold still rather than rolling about. Anything shooting, electric or numb down the leg is the nerve, so move off that spot straight away.

Leg swings – front to back

Start: Stand side-on to a wall with one hand on it, standing tall on the near leg.

Movement: Swing the outside leg forwards and back in an easy rhythm, a little higher each time, then swap sides.

Focus: Let the leg swing from the hip like a pendulum while the trunk stays tall and still. Start small and let the range grow as the hip loosens. Never kick.

Focus: Work the movement from the toes and arch of the foot, keeping the heel down. Move slowly and deliberately, feeling the foot muscles work.

Deadlift – BB

Start: Stand with feet hip-width, the bar over mid-foot. Hinge down to grip just outside your legs, shins to the bar, chest up, back flat, shoulders slightly ahead of the bar, core braced hard.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, keeping the bar dragging close to your legs, hips and shoulders rising together. Finish standing tall with the glutes, then hinge the hips back and lower the bar under control down the same line.

Focus: The back stays flat from floor to lockout, never rounded. Take the load through the legs and hips and keep the bar close to the body throughout. Don't jerk the bar or lean back at the top, just stand tall.

Golf ball release – foot

Start: Sit tall in a chair, or stand beside something solid to hold. Put a golf ball on the floor under the arch of one foot and ease your weight onto it gradually.

Movement: Press into a tender spot and hold until you feel it release, usually thirty to sixty seconds. When it lets go, find another. Two or three spots per foot is plenty.

Focus: Pressure, not pain. Settle onto the spot and stay still, because the tissue softens under a steady load rather than a rolling one. Breathe out slowly and let it go. Work the arch and the front of the heel, and ease off if the foot is sharply sore.
