How to run it
One round: do each exercise once, in order, and you're done. Rest as you need between them.
Main Circuit
Box squat
2 sets8-10 repsBoxBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box or chair, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Box height is set to suit, so work to the height given in the notes.

Movement: Push the hips back and sit down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall by driving through the heels. Control the descent all the way down.

Focus: Keep the knees pushed out in line with the middle toes and the chest up as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping onto it, and drive up through the heels.

Deadlift
2 sets8-10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with the weight on the floor between your feet, feet hip to shoulder-width. Push the hips back and hinge to take hold of it with both hands, back flat, chest up, shoulders over the weight.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall, pushing the hips forward and squeezing the glutes at the top. Hinge the hips back to lower the weight under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat from start to finish, never rounded. The power comes from the hips and legs, not the lower back or arms. Keep the weight close to your body on the way up and down.

Ankle inversion – band
2 sets12-15 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit with the legs out straight and a band round the inside of the working foot, with the other end fixed out to the side.

Movement: Turn the foot inward against the band, pause, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change feet.

Focus: Move only at the ankle, with the knee and leg staying still. Turn the sole in against the band slowly and lower back with the same control.

Hip internal rotation – seated, band
3 sets10-12 repsBand/ChairLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit tall on a bench or chair with the knees bent at 90 degrees and a band round the working ankle, fixed to the inside of that leg.

Movement: Swing the lower leg out to the side against the band, turning at the hip, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change legs.

Focus: Keep the knee still and the thigh on the seat, so the turn comes from the hip. Sit tall and move slowly in both directions. The foot moves out as the thigh turns in.

Single leg deadlift – supported
2 sets8-10 each sideBodyweightBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready, with light fingertip support on a wall, chair back or rail beside you.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return. Use light fingertip support only - enough to steady you, not to hold you up. Take the hand away when you feel steady, not before.

Ankle eversion – band
2 sets12-15 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit with the legs out straight, a loop band round both feet, or one end round the outside of the working foot with the other end fixed to the inside.

Movement: Turn the foot outward against the band, pause, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change feet.

Focus: Move only at the ankle, with the knee and leg staying still. Turn the sole out against the band slowly and lower back with the same control.

Elbow plank – leg lift
2 sets20sMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up in a forearm plank, straight line from head to heels, core braced.

Movement: Keeping the hips level, lift one foot a few centimetres off the floor, hold briefly, lower, then the other.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square as the leg lifts, no rotating or sagging. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and keep the lift small so the back stays neutral.

Goblet squat
How to
2 x 8-10Moderate

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Romanian deadlift
How to
2 x 8-10Moderate

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, holding a weight in front of your thighs, or hands empty, with a soft bend in the knees. Set the shoulders back and brace the core.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front of your legs, keeping it close, until you feel a stretch in the hamstrings. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, never let it round. The movement is a hip hinge, not a squat, and the weight stays close to your legs. Only go as low as you can keep the back flat.

How to

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, tap lightly, then drive back up to stand tall.

Focus: Standing knee over the foot, never caving in, hips level. Keep the movement in the standing leg. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Calf raises – single leg
How to
2 x 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on the floor or a step edge, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

How to

Movement: Keeping the feet together, open the top knee up like a clam, then lower under control.

Focus: Open only as far as the hips stay stacked, not rolling the top hip back. Keep the movement slow, and feel the outer hip and glute work.

Bird-dog
How to
2 x 6 each sideBodyweight

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

How to
60-90s each footBodyweight

Golf ball release – foot
How to
2-3 spots each footBodyweight

Start: Sit tall in a chair, or stand beside something solid to hold. Put a golf ball on the floor under the arch of one foot and ease your weight onto it gradually.

Movement: Press into a tender spot and hold until you feel it release, usually thirty to sixty seconds. When it lets go, find another. Two or three spots per foot is plenty.

Focus: Pressure, not pain. Settle onto the spot and stay still, because the tissue softens under a steady load rather than a rolling one. Breathe out slowly and let it go. Work the arch and the front of the heel, and ease off if the foot is sharply sore.

Hockey ball release – glute
How to
2-3 spots each sideBodyweight

Start: Sit on the floor with a hockey ball under one buttock, hands behind you for support. Cross that ankle over the opposite knee to open the hip, and lower your weight on gradually.

Movement: Lean into a tender spot and hold until you feel it release, usually thirty to sixty seconds. When it lets go, shift and find another. Two or three spots per side.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, not pain. Hold still rather than rolling about, and take weight back through your hands if it is too much. If you get anything shooting, electric or numb down the leg you are on the nerve, so move off that spot straight away.
