Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Dumbbell2 x 2kg

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell2kg

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Wall slides
2 × 12Wall–

Start: Back, head and arms touching wall. Feet few cm away. Elbows at 90°, arms in goalpost.

Focus: Keep entire back and both arms in wall contact throughout — no loss of contact.

Movement: Slide arms up slowly toward overhead while maintaining wall contact. Slide back down with control.

Cossack squat
2 × 8 eachBodyweight–

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 × 45s eachWall–

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Waiter carry – single arm
2 × 20 stepsDumbbell5kg then 2kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

90/90 hip flow
2 × 8 eachMat–

Start: Start in 90/90 sitting position (one leg in front, one to the side).

Focus: Move smoothly and fluidly. Use hands lightly for support only.

Movement: Transition smoothly from one 90/90 position to the other, rotating through hips.

Reverse crunches
2 x 10Mat–

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.

ISO Romanian deadlift – single leg
2 x 5DB–

Start: Hinge into a single leg RDL position with your torso roughly parallel to the floor and one leg extended behind.

Focus: Feel the posterior chain endurance demand. Keep your back flat and hips square throughout.

Movement: Hold the position for the prescribed time. Use a light dumbbell in one or both hands.
