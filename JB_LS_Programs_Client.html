Main Circuit
⌄
Goblet squat
3 x 10KB/DBModerate

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 each sideDumbbellModerate

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

⌄
Reverse lunge – bodyweight
2 x 10 each legBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

⌄
DB single-arm press
3 x 10 each sideDumbbellModerate

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

⌄
Prone Y raise
2 x 12Light DBLight

Start: Lie face down on the floor or bench, thumbs pointing upward.

Focus: Keep the movement small — quality over height. Lower back stays relaxed.

Movement: Raise both arms into a Y shape, lifting a few centimetres. Hold briefly, lower with control.

⌄
Calf raises – slow eccentric
3 x 12BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The single leg eccentric (lowering) phase is the therapeutic component. Go slow.

Movement: Rise onto both feet, transfer to one foot. Lower that heel slowly over 4 seconds. Rise on both feet each rep.

⌄
Dead bug – arms and legs
2 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

⌄
Balance – single leg with arm reach
3 x 20s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Static Program
⌄
Pivot lunges
1 × 10BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft bend in the knee.

Focus: Land softly on each step and control the pivot back. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Build confidence in each direction before moving to the next.

Movement: Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to the starting position. Repeat for all reps, then move to lateral — spring out to the side and pivot back. Finally rotational — jump and rotate in the air, planting your foot behind and diagonally. Complete all reps in each direction before moving to the next.

⌄
Cossack squat
1 × 10BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

⌄
Single arm reverse fly – DB
1 × 10Bodyweight2kg

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge, free hand braced on your thigh or a bench for support.

Focus: Squeeze the shoulder blade at the top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise one arm out to the side to shoulder height, leading with slight external rotation of the upper arm. Complete all reps, then switch arms.

⌄
Reverse crunches
1 × 10MatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.
