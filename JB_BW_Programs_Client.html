Program 1 — Barbell Strength & Variety
WC 1st June 2026
Barbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Barbell

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell in a double overhand grip just outside your legs. Stand tall with the bar at hip height.

Focus: This is a hip hinge — keep your knees soft but nearly straight. Feel the hamstring stretch as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body throughout.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch. Drive your hips forward to return to standing, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Barbell back squat
2 × 8Barbell

Start: Position the bar across your upper traps (high bar) or rear delts (low bar). Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Unrack and take two steps back.

Focus: Keep your chest up and core braced throughout. Track knees over toes — push them out as you descend. Depth to parallel or below if mobility allows.

Movement: Break at the hips and knees simultaneously, descending under control. Drive through your heels to stand, extending hips and knees together. Lock out at the top without hyperextending.

DB lateral lunge
2 × 10 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the bent heel to return.

Barbell bench press
2 × 8Barbell

Start: Lie flat on a bench with eyes under the bar. Grip the bar just wider than shoulder-width, feet flat on the floor. Retract and depress your shoulder blades into the bench.

Focus: Keep your shoulder blades pinched together throughout. Lower with control — don't let the bar bounce off your chest. Maintain a slight arch in your lower back.

Movement: Unrack the bar and lower it in a slight arc to your lower chest. Touch lightly, then press back up and slightly back toward the rack. Lock out at the top without losing tension.

Pull-ups
2 × maxPull-up Bar

Start: Hang from a pull-up bar with hands just wider than shoulder-width, palms facing away. Arms fully extended, shoulders engaged — don't just hang passively.

Focus: Initiate with your shoulder blades — pull them down and back before bending your elbows. Think about driving your elbows toward your hips.

Movement: Pull your chest toward the bar by driving elbows down. Chin clears the bar at the top. Lower with full control back to a dead hang. Avoid swinging or kipping.

DB single-arm press
2 × 10 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Step-ups – forward low
2 × 10 eachStep

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

Lateral raises – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides with a slight bend in elbows.

Focus: Keep shoulders pressed down — don't shrug. Lead with elbows.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lower slowly under control.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Balance – single leg free
2 × 30 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Bicycle twists
2 × 15 eachMat

Start: Lie on your back with hands lightly behind your head and knees bent at 90°.

Focus: Rotation comes from your trunk — not from pulling your neck with your hands. Keep the movement controlled, not rushed.

Movement: Extend one leg while rotating your torso toward the bent knee, bringing the opposite elbow toward it. Alternate sides in a controlled cycling motion.
