How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Overhead squat – TRX
3 sets8-12 repsTRXBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold the TRX handles and raise the arms overhead, feet shoulder-width, taking light tension on the straps.

Movement: Keeping the arms overhead, sit the hips back and down into a squat, then drive up to stand, staying tall throughout.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, the heels down, and the chest up with the arms framing the head. Use the straps only for balance, and reduce depth if the torso wants to tip forward.

Pull-through – band
3 sets8-12 repsBandBlack band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand facing away from a low band, the band between the legs held in both hands, hinged forward with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips forward to stand tall, pulling the band through, squeezing the glutes, then hinge back under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips and glutes, not the arms or lower back, keeping the back flat. Stand tall with the ribs down at the top, and control the hinge back.

Tricep extension – overhead
3 sets8-12 repsAny kitLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand or sit tall, holding a weight in both hands behind the head, elbows pointing up, ribs down.

Movement: Straighten the arms to press the weight overhead, then lower it slowly behind the head, keeping the elbows still and pointing up.

Focus: Move only at the elbows, keeping the upper arms still and close to the head. Keep the ribs down and don't arch the lower back to press the weight up.

Calf raises – single leg
3 sets8-12 each sideStepBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on the floor or a step edge, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

External rotation – band
3 sets8-12 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a band at elbow height, elbow pinned to the side and bent to 90 degrees, forearm across the body.

Movement: Rotate the forearm outward, opening away from the body, keeping the elbow pinned, then return under control.

Focus: Keep the elbow glued to the side and move only the forearm, not the whole arm. Keep the shoulder down and the ribs still, and control the return.

Glute kickback – band
3 sets8-12 repsBand/AnchorLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Loop a band around your ankles or hold it under the standing foot. Stand tall, hands on a support for balance, slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Push one leg back against the band using the glute, keeping it fairly straight, then return with control.

Focus: Squeeze the glute at the back and keep the movement at the hip. Don't arch the lower back or swing the leg to cheat the range.

Elbow plank – arm reach
3 sets20s each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up in a forearm plank, straight line from head to heels, core and glutes braced.

Movement: Keeping the hips level, reach one arm forward off the floor, hold briefly, return, then the other.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square as you reach, resisting any twist. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and keep the reach controlled so the line stays straight.

Side plank – feet stacked
3 sets30 secMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, forearm down with the elbow under the shoulder, legs straight and feet stacked.

Movement: Lift the hips so the body forms a straight line from head to heels and hold for the set time.

Focus: Keep the hips high and the body in one straight line, resisting the sag towards the floor. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow and the neck long, and stop when the line drops.

Single leg box squat

Start: Stand on one leg in front of a box or chair, the other leg extended forward off the floor, arms out for balance.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down on the one leg to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heel to stand.

Focus: Keep the working knee tracking over the toes, never caving in, and the hips level. Touch the box under control, use a higher box to start, and keep the heel down throughout.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall against the band's pull, squeezing the glutes at the top, then hinge the hips back to lower with control.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, power from the hips and legs. Control the return, don't let the band snap you back down.

Neutral grip press

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in each hand at chest height with palms facing each other, elbows tucked, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, keeping the palms facing, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the elbows tucked close, which is easier on the shoulders. Control the lower to a comfortable stretch, and keep the ribs down.

Hip thrust – single leg

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, one foot flat on the floor and roughly under the knee, the other leg lifted and held. Chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute doing the work, and keep the ribs down so the lower back stays out of it.

Wrist flexion – supported

Start: Sit with the forearm resting along a thigh or bench, palm facing up, wrist and hand free over the edge, holding a light weight.

Movement: Curl the knuckles up towards the forearm, hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly to where the range stays pain-free rather than letting the weight drop the wrist back.

Focus: Move only at the wrist and only through the range that stays comfortable. The forearm never lifts off its support. Start lighter than feels necessary and add range before adding weight.

Anterior tibialis – weighted toe raises

Start: Sit tall with one leg out in front, heel on the floor and the knee softly bent. Hang a dumbbell or kettlebell over the forefoot, close to the toes, and let it settle before you start.

Movement: Pull the toes and the front of the foot up towards the shin against the weight, hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly.

Focus: Move only at the ankle. The knee and hip stay still, so nothing swings the weight up for you. Full range every rep, and control the lower rather than letting the weight drop. If a cramp starts to build, that set is finished.
