Barbell Strength & Variety
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Barbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Barbell15kg

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell in a double overhand grip just outside your legs. Stand tall with the bar at hip height.

Focus: This is a hip hinge — keep your knees soft but nearly straight. Feel the hamstring stretch as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body throughout.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch. Drive your hips forward to return to standing, squeezing your glutes at the top.

⌄
Goblet squat
2 × 10KB/DB10kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

⌄
DB lateral lunge
2 × 10 eachDB2 x 5kg

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the bent heel to return.

⌄
Reverse lunge – bodyweight
2 × 10 eachBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

⌄
DB single-arm press
2 × 10 eachDumbbell3kg

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

⌄
Single arm row – DB
2 × 10 eachDB/Bench8kg

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control — don't let weight drop.

⌄
Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12DB1.25kg / 2.5kg

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

⌄
Lateral raises – slow eccentric
2 × 12DBLight

Start: Stand tall with dumbbells at your sides, shoulders pressed down away from ears.

Focus: The slow eccentric lowering is the therapeutic part — particularly good for supraspinatus health. Keep shoulders down throughout.

Movement: Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height at normal speed. Lower over 4 seconds under full control. Repeat.

⌄
Wall slides
2 × 12WallBodyweight

Start: Back, head and arms touching wall. Feet few cm away. Elbows at 90°, arms in goalpost.

Focus: Keep entire back and both arms in wall contact throughout — no loss of contact.

Movement: Slide arms up slowly toward overhead while maintaining wall contact. Slide back down with control.
