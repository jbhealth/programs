Goblet squat – pause at bottom
3 x 8Any kit5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a weight at the chest with both hands, elbows tucked, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat, pause for two to three seconds at the bottom holding still, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down through the pause. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and pause only at a depth you control comfortably.

⌄
Deadlift – BB tempo
3 x 8Barbell25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up as for a conventional deadlift, feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot, flat back, chest up, core braced.

Movement: Lift the bar to standing with control, then lower it slowly over three to four seconds down the same close path, keeping tension the whole way.

Focus: Hold a flat back through the slow lower, the tempo is where the exercise is won or lost. Keep the bar close and don't let the back round as fatigue sets in on the descent.

Neutral grip press
3 x 10Any kit/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on a bench, a dumbbell in each hand at chest height with palms facing each other, elbows tucked, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the dumbbells up until the arms are nearly straight, keeping the palms facing, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the elbows tucked close, which is easier on the shoulders. Control the lower to a comfortable stretch, and keep the ribs down.

Reverse fly
3 x 10Any kit1.25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back, a light dumbbell in each hand hanging below the chest, elbows softly bent.

Movement: Raise the arms out to the sides to shoulder height, squeezing the shoulder blades, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep a soft bend in the elbows and lead with the backs of the hands, squeezing the shoulder blades. Keep the ribs down and don't swing or shrug, and use a light weight for control.
