Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Squat – lateral step-out
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with the feet together and the hands at the chest, or a light weight held at the chest.

Movement: Step one foot out wide to the side and sit into a squat as it lands. Drive up and bring the foot back in to stand tall. Alternate sides each rep.

Focus: Land the stepping foot flat with the toes turned out slightly, then sit back into the squat with the heels down and the knees in line with the middle toes. Keep the chest up.

Standing fall-out – TRX
3 sets8-10 repsRings or TRXBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Face away from the anchor, handles in hand, arms extended forward, body leaning forward in a straight line, core braced.

Movement: Let the arms travel forward and overhead as the body leans further, then pull back to the start using the core.

Focus: Keep the body in a straight line and the ribs down, not letting the lower back arch as the arms extend. Only go as far as you can hold the line, and control the return.

Hip external rotation – seated, band
3 sets8-10 repsBand/ChairLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit tall on a bench or chair with the knees bent at 90 degrees and a band round the working ankle, fixed out to the side of that leg.

Movement: Swing the lower leg in across the other leg against the band, turning at the hip, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change legs.

Focus: Keep the knee still and the thigh on the seat, so the turn comes from the hip. Sit tall and move slowly in both directions. The foot moves in as the thigh turns out.

Reverse fly – band, single arm
3 sets8-10 each sideBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand or hinge holding a band anchored in front, one handle in one hand, arm across the body, elbow softly bent.

Movement: Pull the arm out to the side to shoulder height against the band, squeezing the shoulder blade, then return under control.

Focus: Keep a soft elbow bend and squeeze the shoulder blade, not shrugging. Keep the torso still and the ribs down, and control the return against the band.

Reverse curl – BB
3 sets8-10 repsBarbellModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall holding a barbell at the thighs with an overhand grip, elbows pinned to the sides, ribs down.

Movement: Curl the bar up towards the shoulders with the palms down, keeping the elbows still, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the elbows pinned and the wrists firm, not swinging to lift. Keep the ribs down, use a lighter weight than a normal curl, and control the lower.

Straight arm raise
3 sets10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall holding a weight in both hands at the thighs, arms straight, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Raise the weight forward and up to about shoulder height with straight arms, then lower under control.

Focus: Lead with straight arms and keep the shoulders down, not shrugging, raising only to shoulder height. Keep the ribs down and don't lean back, and control the lower.

Single leg deadlift – bodyweight
3 sets10 repsBodyweightBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Glute bridge – march
3 sets8-10 repsMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a bridge at the top with the hips fully extended and level, feet hip-width, core braced.

Movement: Without letting the hips drop, lift one foot a few centimetres off the floor, set it down, then the other, in a slow march.

Focus: Keep the hips absolutely still and level, no rocking or dipping as the foot lifts. The glutes and core hold the position throughout.

Elbow plank
3 sets30 secMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set your forearms on the floor shoulder-width, elbows under the shoulders, legs extended back, toes tucked under.

Movement: Lift the hips to form a straight line from head to heels and hold that position for the set time, breathing steadily.

Focus: Keep a straight line, hips neither sagging down nor piking up, and the core and glutes braced. Keep the ribs down and the neck long, and stop when the line breaks rather than pushing through a drooping back.

Squat – TRX, single leg
How to
2 x 10-12 each sideBodyweight

Start: Hold the TRX handles, stand on one leg with the other lifted in front, standing foot planted, light tension on the straps.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down on the one leg, using the straps for balance and assistance, then drive up through the heel to stand.

Focus: Keep the working knee in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the hips level. Use the straps to share the load, keep the heel down, and control the depth.

Curl and overhead extension – weighted
How to
3 x 10

Start: Stand tall holding one weight in both hands at the thighs (a plate by its edges, a dumbbell by one end or a kettlebell by the horns), elbows pinned to the sides, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Curl the weight to the shoulders, press it overhead, lower it behind the head by bending the elbows, then reverse the whole path to return.

Focus: Keep the elbows still through the curl rather than letting them travel forward, and keep the ribs down through the overhead portion rather than arching the back. Control the weight on the way back down, which is where most of the work is.

Anterior tibialis – single leg toe raises
How to
3 x 10

Start: Stand with your back against a wall, heels stepped a little forward, and take one foot off the floor. Keep a hand on something if you need it.

Movement: Lift the toes and forefoot of the standing leg up towards the shin as far as they will go, then lower under control.

Focus: One leg takes everything that two were sharing, so expect a clear difference side to side. Match the weaker side's reps on the stronger one rather than chasing a number. Step the standing foot further from the wall to make it harder.

Wrist curls – BB
How to
2 x 10-122.5kg

Start: Sit with the forearms resting on the thighs or a bench, a barbell held with the wrists over the edge, palms up.

Movement: Curl the wrists up to raise the bar, then lower under control through the full range.

Focus: Move only at the wrists through a comfortable range, keeping the forearms still and supported. Use a manageable weight, and control the slow lower.

Single leg deadlift – weighted
How to
2 x 10-12 each sideModerate

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the weight lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, not rounding, and keep the hips level, not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Goblet squat
How to
3 x 105kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Overhead squat – TRX
How to
3 x 8-12Bodyweight

Start: Hold the TRX handles and raise the arms overhead, feet shoulder-width, taking light tension on the straps.

Movement: Keeping the arms overhead, sit the hips back and down into a squat, then drive up to stand, staying tall throughout.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, the heels down, and the chest up with the arms framing the head. Use the straps only for balance, and reduce depth if the torso wants to tip forward.

Pull-through – band
How to
3 x 8-12Black band

Start: Stand facing away from a low band, the band between the legs held in both hands, hinged forward with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips forward to stand tall, pulling the band through, squeezing the glutes, then hinge back under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips and glutes, not the arms or lower back, keeping the back flat. Stand tall with the ribs down at the top, and control the hinge back.

Tricep extension – overhead
How to
3 x 8-12Light

Start: Stand or sit tall, holding a weight in both hands behind the head, elbows pointing up, ribs down.

Movement: Straighten the arms to press the weight overhead, then lower it slowly behind the head, keeping the elbows still and pointing up.

Focus: Move only at the elbows, keeping the upper arms still and close to the head. Keep the ribs down and don't arch the lower back to press the weight up.

Calf raises – single leg
How to
3 x 8-12 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on the floor or a step edge, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

External rotation – band
How to
3 x 8-12Light

Start: Stand side-on to a band at elbow height, elbow pinned to the side and bent to 90 degrees, forearm across the body.

Movement: Rotate the forearm outward, opening away from the body, keeping the elbow pinned, then return under control.

Focus: Keep the elbow glued to the side and move only the forearm, not the whole arm. Keep the shoulder down and the ribs still, and control the return.

Glute kickback – band
How to
3 x 8-12Light

Start: Loop a band around your ankles or hold it under the standing foot. Stand tall, hands on a support for balance, slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Push one leg back against the band using the glute, keeping it fairly straight, then return with control.

Focus: Squeeze the glute at the back and keep the movement at the hip. Don't arch the lower back or swing the leg to cheat the range.

Elbow plank – arm reach
How to
3 x 20s each sideBodyweight

Start: Set up in a forearm plank, straight line from head to heels, core and glutes braced.

Movement: Keeping the hips level, reach one arm forward off the floor, hold briefly, return, then the other.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square as you reach, resisting any twist. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and keep the reach controlled so the line stays straight.

Side plank – feet stacked
How to
3 x 30 sec

Start: Lie on your side, forearm down with the elbow under the shoulder, legs straight and feet stacked.

Movement: Lift the hips so the body forms a straight line from head to heels and hold for the set time.

Focus: Keep the hips high and the body in one straight line, resisting the sag towards the floor. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow and the neck long, and stop when the line drops.

Deadlift – band
How to
3 x 8-12Black

Start: Stand on the middle of a band, feet hip to shoulder-width, and take a handle or the band in each hand. Hinge down with a flat back, chest up.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall against the band's pull, squeezing the glutes at the top, then hinge the hips back to lower with control.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, power from the hips and legs. Control the return, don't let the band snap you back down.

Neutral grip press
How to
3 x 8-125kg

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in each hand at chest height with palms facing each other, elbows tucked, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, keeping the palms facing, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the elbows tucked close, which is easier on the shoulders. Control the lower to a comfortable stretch, and keep the ribs down.

Hip thrust – single leg
How to
3 x 8-12 each sideBodyweight

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, one foot flat on the floor and roughly under the knee, the other leg lifted and held. Chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute doing the work, and keep the ribs down so the lower back stays out of it.

Wrist flexion – supported
How to
3 x 8-121.25kg

Start: Sit with the forearm resting along a thigh or bench, palm facing up, wrist and hand free over the edge, holding a light weight.

Movement: Curl the knuckles up towards the forearm, hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly to where the range stays pain-free rather than letting the weight drop the wrist back.

Focus: Move only at the wrist and only through the range that stays comfortable. The forearm never lifts off its support. Start lighter than feels necessary and add range before adding weight.

Anterior tibialis – weighted toe raises
How to
3 x 8-12Light

Start: Sit tall with one leg out in front, heel on the floor and the knee softly bent. Hang a dumbbell or kettlebell over the forefoot, close to the toes, and let it settle before you start.

Movement: Pull the toes and the front of the foot up towards the shin against the weight, hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly.

Focus: Move only at the ankle. The knee and hip stay still, so nothing swings the weight up for you. Full range every rep, and control the lower rather than letting the weight drop. If a cramp starts to build, that set is finished.

Biceps 21s
How to
3 x 8-122.5kg

Movement: Do seven curls through the bottom half, seven through the top half, then seven full curls, keeping the elbows still.

Focus: Keep the elbows pinned to the sides throughout and don't swing, even as fatigue builds. Keep the ribs down, and control each partial and full rep.

Crunches
How to
3 x 10

Movement: Curl the head and shoulders up off the floor by rounding the upper back, then lower under control.

Focus: Curl from the abdominals and keep the neck neutral, not pulling the chin to the chest. Keep the lower back settled, and control the lower rather than dropping.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes
How to
3 x 30 sec

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.
