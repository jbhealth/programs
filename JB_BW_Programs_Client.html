Back squat – pause
3 x 6BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Bar on the upper back, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced hard, standing tall after unracking.

Movement: Sit down to a comfortable depth, pause for two to three seconds holding still, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down through the pause. Keep the chest up and the core braced so the back stays flat, and pause only at a depth you control.

Deadlift – BB
3 x 6BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with feet hip-width, the bar over mid-foot. Hinge down to grip just outside your legs, shins to the bar, chest up, back flat, shoulders slightly ahead of the bar, core braced hard.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, keeping the bar dragging close to your legs, hips and shoulders rising together. Finish standing tall with the glutes, then hinge the hips back and lower the bar under control down the same line.

Focus: The back stays flat from floor to lockout, never rounded. Take the load through the legs and hips and keep the bar close to the body throughout. Don't jerk the bar or lean back at the top, just stand tall.

Bench press – BB
3 x 8Barbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on the bench, eyes under the bar, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, a slight natural arch. Grip a little wider than shoulders and unrack over the chest.

Movement: Lower the bar under control to the mid-chest, elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, then press it back up over the shoulders to straight arms.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades pinched back and down throughout, which protects the shoulders, and the elbows tucked rather than flared wide. Keep the feet planted and the wrists stacked, lower under control to the chest, and don't bounce the bar.

Bent-over rows – BB
3 x 10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back holding a barbell at arms' length, chest up, hips back, core braced.

Movement: Pull the bar towards the lower chest or upper abdomen, driving the elbows back, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hinge steady, not rising or rounding as you row, this protects the lower back. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and control the lower.

Bulgarian split squat – BB
3 x 8 each sideBarbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Barbell across the upper back, stand a stride in front of a bench with the back foot resting on it, torso tall, core braced.

Movement: Lower by bending the front knee until the thigh is towards parallel, then drive up through the front heel to the start.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the weight through the front leg with the core braced. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the knee stays comfortable.

Landmine rotation
3 x 10 each sideBarbell/LandmineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand holding the end of a landmine bar in both hands at chest height, arms extended, feet planted, core braced.

Movement: Sweep the bar down and across from one side to the other in an arc, rotating through the trunk, then reverse.

Focus: Turn through the trunk with the hips pivoting and the feet allowed to turn, not twisting the lower back or knees. Keep the ribs down, and control the arc.

Band crab walks
2 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, in a slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and out one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, then continue for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees pushing out, not caving in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips and glutes work.

Bird-dog
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

Banded hip thrust – BB
3 x 12Barbell/Bench/BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit with the upper back against a bench, a barbell across the hips, a band around the thighs, feet planted.

Movement: Drive the hips up until the body is level from knees to shoulders, pushing the knees out against the band, then lower under control.

Focus: Drive through the heels and squeeze the glutes at the top, ribs down, not arching the lower back. Push the knees out against the band, and control the lower.

Balance – single leg knee drive
3 x 10 each sideLight DBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other knee ready to drive up, arms ready.

Movement: Drive the free knee up towards hip height, hold the balance briefly, then lower under control and repeat.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, staying tall as the knee drives up. Fix the eyes on a point, control the drive and lower, and keep the core engaged.

Back squat – BB

Start: Set the bar across the upper back on the traps, hands gripping evenly, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Brace the core hard and unrack, stepping back to a stable stance.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down, bending the knees, until the thighs reach a comfortable depth, keeping the chest up and the bar over mid-foot. Drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking out over the toes, never caving in, and the heels planted throughout. Keep the chest up and the core braced so the back stays flat, and take depth only to where you keep good form and comfortable knees.

Romanian deadlift – BB

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

DB single-arm press

Start: Stand or sit tall, a dumbbell at one shoulder, ribs down, core braced against the offset load.

Movement: Press the dumbbell overhead until the arm is straight, then lower back to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist leaning away from the weight, staying upright. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and press within a comfortable range.

Single arm row – DB

Start: Hand and knee on a bench, the other foot planted, a dumbbell in the free hand hanging down, back flat and level.

Movement: Pull the dumbbell towards the hip, driving the elbow back and up, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the back flat and level, not twisting to lift. Keep the shoulder down, and control the lower.

2 x 10 each · Moderate
DB lateral lunge

Start: Hold a dumbbell in each hand, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step wide to one side and sit the hips back into that hip, keeping the other leg fairly straight, then push back to standing.

Focus: Keep the bent knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the chest up as you sit into the hip. Push back through the heel, and keep the trailing leg straight but not locked.

2 x 10 each · Moderate
Figure-4 stretch – seated

Start: Sit on a chair, one ankle crossed over the opposite knee in a figure-4, sitting tall.

Movement: Gently lean the torso forward from the hips until you feel a stretch in the crossed hip, then hold.

Focus: Lean forward only to a comfortable stretch, keeping the back fairly flat rather than rounding. Keep the crossed knee open, breathe, and ease off if it pinches.

2 x 20s each · Bodyweight
KB get-up

Start: Lie on your back, a kettlebell pressed up in one hand with a straight arm, same-side knee bent, other limbs out for support.

Movement: Rise through the set sequence (to elbow, to hand, to kneeling, to standing) keeping the weight up, then reverse it back down.

Focus: Keep the eyes on the weight and the arm locked straight throughout, moving slowly step by step. Keep each position stable before the next, and use a light weight to stay controlled.

Hip abduction – clamshell

Start: Lie on your side, hips and knees bent, feet together, hips stacked, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Keeping the feet together, open the top knee up like a clam, then lower under control.

Focus: Open only as far as the hips stay stacked, not rolling the top hip back. Keep the movement slow, and feel the outer hip and glute work.

Hip abduction – side lying

Start: Lie on your side, legs stacked and straight, hips square, head supported.

Movement: Raise the top leg up to the side, keeping it straight and in line with the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the leg in line with the body, not drifting forward, and the hips stacked, not rolling back. Lead with the heel, keep it slow, and feel the outer hip work.
