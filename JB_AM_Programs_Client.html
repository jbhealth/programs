Main Circuit
Box squat – goblet
3 sets10 repsAny kit/Box5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: High plastic box.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

Hang clean – BB
3 sets6-10 repsBarbellModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Power and speed on the way up. Steady down!

Start: Stand holding a barbell at the thighs, feet hip-width, hinged slightly with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive explosively through the hips to pull the bar up and catch it at the shoulders in a slight dip, then lower under control.

Focus: Drive from a sharp hip extension with a flat back, not yanking with the arms. Catch softly at the shoulders with the ribs down and knees tracking over the feet, and control the lower.

Drop landing – bilateral
3 sets10 repsBox/BenchBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Remember to land into a small squat. And be as soft as you can

Start: Stand on a low box or step, feet hip-width, ready to step off, core braced.

Movement: Step off and land softly on both feet in a quarter-squat, absorbing through the hips and knees, then stand.

Focus: Land softly and quietly, absorbing through the hips and knees, with the knees in line with the middle toes, not caving in. Aim for a stable, controlled catch rather than a stiff or collapsing landing.

Seated cable row – close grip
3 sets8-12 repsCable row machine19.25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Focus on shoulder blade muscles to fight the hunch.

Start: Sit at the low cable, feet braced, a close handle in both hands, arms extended, chest up, back tall.

Movement: Pull the handle to the lower abdomen, driving the elbows back close to the body, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Drive the elbows back and squeeze the shoulder blades, not just bending the arms. Keep the back tall and still, no rocking, and keep the shoulders down.

Knee flexion – cable
3 sets8-12 repsCable machine1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Hip and thigh don’t move, solely a knee bend.

Start: Stand facing a low cable with a cuff around one ankle, holding the frame, torso tall.

Movement: Curl the heel towards the buttock by bending the knee, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the thigh fairly still and move at the knee, curling the heel up. Keep the torso tall and the lower back neutral, and control the lower.

Knee extension – cable
3 sets8-12 repsCable machineModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Hip and thigh don’t move, solely a knee bend.

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

Chest press – single arm
3 sets10 repsAny kit/Bench5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in one hand at chest height, the other arm out for balance, shoulder blades set, core braced.

Movement: Press the weight up until the arm is nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and resist the torso twisting towards the free side. Keep the elbow at about 45 degrees, not flared, and control the lower.

Side plank – with rotation
2 sets30s each sideBodyweightBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a side plank, top arm reaching towards the ceiling, body in a straight line.

Movement: Reach the top arm under and through the gap beneath your body, rotating the upper back, then return to the open position.

Focus: Keep the hips lifted and level as you rotate, letting the twist come from the upper back, not the lower spine. Keep the shoulder stacked and don't let the hips sag through the reach.

Ski erg
3 minutesSki ergBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Get your heart pumping!

Start: Stand tall holding the handles overhead, feet hip-width, knees soft, core braced.

Movement: Pull the handles down and back while hinging slightly at the hips, then return under control, repeating in rhythm.

Focus: Drive from the trunk and hips with a flat back, not rounding to pull. Keep the knees soft and the movement smooth, and build the pace gradually.

Single-leg deadlift – weighted

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the kettlebell lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, not rounding, and keep the hips level, not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Bulgarian split squat

Start: Stand a stride in front of a bench, the top of your back foot resting on it, torso tall, arms for balance.

Movement: Lower straight down by bending the front knee until the thigh is towards parallel, then drive up through the front heel to the start.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and most of the weight through the front leg. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the front knee stays comfortable.

2 x 10 each · Moderate
Terminal knee extension

Start: Stand with a band around the back of one knee anchored in front, a slight bend in that knee, holding a support.

Movement: Straighten the knee fully against the band, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend slightly under control.

Focus: Focus on fully straightening the knee and squeezing the front of the thigh hard at the top. Keep it slow and controlled, stay tall, and keep the movement pain-free.

3 × 15 each · Band
Reverse lunge to knee raise

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, arms ready to help drive.

Movement: Step one foot back into a reverse lunge, then drive back up and bring that knee up in front to hip height, balancing briefly, before stepping back again.

Focus: Keep the front knee over the ankle in the lunge and the hips level on the knee raise. Control the balance at the top, and drive through the standing heel rather than swinging up.

3 × 8 each · 2.5kg
Lateral band walks

Start: Stand with a band around the legs (ankles or above the knees), feet hip-width, knees softly bent, torso tall.

Movement: Step sideways one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps, then return the other way.

Focus: Keep tension on the band throughout and the feet pointing forward, not letting the knees cave in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips work.

2 × 12 each · Band
Balance – single leg with arm reach

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to reach.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft as you reach, moving slowly. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and return under control.

Movement: Reach the arms (and torso if set) out to the sides or forward, challenging balance, then return to centre.

3 x 20s each · 2.5kg
Pallof press

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

2 x 10 · 4.25kg
Calf raises – loaded single leg

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on a step edge, a weight in the hand, holding a support with the other.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.
