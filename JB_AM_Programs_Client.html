Main Circuit
⌄
Single leg deadlift – KB
To burn x2KB5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell in the opposite hand.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the kettlebell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance. Drive through your heel to return.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat
2 x 10 eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

⌄
Terminal knee extension
3 × 15 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

⌄
Reverse lunge to knee raise
3 × 8 eachBodyweight2.5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

⌄
Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

⌄
Single-Leg Balance Reach (Multi-Directional)
3 × 6-8 eachBodyweight2.5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with a soft knee. Arms out to the sides for balance if needed.

Movement: Reach forward with one arm (or tap the ground in front), return to center. Reach to the side, return. Reach behind, return. Move through these directions in a controlled, flowing manner. Switch legs and repeat.

Focus: Keep your standing leg stable. Move smoothly through all directions. The goal is dynamic balance, not static—keep moving, keep thinking.

⌄
Pallof press
2 x 10Cable/Band4.25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

⌄
Calf raises – loaded single leg
3 × 10 eachKB/StepLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.
