How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
⌄
Back squat – high bar
3 x 6-10Barbell/RackModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Rest the bar high on the traps, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced hard, standing tall after unracking.

Movement: Sit down with a fairly upright torso, knees tracking forward over the toes, to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees over the toes and the heels down, chest tall throughout. Keep the core braced to protect the back, and control the depth to your comfortable range.

⌄
Deadlift – trap bar, tempo
3 x 6-10Trap barModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar, feet hip-width, the handles in line with the middle of your feet. Hinge down and grip the handles in the middle, chest up, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Stand tall at a normal pace, then take a slow count of three to four on the way down, keeping the tension all the way to the floor. Reset the brace before each rep.

Focus: Keep the back flat through the whole slow lower, never letting the hips rise first or the back round as you tire. Keep the load through the heels.

⌄
Incline bench press – BB
3 x 6-10Barbell/Incline bench/RackModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on an incline bench, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, grip a little wider than shoulders, unrack over the upper chest.

Movement: Lower the bar under control to the upper chest, elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set back and down and the elbows tucked, not flared, to protect the shoulders. Keep the feet planted, lower under control, and don't bounce the bar.

⌄
Step-down – single leg
3 x 8-12 each sideStep boxBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on top of a low step on one leg, the other foot hovering off the edge, arms out for balance.

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, lightly tap, then drive back up to stand tall on the one leg.

Focus: Keep the standing knee tracking over the foot, never caving in, and the hips level throughout. Control the lower down slowly, and keep the movement in the standing leg, not by reaching with the free foot.

⌄
Hip thrust – BB paused
3 x 6-10Barbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Upper back on a bench, padded bar across the hips, feet flat and hip-width, shins vertical at the top, chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips up to a straight line, pause for two to three seconds with the glutes fully squeezed, then lower with control.

Focus: Hold the pause at a straight line, not over-arched. Keep the squeeze in the glutes and the ribs down, and don't let the lower back take over during the hold.

⌄
Knee extension – cable
3 x 8-12Cable machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

⌄
Anterior tibialis – single leg toe raises
3 x 8-12 each sideWallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your back against a wall, heels stepped a little forward, and take one foot off the floor. Keep a hand on something if you need it.

Movement: Lift the toes and forefoot of the standing leg up towards the shin as far as they will go, then lower under control.

Focus: One leg takes everything that two were sharing, so expect a clear difference side to side. Match the weaker side's reps on the stronger one rather than chasing a number. Step the standing foot further from the wall to make it harder.

⌄
Straight leg raises
3 x 8-12BodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back, legs straight, hands under the lower back or by the sides, ribs down.

Movement: Raise both straight legs towards the ceiling, then lower them slowly without letting the lower back arch.

Focus: Keep the lower back gently pressed down throughout, and stop lowering the moment it wants to arch. Move slowly, and bend the knees a little if the back lifts.

Single-leg deadlift – weighted

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the kettlebell lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, not rounding, and keep the hips level, not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Bulgarian split squat

Start: Stand a stride in front of a bench, the top of your back foot resting on it, torso tall, arms for balance.

Movement: Lower straight down by bending the front knee until the thigh is towards parallel, then drive up through the front heel to the start.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and most of the weight through the front leg. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the front knee stays comfortable.

Terminal knee extension

Start: Stand with a band around the back of one knee anchored in front, a slight bend in that knee, holding a support.

Movement: Straighten the knee fully against the band, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend slightly under control.

Focus: Focus on fully straightening the knee and squeezing the front of the thigh hard at the top. Keep it slow and controlled, stay tall, and keep the movement pain-free.

3 × 15 each · Band
Reverse lunge to knee raise

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, arms ready to help drive.

Movement: Step one foot back into a reverse lunge, then drive back up and bring that knee up in front to hip height, balancing briefly, before stepping back again.

Focus: Keep the front knee over the ankle in the lunge and the hips level on the knee raise. Control the balance at the top, and drive through the standing heel rather than swinging up.

3 × 8 each · 2.5kg
Lateral band walks

Start: Stand with a band around the legs (ankles or above the knees), feet hip-width, knees softly bent, torso tall.

Movement: Step sideways one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps, then return the other way.

Focus: Keep tension on the band throughout and the feet pointing forward, not letting the knees cave in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips work.

2 × 12 each · Band
Balance – single leg with arm reach

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to reach.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft as you reach, moving slowly. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and return under control.

Movement: Reach the arms (and torso if set) out to the sides or forward, challenging balance, then return to centre.

3 x 20s each · 2.5kg
Pallof press

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

2 x 10 · 4.25kg
Calf raises – loaded single leg

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on a step edge, a weight in the hand, holding a support with the other.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.
