Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 x 10 eachWallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground, all movement is at the ankle.

⌄
Romanian deadlift – KB
2 x 10KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell in both hands in front of your thighs, feet hip-width, with soft knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the kettlebell down the front of your legs to mid-shin, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. The bell sits a touch closer to the body than a barbell.

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch building before you drive the hips forward to return.

⌄
Lateral band walks
2 x 10 each wayBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

⌄
Balance – single leg assisted
2 x 30s eachWallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

⌄
Band row – seated on floor
2 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Movement: Pull band toward lower abdomen. Seated position isolates the back.

Focus: Drive elbows back and squeeze shoulder blades. No lower body movement.

⌄
Dead bug – arms only
2 x 10MatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead toward the floor, return, then alternate sides. Never let your lower back arch.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time, this is non-negotiable.

Band pull-apart
2 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a resistance band at chest height with straight arms, hands shoulder-width apart.

Movement: Pull the band apart until your arms are wide. Control the return, don't let it snap back.

Focus: Keep arms straight throughout. Squeeze shoulder blades together at the end.

Deadlift – KB
2 x 8-10KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Band overhead press
2 x 10BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on a resistance band.

Movement: Press the band overhead from shoulder height. Accommodating resistance pattern.

Focus: Feel the resistance increase as you press overhead. Reinforce the lockout at the top.

⌄
Band hamstring curl – standing
2 x 12 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Anchor the band low in front of you and loop the other end around one ankle. Face the anchor, step back until the band is taut, and hold a wall or chair for balance.

Movement: Curl your heel up toward your glute against the band, then lower back down under control.

Focus: Keep your hip still and torso upright. Feel constant tension in the back of the thigh through the whole rep.

⌄
Hip abduction – standing band
2 x 10 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Fix a band around your ankles and stand on one leg.

Movement: Slowly lift other leg out to the side against band resistance. Control both lift and return.

Focus: Standing knee slightly bent, torso upright. Don't lean to the side.

Bird-dog
2 x 8 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still, no rotation or hip drop.

⌄
Squat matrix
2 x 6 each stanceBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with good posture, feet together.

Movement: Squat with feet narrow, then shoulder-width, then wide. Then stagger your stance, one foot slightly ahead of the other, squat, then switch which foot is forward and repeat. Each stance offers a different feel and range.

Focus: Explore range in each stance. Tall spine, knees track over toes. Slow, controlled tempo.

⌄
Single leg deadlift – bodyweight
2 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft knee, arms by your sides.

Movement: Lower the torso as the free leg extends behind you to a comfortable balance point, then return to standing under control.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back and keep the hips square to the floor. Slow and controlled.

⌄
Wall push-up plus
2 x 12WallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand facing a wall with hands at shoulder width.

Movement: Perform a push-up, then protract your shoulder blades. This is the easiest version for early shoulder rehab.

Focus: At the top of the push-up, push further away from the wall, rounding your upper back.

⌄
Band monster walk – diagonal
2 x 10 each wayBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up as for a band monster walk with band above knees.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and to the side, alternating directions.

Focus: Step at 45° angles, alternating directions. Feel your hip stabilisers working in multiple planes.

⌄
Terminal knee extension
2 x 12 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

⌄
Pallof press
2 x 10 each sideCable/BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Focus: Resist the rotation, the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Band dislocate
2 x 10BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a resistance band with a wide grip in front of you, arms straight.

Movement: Rotate the band overhead and behind you. Touch your lower back, reverse back.

Focus: Start with a very wide grip, narrow it progressively as mobility improves.

⌄
Suitcase carry
2 x 30s each sideDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

⌄
Band face pull – seated
2 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on the floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Movement: Pull band toward your face. Seated position prevents cheating with momentum.

Focus: Drive elbows wide and back. Squeeze with high elbows at the end.

⌄
Calf raises – loaded single leg
2 x 10 eachKB/StepLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.

⌄
Glute bridges – weighted
2 x 12Barbell/DB/KBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on back with barbell, dumbbell or kettlebell across hips.

Movement: Drive through heels to push hips toward ceiling. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Feel the full hip extension.

⌄
Side plank – knees bent
2 x 20s eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

Focus: Keep your hips straight, don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.
