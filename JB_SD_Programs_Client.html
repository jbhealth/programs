⌄
Assisted pull-up
3 x 10Assisted pull-up machine70kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the assistance weight, kneel or stand on the pad, and take a grip wider than your shoulders. Hang with your arms straight.

Movement: Pull your chin up toward the bar, leading with the elbows. Lower slowly to a full hang. The machine carries some of your weight so you can train the whole range.

Focus: Drive your elbows down and back rather than pulling with the hands. Lower as slowly as you rise, that is where the strength is built.

Main Circuit
⌄
Hip thrust – BB
3 x 8Barbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level, no side drop.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 eachDumbbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
DB chest press
3 x 10DB/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Movement: Press both dumbbells upward to full arm extension, allowing them to naturally travel slightly inward at the top. Lower under control.

Focus: Use the full range of motion. Control the lowering phase, don't let the dumbbells drop.

⌄
Pull-ups – machine assisted
3 x 12Assisted pull-up machineBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the counterweight on an assisted pull-up machine. Grip the handles.

Movement: Pull down until chin is at bar level, then lower under control. Reduce counterweight as you get stronger.

Focus: Drive your elbows down. Use the same technique as free pull-ups.

⌄
Suitcase carry
3 x 30s eachDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

⌄
Lateral band walks
2 x to burnBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

⌄
Bird-dog
2 x 8 eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still, no rotation or hip drop.
