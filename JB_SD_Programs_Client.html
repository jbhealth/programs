Landmine squat
3 x 8Barbell/LandmineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold the end of the landmine at chest height with both hands, feet shoulder-width apart.

Movement: Squat to depth holding the landmine end at chest. The guided movement path helps develop squat mechanics.

Focus: The anterior load guides your movement and supports an upright chest. Keep tall posture throughout.

⌄
Romanian deadlift – KB
3 x 10KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell in both hands in front of your thighs, feet hip-width, with soft knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the kettlebell down the front of your legs to mid-shin, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. The bell sits a touch closer to the body than a barbell.

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch building before you drive the hips forward to return.

⌄
DB incline press
3 x 10DB/Incline benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set a bench to 30–45° incline. Sit with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Movement: Press dumbbells upward and slightly inward to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Focus: Upper chest and anterior delt focus. Use the full range of motion and control the lowering.

⌄
Barbell seal row
3 x 10Barbell/Elevated benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie prone on an elevated bench with the barbell hanging below. Grip with a shoulder-width overhand grip, arms straight.

Movement: Pull the bar to the bench by driving your elbows back. Squeeze your shoulder blades at the top. Lower under full control.

Focus: Keep your chest on the bench throughout, no body swing. This is a strict row with zero lower back involvement.

Balance – single leg eyes closed
3 x 30s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Establish a controlled single leg stance with eyes open first. Soft knee bend, tall spine.

Movement: Once you have solid eyes-open control, close your eyes and hold balance. Open if needed to reset. Build duration progressively.

Focus: Closing your eyes dramatically increases proprioceptive demand by removing the visual cue. Master eyes-open first.

⌄
Bird-dog – hold and pulse
3 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start on all fours with hands under shoulders and knees under hips. Spine neutral.

Movement: Extend the opposite arm and leg, hold for 5 seconds, then add 3 small pulses before returning.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still throughout both the hold and the pulses. The pulses challenge stability endurance.

⌄
Calf raises – single leg
3 x 12 eachStepBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg on the edge of a step, holding a rail or wall for balance.

Movement: Rise as high as possible onto the ball of the foot, then lower under control. Move to the loaded version once 3x15 is easy.

Focus: Lower the heel slowly below the step to a full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthen.

Main Circuit
⌄
Single leg Romanian deadlift
3 x 8 eachDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a slight bend in that knee, holding a weight in front of your thigh, or just bodyweight to begin.

Movement: Hinge from the hip, lowering the weight down the front of your standing leg as your free leg reaches behind you. Drive the hip forward to return tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips level, don't let the lifted side open up. Feel the stretch build in the standing-leg hamstring.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat
3 x 8 eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

⌄
Neutral grip press – DB
3 x 10DB/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells in a neutral grip, palms facing each other.

Movement: Press both dumbbells directly upward to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Focus: Keep elbows at roughly 45° from your body throughout. Neutral grip is more shoulder-friendly than pronated.

⌄
Barbell hip row
3 x 10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Hinge at the hips and pull the bar toward your hip crease. Squeeze at the bottom, return to standing.

Focus: This is a hip hinge movement. Feel your lower lats engaging as you pull.

⌄
Cross-body carry
3 x 20mDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at your side.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance, resisting the pull of the load.

Focus: The single arm load creates both lateral and rotational forces. Resist all compensations, stay tall.

⌄
Landmine half-kneeling rotation
3 x 10 eachBarbell/LandmineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Kneel on one knee perpendicular to the landmine, holding the end with both hands at chest height.

Movement: Rotate the landmine from side to side in a controlled arc. The single leg base adds significant anti-rotation demand.

Focus: The single leg base increases the stability demand. Core braced throughout.

⌄
Balance – single leg knee drive
3 x 8 eachLight DBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall on one leg holding a light dumbbell at your chest, standing knee soft. Place a box beside you as a touch-down safety, not something to lean on.

Movement: Slowly raise the free-leg knee toward hip height, hold for a beat, then lower under control without letting the foot touch down. Repeat, then switch sides.

Focus: Keep the standing hip level and the standing ankle quiet, let the foot grip and adjust. No swinging the knee up with momentum.

Cardio Finisher
Cycling / bike
6-8 min steady, low impact.
