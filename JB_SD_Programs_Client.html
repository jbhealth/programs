Program 1 — Hinge, Pull & Functional Core
WC 1st June 2026
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

Seated row – machine
2 × 10Machine

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

Step-ups – forward low
2 × 8 eachStep

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

DB single-arm press
2 × 8 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 × 45 sec eachWall

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Wood chop – med ball / DB
2 × 10 eachDumbbell

Start: Hold a medicine ball or dumbbell with both hands overhead to one side.

Focus: Power comes from your hips and obliques — not just arms.

Movement: Rotate and chop explosively diagonally down toward opposite hip. Return under control.

Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachResistance Band

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Side plank – knees bent
2 × 20 sec eachMat

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Keep your hips straight — don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

Program 2 — Squat, Push & Ankle Stability
WC 1st June 2026
Box squats – high box
2 × 10Step

Start: Stand in front of a high box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees in line with hips and ankles throughout. Use heel drive to stand.

Movement: Sit back onto the box with control, then stand fully using your heels.

Waiter carry – single arm
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

Balance – single leg assisted
2 × 30 sec eachWall

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

DB lateral lunge
2 × 8 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the bent heel to return.

Calf raises – slow eccentric
2 × 10 eachBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The single leg eccentric (lowering) phase is the therapeutic component. Go slow.

Movement: Rise onto both feet, transfer to one foot. Lower that heel slowly over 4 seconds. Rise on both feet each rep.

Single arm DB row – incline
2 × 10 eachDumbbell

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Shoulder press – seated DB
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Sit tall with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Core braced, lower back neutral. Don't arch your lower back.

Movement: Press both dumbbells directly overhead to full arm extension. Lower to shoulder height under control.

Pivot lunges
2 × 8 eachBodyweight

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft bend in the knee.

Focus: Land softly on each step and control the pivot back. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Build confidence in each direction before moving to the next.

Movement: Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to the starting position. Repeat for all reps, then move to lateral — spring out to the side and pivot back. Finally rotational — jump and rotate in the air, planting your foot behind and diagonally. Complete all reps in each direction before moving to the next.

Elbow plank – arm reach
2 × 8 eachMat

Start: Set up in a forearm plank with core firmly braced.

Focus: Master earlier progressions first. Keep hips level and core braced throughout.

Movement: Slowly reach one arm forward, keeping it low. Hold 3 seconds, return, switch sides.
