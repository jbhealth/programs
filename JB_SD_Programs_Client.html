Back-Care Strength & Stability
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Cable pull-through
3 x 12Cable machine37.5kg

Start: Set a cable at the lowest position

Focus: Stand facing away from the machine and hold the rope between your legs

Movement: Hinge forward, pushing hips back, then drive them forward explosively to stand. This is an excellent hip hinge pattern reinforcer and glute

⌄
Box squats – medium box
3 x 10BoxReebok high

Start: Stand in front of a medium-height box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees tracking over toes. Maintain tension — don't collapse onto the box.

Movement: Sit back and down to the box under control. Drive through heels to stand.

⌄
Hip thrust – BB
2 x 10Barbell/Bench30kg

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

⌄
Seated row – machine
3 x 12Seated row machine41.25kg

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

⌄
Bench press – BB
3 x 8Barbell/BenchModerate

Start: Lie on the bench with feet flat on the floor. Retract your shoulder blades and grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.

Focus: Keep your shoulder blades retracted and feet flat on the floor throughout. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Lower the bar to your mid-chest under control. Drive back to full extension.

⌄
Pallof press
2 x to burnCable/Band15.25kg

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

⌄
Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 x 12 each sideWallBodyweight

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

⌄
Balance – single leg assisted
3 x 20s each sideWallBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

⌄
Lateral band walks
To burn x 2BandLight

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Single-Leg Strength & Core Control
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 each sideDumbbellModerate

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

⌄
Reverse lunge – bodyweight
2 x 10 each legBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

⌄
Glute bridges – march
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Hold a glute bridge at the top with hips fully extended.

Focus: Keep hips absolutely still — no rocking or dropping during the march.

Movement: Alternately lift each foot slightly off the floor in a marching motion. Maintain hip stability.

⌄
Lat pulldown
3 x 12Lat pulldown machineModerate

Start: Sit at lat pulldown machine, grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width.

Focus: Drive elbows down toward hips. Don't use momentum.

Movement: Lean back slightly and pull bar to chin level. Squeeze at bottom, control return to full extension.

⌄
DB chest press
3 x 10DB/BenchModerate

Start: Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Use the full range of motion. Control the lowering phase — don't let the dumbbells drop.

Movement: Press both dumbbells upward to full arm extension, allowing them to naturally travel slightly inward at the top. Lower under control.

⌄
Dead bug – arms and legs
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

⌄
Cable rotation – low to high
3 x 10 each sideCable machineLight

Start: Stand side-on to a low cable pulley.

Focus: Drive from your hip and rotate through your thoracic spine. Control the return.

Movement: Rotate and lift cable from low outside to high across body diagonally.

⌄
Calf raises – slow eccentric
3 x 12BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The single leg eccentric (lowering) phase is the therapeutic component. Go slow.

Movement: Rise onto both feet, transfer to one foot. Lower that heel slowly over 4 seconds. Rise on both feet each rep.
