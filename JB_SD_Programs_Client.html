How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Goblet squat
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit~10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Hip thrust – BB, single leg
3 sets6-8 each sideBarbell/BenchModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Upper back on a bench, a padded bar across the hips, one foot flat and hip-width, the other leg lifted and held. Chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute, not by arching the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

Bench press – BB, incline
2 sets8-10 repsBarbell/Incline bench/RackModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on an incline bench, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, grip a little wider than shoulders, unrack over the upper chest.

Movement: Lower the bar under control to the upper chest, elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set back and down and the elbows tucked, not flared, to protect the shoulders. Keep the feet planted, lower under control, and don't bounce the bar.

Hip adduction – cable
3 sets8-10 repsCable machineLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a low cable with the ankle strap on the inside leg, holding the machine for support.

Movement: Sweep the working leg in across the front of the standing leg, then return slowly to the start. Finish the set, then change sides.

Focus: Stand tall on the outside leg and keep the hips level. Move the working leg slowly in both directions, without swinging.

Cuban press
2 sets8-10 repsDB/BandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a light weight in each hand in front of the thighs, palms facing you.

Movement: Lift the elbows out to the sides to shoulder height, then turn the forearms up so the hands are above the elbows. Press the weights overhead, then reverse each stage slowly back down.

Focus: Use a light weight and move slowly through each stage, keeping the elbows at shoulder height before you turn. Stop short of any pinching at the top of the shoulder.

Calf raises – single leg, loaded
3 sets8-10 each sideKB/StepLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on a step edge, a weight in the hand, holding a support with the other.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Wall slides
2 sets8-10 repsWallBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with your back, head and arms against a wall, feet a few centimetres out so the lower back stays flat, elbows bent to 90 degrees in a goalpost.

Movement: Slide the arms slowly up towards overhead keeping every point of contact, then slide back down under control.

Focus: Keep the whole back and both arms on the wall throughout. The moment the lower back arches or the hands lift away is your limit, so work just short of it, and breathe steadily.

Pike – Swiss ball
2 sets8-10 repsSwiss ball/MatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hands on the floor, shins or feet on a Swiss ball, body in a straight line, core braced hard.

Movement: Lift the hips towards the ceiling into a pike, rolling the ball in, then lower back to the straight line under control.

Focus: Lift from the core and control the ball, not letting the hips sag on the return. Keep the shoulders steady over the hands, and move smoothly.

Box squat – goblet

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square and the back flat through every pulse, resisting the twist. Keep the pulses small and controlled, and stop if the back starts to sag or rotate.
