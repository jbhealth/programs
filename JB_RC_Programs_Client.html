Posterior Chain & Hamstrings
Week commencing: 8 June 2026
Main Circuit
Hip thrust – BB
3 × 10Barbell–

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Romanian deadlift – BB
3 × 8Barbell–

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Leg curl – seated
3 × 12Leg curl machine–

Start: Sit at the leg curl machine, pad just below your calves.

Focus: Keep your back strong throughout. Use full range of motion.

Movement: Curl both heels toward your glutes. Hold briefly at top, lower with control.

Single-leg deadlift – DB
2 × 8 eachDumbbell–

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand pressed overhead, arm fully extended. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep hips square throughout — the overhead load increases the anti-rotation challenge significantly. Control the movement slowly.

Movement: Hinge at the hip, pushing the free leg behind as counterbalance while maintaining the dumbbell overhead. Lower until your torso is near parallel. Drive the standing hip forward to return.

Cable hip abduction
2 × 12 eachCable machine–

Start: Attach ankle strap to cable machine at lowest setting. Stand side-on to machine.

Focus: Keep your torso upright. Resist leaning away from the cable.

Movement: Step away from machine and lift attached leg out to side against cable.

Calf raises – bilateral
2 × 15Step–

Start: Stand tall, feet flat on the floor or edge of a step.

Focus: Go through the full range — let heels dip below the step on the way down.

Movement: Rise up onto the balls of your feet as high as possible. Lower slowly through full range.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCable–

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Hollow body hold
2 × 30sMat–

Start: Lie on your back with arms extended overhead and legs straight out.

Focus: Press your entire lower back firmly into the floor. Feel like a hollow banana shape.

Movement: Hold position with core tension. If lower back lifts off, raise your legs higher.

Knee Stability & Single-Leg Control
Week commencing: 8 June 2026
Main Circuit
Bulgarian split squat
3 × 8 eachBench–

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Nordic hamstring curls
2 × 6Mat–

Start: Kneel on a mat with your feet anchored securely by a partner or machine.

Focus: Control is everything. Lower as slowly as you can — use your hands to catch yourself at the bottom if needed.

Movement: Slowly lower your body toward the floor using your hamstrings as the brake. This is the most effective knee injury prevention exercise available.

Single leg box squats
2 × 10 eachBox–

Start: Stand on one leg in front of a box.

Focus: Sit fully onto the box — don't just tap it. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Avoid the knee collapsing inward.

Movement: Slowly lower yourself to sit fully on the box by bending the standing knee. Pause briefly, drive through your heel to stand.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 × 10 eachBodyweight–

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Hip abduction machine
2 × 15Hip abduction machine–

Start: Sit in the hip abduction machine, pads on the outside of your knees.

Focus: Keep your back flat against the seat. Push legs apart against resistance.

Movement: Push legs apart, hold briefly at the end range, return with control.

Single arm DB row – incline
2 × 10 each · slow eccentricDumbbell–

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Dead bug – arms and legs
2 × 10 eachMat–

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Bicycle twists
2 × 15 eachMat–

Start: Lie on your back with hands lightly behind your head and knees bent at 90°.

Focus: Rotation comes from your trunk — not from pulling your neck with your hands. Keep the movement controlled, not rushed.

Movement: Extend one leg while rotating your torso toward the bent knee, bringing the opposite elbow toward it. Alternate sides in a controlled cycling motion.
