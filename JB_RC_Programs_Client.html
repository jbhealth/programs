Romanian deadlift – BB
3 x 8BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat
2 x 10 eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

⌄
Swiss ball hamstring curl – bilateral
3 x 10Swiss ballBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with both heels on the Swiss ball and hips raised off the floor.

Movement: Bend your knees to roll the ball toward your glutes. Straighten your legs to return. Hips stay raised throughout.

Focus: Keep your hips elevated throughout. Feel both your hamstrings and hip extensors working.

⌄
Step-ups – lateral high with DB
3 x 8 eachBox/DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand to the side of a high box holding a dumbbell.

Movement: Step up laterally onto the high box driving through your heel to stand. Step back down under control.

Focus: Place your full heel on the box before driving up. Control the descent completely.

⌄
Band monster walk – diagonal
3 x 10 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up as for a band monster walk with band above knees.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and to the side, alternating directions.

Focus: Step at 45° angles, alternating directions. Feel your hip stabilisers working in multiple planes.

⌄
Single arm row – DB
2 x 10 eachDB/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control, don't let weight drop.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

⌄
Copenhagen plank – short lever
3 x 20s eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up in side plank with lower shin resting on a bench.

Movement: Other leg hovers below bench, unsupported. Hold position.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

⌄
Calf raises – loaded single leg
3 x 12 eachKB/StepLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.

Main Circuit
⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
2 x 8 eachDumbbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
Hip thrust – BB single leg
3 x 8 eachBarbell/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Upper back on bench, bar across hips. One foot flat on the floor, the other raised.

Movement: Drive through your planted foot to full hip extension. Keep your hips level throughout.

Focus: Single leg thrust maximises glute activation on the working side. Feel it working hard.

⌄
Reverse lunge to knee raise
3 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

⌄
Leg curl – with pause
2 x 10Leg curl machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down at the leg curl machine with your heels under the pad.

Movement: Curl your heels toward your glutes, hold at the top for 3 seconds. Lower under control.

Focus: The pause at the top increases hamstring activation. Keep your hips flat on the pad throughout.

⌄
Ice skaters
3 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall ready to bound laterally, with a slight squat in your hips.

Movement: Push off one leg and bound laterally to land on the opposite foot. Mimic the movement of a speed skater. Repeat continuously.

Focus: Land softly on each foot. Stay on the balls of your feet throughout.

⌄
Bird-dog – weighted arm
3 x 8 eachDBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up in bird-dog position holding a light dumbbell in extending arm.

Movement: Extend arm forward and opposite leg behind simultaneously. No momentum.

Focus: Added weight increases anti-rotation challenge. Slow, controlled movement.

⌄
Pallof press
2 x 10 eachCable/BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Focus: Resist the rotation, the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

⌄
Balance – single leg eyes closed
2 x 20s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Establish a controlled single leg stance with eyes open first. Soft knee bend, tall spine.

Movement: Once you have solid eyes-open control, close your eyes and hold balance. Open if needed to reset. Build duration progressively.

Focus: Closing your eyes dramatically increases proprioceptive demand by removing the visual cue. Master eyes-open first.
