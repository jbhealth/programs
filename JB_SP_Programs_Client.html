Main Circuit
Back squat – high bar
4 x 6BarbellHeavy

Start: Place the barbell high on your traps close to the base of your neck, feet shoulder-width.

Focus: High bar position requires a more upright torso than low bar. Keep your chest tall and knees tracking over toes.

Movement: Squat to parallel or below with an upright torso. Drive through your heels to stand.

Reverse lunge – BB
3 x 8 each legBarbellModerate

Start: Place the barbell across your upper traps with feet hip-width apart.

Focus: Front knee tracks over your ankle. Reverse lunge places less stress on the knee than forward lunge.

Movement: Step back into a lunge, lowering the rear knee toward the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Single arm DB row – incline
3 x 8-10 each sideDB/Incline benchModerate

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

DB single-arm press
3 x 8-10 each sideDumbbellModerate

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Hip thrust – BB
3 x 8-10Barbell/BenchModerate

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 each sideDumbbellModerate

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Landmine half-kneeling rotation
3 x 10 each sideBarbell/LandmineModerate

Start: Kneel on one knee perpendicular to the landmine, holding the end with both hands at chest height.

Focus: The single leg base increases the stability demand. Core braced throughout.

Movement: Rotate the landmine from side to side in a controlled arc. The single leg base adds significant anti-rotation demand.

Suitcase carry
3 x 30s each sideDB/KBModerate

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Cardio Finisher
Ski erg intervals
6 rounds: 30s hard / 30s easy.
