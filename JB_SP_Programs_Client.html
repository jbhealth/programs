Program 1 — Squat, Pull & Carry
Week commencing: 1 June 2026
Squat pattern · Posterior chain · Loaded stability
2 sets per exerciseRest as needed30 minsBB · DB · TRX · Band
Main Circuit
2 sets per exercise · Rest as needed
Back Squat – BB
2 × 6 · ModerateBarbell

Start: Place the barbell across your upper traps with feet shoulder-width apart and toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Keep your chest tall and knees tracking over your toes. Core braced throughout — breathe in at the top, out on the way up.

Movement: Push your hips back and knees out as you descend to hip crease at or below parallel. Drive through your heels to stand.

Single Arm DB Row – Incline
2 × 8 each · ModerateDumbbell / Incline Bench

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

TRX Hamstring Curl
2 × 10TRX

Start: Lie on your back with both heels in the TRX loops and hips raised off the floor.

Focus: Keep your hips elevated throughout the entire set — don't let them drop between reps. Feel the hamstrings loading.

Movement: Bend your knees to pull the handles toward your glutes. Extend back to straight legs. Hips stay up throughout.

Suitcase Carry
2 × 20m eachDumbbell / Kettlebell

Start: Hold a heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist the lateral pull trying to lean you toward the loaded side. Stay completely upright — anti-lateral flexion challenge.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance – Single Leg Eyes Closed
2 × 20 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Establish a controlled single leg stance with eyes open first. Soft knee bend, tall spine.

Focus: Closing your eyes dramatically increases proprioceptive demand by removing the visual cue. Master eyes-open first — don't rush.

Movement: Once you have solid eyes-open control, close your eyes and hold balance. Open if needed to reset. Build time gradually.

Dead Bug – Slow with Band
2 × 6 eachBand

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°. Loop a band between both hands and the opposite foot.

Focus: 4-second tempo both extending and returning. Quality is the priority — this is the most challenging dead bug variation.

Movement: Slowly extend the opposing arm and leg over 4 seconds while maintaining lower back contact with the floor. Return under control.

Copenhagen Plank – Short Lever
2 × 20 sec eachBench

Start: Set up in side plank with lower shin resting on a bench.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

Movement: Other leg hovers below bench — unsupported. Hold position for prescribed time each side.

Cardio Finisher
Pivot Lunges
30 sec on · 15 sec off · 4 rounds · ~3 mins. Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to start. Land softly, control the pivot. Keep your knee in line with hip and ankle throughout.
