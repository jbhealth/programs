Box squats – medium box
3 x 10BoxBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand in front of a medium-height box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees tracking over toes. Maintain tension — don't collapse onto the box.

Movement: Sit back and down to the box under control. Drive through heels to stand.

⌄
Leg extension – partial range (terminal)
10 eachLeg extension machineBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the leg extension to work only the final 30° of knee extension.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard in this range. This is vital for knee rehab.

Movement: Extend knees in the final range only. Hold and squeeze briefly, return.

⌄
Cable hip abduction
To burn x2Cable machine1.5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Attach ankle strap to cable machine at lowest setting. Stand side-on to machine.

Focus: Keep your torso upright. Resist leaning away from the cable.

Movement: Step away from machine and lift attached leg out to side against cable.

⌄
Single leg deadlift – bodyweight
2 x 10 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft knee, arms by your sides.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back and keep the hips square to the floor. Slow and controlled.

Movement: Lower the torso as the free leg extends behind you to a comfortable balance point, then return to standing under control.

⌄
Anterior tibialis toe raises
To burn x 2BodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your back against a wall, heels stepped a little forward, feet hip-width.

Focus: Keep the heels planted and knees straight. Pull only from the shins.

Movement: Lift the toes and forefeet up towards your shins as far as you can, then lower under control. Step the feet further out to make it harder.

⌄
Seated cable row – single arm
To burn x 2Cable row machine1 plate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit at the cable row station with a single arm handle attached. Sit tall with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your torso square throughout — resist the rotation from the single arm. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the end.

Movement: Pull the handle toward your lower abdomen with one arm. Return to full arm extension. Complete all reps before switching sides.

⌄
External rotation – band
To burn x2BandYellow band
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a band anchor, elbow bent at 90°, tucked at your side.

Focus: Keep elbow still and tucked. Never sacrifice range for resistance.

Movement: Rotate forearm outward against the band. Return slowly.

⌄
Shoulder mobility – overhead
10 x 2BandYellow band
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding a band with a wide overhand grip, arms straight out in front at shoulder height.

Focus: Keep the arms straight and the band lightly taut. Go only as far as the shoulders allow, never forcing it. Widen the grip if it's tight.

Movement: With wide straight arms, sweep the band up and over your head and down behind the body, then reverse the same path back to the front.
