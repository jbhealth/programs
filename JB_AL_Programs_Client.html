From James, for you: Keep forearms in contact with hips to save pressure on your lower back.

Anterior tibialis – isometric hold
3 x 30-40sBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on a chair with one leg out in front of you and the heel resting on the floor, knee softly bent. Everything above the ankle stays relaxed.

Movement: Pull the toes and the front of the foot up towards your shin and hold. Start with five seconds at about half effort and rest the same again. Six to eight holds is plenty.

Focus: This is the way back in for an ankle that has been out of action, where loaded raises are still too much. The first hint of a cramp is your ceiling, so work just underneath it and end the set there rather than pushing through. Half effort that never cramps beats full effort that does, and the ceiling moves within a fortnight.
