How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Box squat – goblet
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit/Box5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

Good mornings – hugged
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit4kg or 5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, knees soft, hugging a weight to your chest with both arms, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and hinge forward with a flat back until the hamstrings load, then drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: The weight sits high on the chest, so the back works hard to stay flat. Hinge at the hips, not the waist, and only go as far as that flat back allows.

Chest press
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit/Bench2 x 3kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in each hand at chest height, elbows a little below shoulder line, shoulder blades set back, feet planted.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set back on the bench and the elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, not flared wide, to protect the shoulders. Control the lower rather than dropping.

ISO single leg deadlift – supported
2 sets20s each sideDB3kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Hip adduction machine
3 sets10 repsHip adduction machine19.25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the machine, the inner thighs against the pads, legs apart, back supported.

Movement: Squeeze the thighs together against the pads, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Squeeze together smoothly and control the return, not letting the pads pull the legs wide fast. Keep the back settled, and go only to a comfortable stretch on the return.

Knee extension – cable
2 sets8-10 repsCable machine1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

Hip abduction – cable, side step
2 sets8-10 repsCable machine1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a cuff or band low around one ankle attached to a side cable, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step the working leg out to the side against the resistance, then step it back under control.

Focus: Keep the torso tall and the hips level, stepping from the outer hip, not leaning. Keep the steps controlled, and the working knee soft.

Side plank – feet stacked
3 sets30s each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, forearm down with the elbow under the shoulder, legs straight and feet stacked.

Movement: Lift the hips so the body forms a straight line from head to heels and hold for the set time.

Focus: Keep the hips high and the body in one straight line, resisting the sag towards the floor. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow and the neck long, and stop when the line drops.

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

Start: Sit on a chair with one leg out in front of you and the heel resting on the floor, knee softly bent. Everything above the ankle stays relaxed.

Movement: Pull the toes and the front of the foot up towards your shin and hold. Start with five seconds at about half effort and rest the same again. Six to eight holds is plenty.

Focus: This is the way back in for an ankle that has been out of action, where loaded raises are still too much. Half effort that never cramps beats full effort that does, and the ceiling moves within a fortnight.
