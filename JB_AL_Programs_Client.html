Program 1 — Pain-Free Steps & Knee Stability
WC 1st June 2026
Step-ups – forward low
2 × 10 each sideStep

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

Step-ups – lateral low
2 × 10 each sideStep

Start: Stand to the side of a low step box.

Focus: Keep heel fully in contact with the step — don't let it hang off.

Movement: Step nearest foot up and sideways. Drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower under control.

Wall sit – static hold
2 × 30-45 secWall

Start: Stand with back flat against a wall, feet flat, knees over ankles.

Focus: Keep steady breathing. Build time progressively — 30s, then 60s, then 90s.

Movement: Slide down until thighs are roughly parallel to the floor. Hold for the prescribed time.

Terminal knee extension
2 × 15 eachResistance Band

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.

Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachResistance Band

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Balance – single leg assisted
2 × 30 sec eachWall

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 × 45 sec eachWall

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Reverse crunches
3 × 15Mat

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.
