KB Deadlift (Compound hip extension - back safe)
3 x 8-10 · ModerateKettlebell

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

Goblet Squat & Reach (Hip mobility + functional core)
3 x 8Dumbbell

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Pallof Press (Anti-rotation core stability)
3 x 8 each side · Light-ModerateCable

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Single-Leg Deadlift (Hamstring + stability, supported)
3 x 8 each · Bodyweight or LightBodyweight or Light DB

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square. Feel your hamstring stretching on the way down.

Movement: Hinge at hip, push free leg behind as counterbalance while lowering weight. Drive standing hip forward to return.

Reverse Lunge to Knee Raise (Hip flexor stretch + control)
3 x 8 eachBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Back Extension Machine (Posterior chain + lower back)
3 x 10 · ModerateMachine

Start: Set up on the back extension machine with hips on the pad.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Keep your lower back neutral throughout.

Movement: Lower your torso, extend back to neutral.

Cossack Squat (Hip mobility + ankle control)
3 x 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Pigeon Pose Hold (Hip opener - cyclist-specific mobility)
3 x 45-60 sec each sideBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Cross one ankle over the opposite knee so your leg forms a figure-4 shape.

Focus: Keep your lower back flat on the floor throughout. Breathe steadily and let the stretch develop gradually — don't force it.

Movement: Gently press the top knee away from you until you feel a deep stretch in your buttock. Hold for the required time, then switch sides.

Reverse Crunch (Anti-extension core, back-safe)
3 x 12Bodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.

Dead Bug with Shoulder Reach (Integrated core control)
3 x 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.
