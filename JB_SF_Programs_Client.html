From James, for you: Plastic box medium height.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

Romanian deadlift – BB
3 sets5-8 repsBarbell25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

Bench press – BB
3 sets8-10 repsBarbell/Bench/Rack15kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on the bench, eyes under the bar, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down, a slight natural arch. Grip a little wider than shoulders and unrack over the chest.

Movement: Lower the bar under control to the mid-chest, elbows tucked to about 45 degrees, then press it back up over the shoulders to straight arms.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades pinched back and down throughout, which protects the shoulders, and the elbows tucked rather than flared wide. Keep the feet planted and the wrists stacked, lower under control to the chest, and don't bounce the bar.

Focus: Keep the upper arm fixed at shoulder height and move only the forearm. Use a light band, keep the shoulder from shrugging, and control both directions.

Movement: Reach the arms (and torso if set) out to the sides or forward, challenging balance, then return to centre.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft as you reach, moving slowly. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and return under control.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

Movement: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to push your hips up until the body is level. Hold two seconds, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Push with the glute, not the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

Side plank – with hip dip

Start: Hold a side plank, body in a straight line, shoulder over the elbow.

Movement: Lower the hips slowly towards the floor a short way, then lift back up to the straight line, controlled, for the set reps.

Focus: Control the dip and the lift with the side waist, keeping the body in line front to back. Don't let the hips sag at the bottom or the torso rotate, and keep the shoulder stacked.

Goblet squat and reach

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat and, at the bottom, reach the weight forward to help you sit upright, then draw it back to the chest and stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Use the reach to stay upright rather than tipping forward, and squat only as deep as the knees allow.

Clean – DB

Start: Stand with dumbbells at the thighs or floor, feet hip-width, hinged with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive up through the hips to pull the dumbbells up and catch them at the shoulders, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and drive from the hips and legs, not yanking with the arms or rounding. Catch softly at the shoulders with the ribs down, and control the lower.

Press – landmine, single arm

Start: Hold the end of a landmine bar at one shoulder, standing tall, ribs down, core braced against the offset.

Movement: Press the bar up and slightly forward along its arc until the arm is straight, then lower to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist twisting towards the bar, staying square. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and follow the bar's arc rather than forcing it up.

Start: Sit at the machine, the inner thighs against the pads, legs apart, back supported.

Movement: Squeeze the thighs together against the pads, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Squeeze together smoothly and control the return, not letting the pads pull the legs wide fast. Keep the back settled, and go only to a comfortable stretch on the return.

ISO single leg deadlift – supported

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Sit-ups

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, hands across the chest or lightly behind the head, ribs down.

Movement: Curl up from the floor one vertebra at a time until the torso is upright over the hips, then lower back down under control.

Focus: Curl rather than swing, keeping the neck neutral and the feet down without hooking them under anything. Lower slowly instead of dropping, and stop short of the range if the lower back complains.

Dorsiflexion – banded eccentric

Start: Sit with the leg out in front and a band around the forefoot, anchored ahead of you. Use a heavier band than usual.

Movement: Pull the foot up towards the shin, using the other hand or foot to help if needed, then lower slowly against the band.

Focus: The lowering is the work. Take three or four seconds and resist the whole way. Because you help the foot up, the band can be heavier than the shin could lift alone.

Push-up – Smith machine

Start: Set the Smith machine bar at the height you want and lock it off. Take a push-up position with your hands on the bar, just wider than your shoulders, body in a straight line from head to heels.

Movement: Lower your chest towards the bar under control, pause just short of touching, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: The bar height sets the difficulty. The higher the bar, the easier it is. Keep your body in one line, no sagging through the middle and no piking up at the hips. Keep the elbows tucked to about 45 degrees rather than flaring them out.

Calf raises – eccentric heel drop

Start: Stand with the balls of both feet on the floor or the edge of a step, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise onto the toes on both feet, take the other foot off, then lower that one heel slowly over three to four seconds. Put the foot back down and rise on both again for every rep. Work one side at a time.

Focus: The slow lowering is the part that does the work, so give it the full three or four seconds and do not let the heel drop away. Going up on two feet and down on one lets you load the lowering long before you can rise on that leg alone, which is why this comes before the single leg version. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out.

3 x 12 each · Bodyweight
Anterior tibialis – toe raises off a step

Start: Stand on a step or box with the heels on the surface and the toes hanging out over the edge. Hold a rail or frame for balance.

Movement: Let the toes lower below the level of the step under control, then pull them up towards the shins as high as they will go.

Focus: This is about range, not load. The edge lets the foot drop below level, which is where the shin has to work hardest and where most people have never trained. Go as low as the ankle allows before pulling back up, and keep the knees straight throughout.

Broad jump – repeated

Movement: Jump forward and land softly, then immediately load and jump again, for the set reps.

Focus: Land softly with the knees tracking over the toes, absorbing through the hips and knees each time. Keep every landing controlled before the next jump, not rushing into a sloppy rep.

Pivot lunges

Movement: Hop forward onto the other foot, then spring back to the leg you started on. Do all the reps forwards, rest, then work the same way out to the side, and finish with a turn in the air, landing behind and across.

Focus: Land softly and control the spring back, keeping the knee in line with the hip and ankle. Finish one direction before you start the next, and take the rest in between.
