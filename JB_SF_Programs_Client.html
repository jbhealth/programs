From James, for you: 2 seconds up, 2 seconds down.

Start: Sit in the leg press with the feet placed high on the platform, hip to shoulder-width, hips and back into the pad, core braced.

Movement: Lower under control to a comfortable depth, then press up through the heels, keeping the knees soft at the top. The high foot position shifts more work to the hips and hamstrings.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the hips flat on the pad. Press through the heels, control the depth, and avoid rounding the lower back at the bottom.

From James, for you: Remember to engage your hips/glutes both on the way up but also on the landing (soft landing).

Start: Stand side-on to a step holding a dumbbell in each hand, near foot ready to step onto it.

Movement: Step the near foot up and drive through the heel to stand tall on the step, then lower back down with control, and repeat.

Focus: Keep the stepping knee over the foot, not caving in, and the hips level. Drive through the heel, control the lower, and keep the torso tall rather than leaning.

Box squat – goblet

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

3 x 8 · 5kg
Cable pull-through

Start: Face away from a low cable, rope between your legs, feet shoulder-width. Step forward to take the tension, hands holding the rope, soft knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and let the rope draw between your legs with a flat back, then drive the hips forward to stand tall and squeeze the glutes.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips, not the arms, the arms just hold the rope. Keep the back flat, and finish standing tall without leaning back or arching.

3 x 12 · 15.25kg
Cable hip extension

Start: Stand facing a low cable with a cuff around one ankle, holding the frame for balance, torso tall.

Movement: Drive the working leg straight back from the hip, squeezing the glute, then return under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the glute and keep the leg fairly straight, not arching the lower back to gain range. Keep the torso tall and the standing knee soft, and control the return.

3 x 12 each · 1.5kg
Hip abduction machine – slow eccentric

Start: Sit at the machine, outer thighs against the pads, back supported, feet on the rests.

Movement: Push the thighs apart, then return slowly over three to four seconds under control.

Focus: Push apart smoothly and control the slow return, not letting the pads snap in. Keep the back settled, and feel the outer hips work on the return.

3 x 12 · 35.75kg
ISO split squat – weighted

Start: Hold weights at the sides or a weight at the chest in a split stance, torso tall.

Movement: Lower straight down until both knees are bent towards right angles and hold that bottom position still for the set time.

Focus: Keep the front knee over the foot, not caving in, and the torso upright through the hold. Keep the weight balanced between both legs, and hold only at a depth the knees tolerate.

3 x 20 sec each · Moderate
Cable abductor – side step

Start: Stand with a cuff or band low around one ankle attached to a side cable, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step the working leg out to the side against the resistance, then step it back under control.

Focus: Keep the torso tall and the hips level, stepping from the outer hip, not leaning. Keep the steps controlled, and the working knee soft.

3 x 12 each · 1.5kg
Balance – single leg knee drive

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other knee ready to drive up, arms ready.

Movement: Drive the free knee up towards hip height, hold the balance briefly, then lower under control and repeat.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, staying tall as the knee drives up. Fix the eyes on a point, control the drive and lower, and keep the core engaged.

3 x 30 sec each · Light
Farmer's carries

Start: Stand tall holding a heavy weight in each hand at the sides, feet hip-width, ribs down, shoulders back.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and steady.

Focus: Stand tall with the ribs down and shoulders back, not letting the weights pull you into a slump. Take steady steps, brace the core, and keep the weights from swinging.

3 x 30 m · Moderate
Bird-dog

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill

Start: Half kneel or stand with one foot forward, flat on the floor, toes pointing straight ahead.

Movement: Gently drive the knee forward over the toes, keeping the heel down, to a comfortable stretch at the front of the ankle, then return.

Focus: Keep the heel firmly down and let the knee travel over the toes only to a comfortable range. Move slowly, keep the foot pointing straight, and don't force the stretch.

Wall sit – static hold

Start: Stand with your back flat against a wall, feet a stride out in front and hip-width apart.

Movement: Slide down the wall until your knees are bent towards a right angle, thighs working, and hold the position for the set time.

Focus: Keep the knees stacked over the ankles, not drifting past the toes, and the whole back in contact with the wall. Drop only as low as your knees stay comfortable, and breathe steadily through the hold.
