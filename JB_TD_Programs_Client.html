Warm-Up
Wall slides
2 sets10 repsBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with your back, head and arms against a wall, feet a few centimetres out so the lower back stays flat, elbows bent to 90 degrees in a goalpost.

Movement: Slide the arms slowly up towards overhead keeping every point of contact, then slide back down under control.

Focus: Keep the whole back and both arms on the wall throughout. The moment the lower back arches or the hands lift away is your limit, so work just short of it, and breathe steadily.

Lateral band walks
2 sets10 repsBodyweightModerate

Start: Stand with a band around the legs (ankles or above the knees), feet hip-width, knees softly bent, torso tall.

Movement: Step sideways one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps, then return the other way.

Focus: Keep tension on the band throughout and the feet pointing forward, not letting the knees cave in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips work.

Main Circuit
Box squat
3 sets10 repsBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand in front of a box or chair, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Box height is set to suit, so work to the height given in the notes.

Movement: Push the hips back and sit down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall by driving through the heels. Control the descent all the way down.

Focus: Keep the knees pushed out in line with the middle toes and the chest up as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping onto it, and drive up through the heels.

Deadlift
3 sets8 repsBodyweightModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with the weight on the floor between your feet, feet hip to shoulder-width. Push the hips back and hinge to take hold of it with both hands, back flat, chest up, shoulders over the weight.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall, pushing the hips forward and squeezing the glutes at the top. Hinge the hips back to lower the weight under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat from start to finish, never rounded. The power comes from the hips and legs, not the lower back or arms. Keep the weight close to your body on the way up and down.

Low cable row – standing
3 sets10 repsBodyweightModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand at a low cable, a handle in both hands, arms extended, chest up, staggered stance, core braced.

Movement: Pull the handle towards the abdomen, driving the elbows back, then return under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbows and squeeze the shoulder blades, keeping the torso still rather than leaning back to pull. Keep the ribs down and the shoulders down.

Press – landmine
3 sets8 each sideBodyweightModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold the end of a landmine bar at one shoulder, standing tall or half kneeling, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Press the bar up and slightly forward along its arc until the arm is straight, then lower back to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and the core braced so the lower back doesn't arch. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and follow the bar's arc rather than forcing it straight up.

Suitcase carry
3 sets20m each sideBodyweightModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a weight in one hand at the side like a suitcase, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso upright and level.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the weighted side, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the ribs down. Take steady steps, brace hard, and don't let the weight swing.

Balance – single leg knee drive
3 sets30s each sideBodyweightModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other knee ready to drive up, arms ready.

Movement: Drive the free knee up towards hip height, hold the balance briefly, then lower under control and repeat.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, staying tall as the knee drives up. Fix the eyes on a point, control the drive and lower, and keep the core engaged.

Cool-Down
Cat-cow
2 sets8 repsBodyweightBodyweight

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, spine in a neutral start.

Movement: Slowly arch the back and lift the head (cow), then round the back and tuck the chin (cat), flowing between the two with the breath.

Focus: Move slowly and segment by segment through the spine, only to a comfortable range. Let the breath lead, and keep the movement gentle rather than forcing either end.

90/90 hip flow
2 sets6 each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Sit on the floor with one leg bent in front at 90 degrees and the other out to the side at 90 degrees, sitting tall.

Movement: Flow the knees from side to side, rotating through both hips, pausing where you feel a gentle stretch, then continuing.

Focus: Move from the hips with the torso tall, easing into each side rather than forcing. Use the hands for support, and stay within a comfortable, pain-free range.
