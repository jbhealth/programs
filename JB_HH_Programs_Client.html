Back-Safe Foundation & Functional Core
KB Deadlift (Compound hip extension - back safe)
3 × 8-10Dumbbell14kg

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

Goblet squat
3 × 8Kettlebell8kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Pallof press
To burn eachCableRed

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Single leg deadlift – KB
3 × 8 eachKettlebell2.5kg plate

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell in the opposite hand.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the kettlebell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance. Drive through your heel to return.

Curtsey lunge to knee raise
2 x 10 eachBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Control the landing before the next rep. Feel the glute med and hip flexors working.

Movement: Perform curtsey lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward as you return.

Back extension machine – bilateral
2 x burnBack extension machineBodyweight.

Start: Set up on the back extension machine with hips on the pad.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Keep your lower back neutral throughout.

Movement: Lower your torso, extend back to neutral.

Wood chop – med ball / DB
2 × 10 eachMedicine ball6kg

Start: Hold a medicine ball or dumbbell with both hands overhead to one side.

Focus: Power comes from your hips and obliques — not just arms.

Movement: Rotate and chop explosively diagonally down toward opposite hip. Return under control.

Waiter carry – single arm
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell8kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

Dead bug – arms and legs
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Reverse crunches
2 x 10Matweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.
