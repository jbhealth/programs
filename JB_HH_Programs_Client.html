How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Box squat – weighted
3 sets8-10 repsDB/Box14kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about knee height holding a light weight at the chest, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall through the heels, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the chest up. Sit back under control rather than dropping onto the box, and drive up through the heels.

Good mornings – band
3 sets10 repsBandPurple band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on the middle of a band, feet hip-width, and loop the other end behind the neck and across the upper back. Soft bend in the knees, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and hinge the torso forward with a flat back until the hamstrings load, then drive the hips forward to stand tall against the band.

Focus: Keep the back flat the whole way. Hinge at the hips rather than the waist, and let the band pull you forward only as far as that flat back allows. The band gets harder as you stand up, so drive the hips through to finish.

Back extension – machine
1 set22 repsBack extension machine/GHDLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set the pad at the top of your hips so you can bend freely. Cross your arms over your chest or place your hands by your temples, body in a straight line.

Movement: Hinge down at the hips with control, then raise back up until your body is straight. Move smoothly, no jerking.

Focus: Stop at a straight line, don't arch past it into the lower back. Lead the lift with the glutes and hamstrings, and keep the neck in line rather than craning up.

Neutral grip press
3 sets10 repsAny kit/Bench2 x 8kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in each hand at chest height with palms facing each other, elbows tucked, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, keeping the palms facing, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the elbows tucked close, which is easier on the shoulders. Control the lower to a comfortable stretch, and keep the ribs down.

Seated cable row – close grip
3 sets10 repsCable row machine35.75kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the low cable, feet braced, a close handle in both hands, arms extended, chest up, back tall.

Movement: Pull the handle to the lower abdomen, driving the elbows back close to the body, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Drive the elbows back and squeeze the shoulder blades, not just bending the arms. Keep the back tall and still, no rocking, and keep the shoulders down.

Knee extension – cable
3 sets10 each sideCable machine4.25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

Drop landing – bilateral
3 sets5 repsBox/BenchBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on a low box or step, feet hip-width, ready to step off, core braced.

Movement: Step off and land softly on both feet in a quarter-squat, absorbing through the hips and knees, then stand.

Focus: Land softly and quietly, absorbing through the hips and knees, with the knees in line with the middle toes, not caving in. Aim for a stable, controlled catch rather than a stiff or collapsing landing.

Elbow plank – arm reach
3 sets30 secMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up in a forearm plank, straight line from head to heels, core and glutes braced.

Movement: Keeping the hips level, reach one arm forward off the floor, hold briefly, return, then the other.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square as you reach, resisting any twist. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and keep the reach controlled so the line stays straight.

For your game: A braced, stable trunk holds your posture and spine angle steady through the swing.

Squat – landmine

Start: Hold the end of a landmine bar at your chest with both hands, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, letting the bar's arc keep you upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. The bar helps you stay upright, so keep the chest tall, and squat only as deep as the knees stay comfortable.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

3 x 8-10 · 20kg bar + 20kg
Romanian deadlift – BB

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

For your game: Hinging from the hips, not the back, is how you hold your posture and load into the ground in the swing.

Curtsey lunge with hop

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, knees soft, core braced.

Movement: Step one leg back and across into a curtsey lunge, then drive up into a small hop, landing softly and repeating.

Focus: Land softly with the front knee in line with the middle toes, not caving in, absorbing through the hip and knee. Keep the movement controlled, and keep the hop low and manageable.

For your game: Explosive hip drive here is the same ground-up power you snap through impact.

Chest press – single arm

Start: Lie on a bench, a weight in one hand at chest height, the other arm out for balance, shoulder blades set, core braced.

Movement: Press the weight up until the arm is nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and resist the torso twisting towards the free side. Keep the elbow at about 45 degrees, not flared, and control the lower.

For your game: Keeping your shoulders and arms in good condition helps you swing freely and without pain.

March – front rack

Movement: Lift one knee to hip height, pause, lower it under control, then repeat on the other side for the set time or reps.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and the back from arching as each foot leaves the floor, and keep the hips level rather than dropping on the lifted side. Move slowly, with no bounce or swing between steps.

For your game: A trunk that stays quiet while the legs move keeps your posture steady through the swing.

Movement: Straighten the knees to raise the pad, then lower under control, for higher repetitions.

Focus: Keep the weight light and every rep smooth and controlled, never kicking. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, building the thigh without stressing the knee.

For your game: Keeps your knees strong and comfortable for walking a full round.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.
