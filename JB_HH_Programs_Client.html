Main Circuit
⌄
Cable pull-through
2 x 12Cable machine20.75kg

Start: Set a cable at the lowest position

Focus: Stand facing away from the machine and hold the rope between your legs

Movement: Hinge forward, pushing hips back, then drive them forward explosively to stand. This is an excellent hip hinge pattern reinforcer and glute

⌄
Dumbbell squat
2 x 10DB2 x 5kg

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

Focus: Keep chest tall and torso upright. Knees track over toes.

Movement: Squat to depth under control. Drive through heels to stand.

⌄
Seated row – machine
2 x 10Seated row machine24.75kg

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

⌄
Lat pulldown
2 x 10Lat pulldown machine19.25kg

Start: Sit at lat pulldown machine, grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width.

Focus: Drive elbows down toward hips. Don't use momentum.

Movement: Lean back slightly and pull bar to chin level. Squeeze at bottom, control return to full extension.

⌄
Suitcase carry
2 x to burnDB/KB14kg

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

⌄
Bird-dog
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

⌄
Pallof press – tall kneeling
2 x 10 each sideCable machine9.75kg

Start: Assume tall kneeling position — both knees on floor, hips extended.

Focus: Resist the rotational pull. Keep your hips square and facing forward.

Movement: Press out from chest to full arm extension. Without feet support, core works harder.

⌄
Balance – single leg assisted
3 x 20s each sideWallBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.
