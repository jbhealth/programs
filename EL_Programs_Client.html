Program 1 — Compound Strength & Conditioning
WC 1st June 2026
Goblet squat
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Romanian deadlift
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding weight in front of your thighs.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout — feel the stretch develop in your hamstrings.

Movement: Push hips back and lower weight down the front of your legs. Drive hips forward to return.

Step-ups – forward low
2 × 10 eachStep

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

DB single-arm press
2 × 10 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

TRX row – bodyweight
2 × 12TRX

Start: Face the TRX anchor, hold handles with palms down. Lean back to create load.

Focus: The more horizontal you lean, the harder the row. Drive elbows back strongly.

Movement: Pull your chest toward the handles by driving elbows back. Lower with control.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance – single leg free
2 × 30 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCable

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Dead bug – arms only
2 × 10 eachMat

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time — this is non-negotiable.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead toward the floor, return, then alternate sides. Never let your lower back arch.

Side plank – knees bent
2 × 20-30 sec eachMat

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Keep your hips straight — don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

Ski Erg — Cardio Finisher
3 × 30 sec on / 30 sec offSki Erg

Focus: Full body conditioning. Drive from the hips, not just the arms. Keep consistent pace across all intervals.

Movement: Stand on the footplates, grip handles overhead. Pull down and back explosively, hinging at the hips. Return arms overhead with control and repeat.
