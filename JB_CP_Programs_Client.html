Barbell Strength & Hip Stability
Front squats
4 x 6Barbell27.5kg

Start: Hold the barbell across your front deltoids with elbows high and pointing forward. Feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep your elbows high throughout — if they drop, the bar will roll forward. This requires significant thoracic mobility.

Movement: Squat with an upright torso to parallel or below. Drive through your heels to stand, keeping elbows pointing forward throughout.

Hip thrust – BB
3 x 8Barbell/BenchModerate

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Bent-over row – BB overhand
3 x 8BarbellModerate

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge forward to roughly 45° with a flat back and overhand grip on the barbell.

Focus: Drive your elbows back hard and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top.

Movement: Pull the bar toward your lower ribs. Squeeze at the top. Return under control.

DB chest press
3 x 10DB/BenchModerate

Start: Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Use the full range of motion. Control the lowering phase — don't let the dumbbells drop.

Movement: Press both dumbbells upward to full arm extension, allowing them to naturally travel slightly inward at the top. Lower under control.

KB goblet lateral lunge
3 x 8 each sideKBModerate

Start: Stand tall holding a kettlebell at chest height in goblet position.

Focus: The goblet position adds an anterior load that increases core demand throughout. Keep your chest tall.

Movement: Step wide to the side into a lateral lunge, bending that knee while the other stays straight. Drive through the bent heel to return.

Cable hip abduction
3 x 12 each sideCable machineModerate

Start: Attach ankle strap to cable machine at lowest setting. Stand side-on to machine.

Focus: Keep your torso upright. Resist leaning away from the cable.

Movement: Step away from machine and lift attached leg out to side against cable.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 x 12 each sideWallBodyweight

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
3 x 25s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Pallof press
3 x 10 each sideCable/BandLight

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.
