Barbell Strength & Hip Stability
Week commencing: 8 June 2026
Main Circuit
Barbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 6-8Barbell–

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell in a double overhand grip just outside your legs. Stand tall with the bar at hip height.

Focus: This is a hip hinge — keep your knees soft but nearly straight. Feel the hamstring stretch as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body throughout.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch. Drive your hips forward to return to standing, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Barbell back squat
2 × 5Barbell–

Start: Position the bar across your upper traps (high bar) or rear delts (low bar). Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Unrack and take two steps back.

Focus: Keep your chest up and core braced throughout. Track knees over toes — push them out as you descend. Depth to parallel or below if mobility allows.

Movement: Break at the hips and knees simultaneously, descending under control. Drive through your heels to stand, extending hips and knees together. Lock out at the top without hyperextending.

Single-leg deadlift to overhead press – DB
2 × 8 eachDumbbell–

Start: Stand tall on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height, arm relaxed. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your hips square and back flat through the hinge — resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg. Stay tall and braced before pressing, and only press once you're balanced. Control every phase.

Movement: Hinge at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor as the free leg extends behind as a counterbalance, until your torso is near parallel. Drive through the standing heel to return to tall. Standing tall, press the dumbbell overhead to full extension, then lower it back to hip height ready for the next rep.

Cossack squat
2 × 8 eachBodyweight–

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Dead hang – timed
2 × 30-45sPull-up bar–

Start: Reach up and take an overhand grip on the bar, hands shoulder-width. Step or hop up so you are hanging with arms straight, feet off the floor (use a box to assist if needed).

Focus: Build grip and shoulder stability and decompress the spine. Keep shoulders gently engaged (not fully shrugged to the ears), ribs down, body still.

Movement: Hang for the target time, controlled breathing, then lower under control. Rest and repeat.

DB single-arm press
2 × 8 eachDumbbell–

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Calf raises – loaded single leg
2 × 12 eachKettlebell–

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachBand–

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Copenhagen plank – short lever
2 × 20-30s eachBench–

Start: Set up in side plank with lower shin resting on a bench.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

Movement: Other leg hovers below bench — unsupported. Hold position.

Ankle dorsiflexion drill
2 × 45s eachWall–

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.
