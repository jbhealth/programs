Program 1 — Ankle Mobility & Hip Opening
WC 1st June 2026
Goblet Squat
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Cossack Squat
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Suitcase Carry
2 × 20 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance — Single Leg Free
2 × 30 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Ankle Alphabet
2 × 26 letters eachBodyweight

Start: Sit in a chair with your back straight and one foot lifted just off the floor. Rest your hands on your thighs.

Focus: Keep your leg completely still — all the movement should come from your ankle only, nothing else.

Movement: Use your big toe like a pen and trace every letter of the alphabet in the air. Go through all 26 letters, then swap to the other foot.

Ankle Dorsiflexion Drill
2 × 45 sec eachWall

Start: Stand facing a wall with your foot close to it.

Focus: Keep heel flat on the ground — all movement is at the ankle.

Movement: Bend knee forward, trying to touch wall while heel stays flat. Progressively move foot further from wall.

Bird-Dog — Hold & Pulse
2 × 10 each sideMat

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

Side Plank — Knees Bent
2 × 20-30 sec eachMat

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Keep your hips straight — don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

Program 2 — Ankle Stability & Lower Body Strength
WC 1st June 2026
Dumbbell Squat
2 × 10Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

Focus: Keep chest tall and torso upright. Knees track over toes.

Movement: Squat to depth under control. Drive through heels to stand.

Romanian Deadlift — Single Leg
2 × 8 each sideDumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding weight in front of your thighs.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout — feel the stretch develop in your hamstrings.

Movement: Push hips back and lower weight down the front of your legs. Drive hips forward to return.

Waiter Carry — Single Arm
2 × 20 stepsDumbbell

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

Balance — Single Leg with Arm Reach
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Calf Raises — Slow Eccentric
2 × 10Bodyweight

Start: Stand with feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The single leg eccentric (lowering) phase is the therapeutic component. Go slow.

Movement: Rise onto both feet, transfer to one foot. Lower that heel slowly over 4 seconds. Rise on both feet each rep.

Pivot Lunges
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft bend in the knee.

Focus: Land softly on each step and control the pivot back. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Build confidence in each direction before moving to the next.

Movement: Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to the starting position. Repeat for all reps, then move to lateral — spring out to the side and pivot back. Finally rotational — jump and rotate in the air, planting your foot behind and diagonally. Complete all reps in each direction before moving to the next.

Glute Bridges — Single Leg
2 × 10 each sideMat

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with foot flat, other leg extended.

Focus: Keep hips level — don't let one side drop.

Movement: Drive through the bent knee heel to push hips up. Hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

Elbow Plank — Arm Reach
2 × 8 each sideMat

Start: Lie face down, elbows under shoulders, forearms on the floor.

Focus: Brace your core tight. Keep your body in a straight line — hips don't sag or pike.

Movement: Lift your body to create a straight line from head to heels. Hold for the prescribed time, breathing steadily.
