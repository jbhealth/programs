Main Circuit
Goblet squat – pause at bottom
3 x 8KB/DBModerate

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell in front of chest.

Focus: At the bottom, keep your chest tall and lower back neutral. Hold the tension.

Movement: Squat down and pause 3 seconds at bottom. Drive through heels to stand.

Cable pull-through
3 x 12Cable machineModerate

Start: Set a cable at the lowest position

Focus: Stand facing away from the machine and hold the rope between your legs

Movement: Hinge forward, pushing hips back, then drive them forward explosively to stand. This is an excellent hip hinge pattern reinforcer and glute

Reverse lunge – bodyweight
3 x 10 each legBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

Hip thrust – BB
3 x 10Barbell/BenchModerate

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

Hip abduction – standing band
3 x 12 each sideBandLight

Start: Fix a band around your ankles and stand on one leg.

Focus: Standing knee slightly bent, torso upright. Don't lean to the side.

Movement: Slowly lift other leg out to the side against band resistance. Control both lift and return.

Calf raises – slow eccentric
3 x 12BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The single leg eccentric (lowering) phase is the therapeutic component. Go slow.

Movement: Rise onto both feet, transfer to one foot. Lower that heel slowly over 4 seconds. Rise on both feet each rep.

Seated row – machine
3 x 10Seated row machineModerate

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
3 x 20s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Dead bug – arms and legs
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.
