Box squats – high box
2 × 10Boxweight

Start: Stand in front of a high box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees in line with hips and ankles throughout. Use heel drive to stand.

Movement: Sit back onto the box with control, then stand fully using your heels.

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Dumbbellweight

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

Step-ups – lateral low
2 × 10 eachStepweight

Start: Stand to the side of a low step box.

Focus: Keep heel fully in contact with the step — don't let it hang off.

Movement: Step nearest foot up and sideways. Drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower under control.

Seated row – machine
2 × 10Seated row machineweight

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

Shoulder press – seated DB
2 × 10Dumbbellweight

Start: Sit tall with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Core braced, lower back neutral. Don't arch your lower back.

Movement: Press both dumbbells directly overhead to full arm extension. Lower to shoulder height under control.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbellweight

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 × 8 eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Calf raises – bilateral
2 × 12Stepweight

Start: Stand tall, feet flat on the floor or edge of a step.

Focus: Go through the full range — let heels dip below the step on the way down.

Movement: Rise up onto the balls of your feet as high as possible. Lower slowly through full range.

Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachBandweight

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbellweight

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Dead bug – arms and legs
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Bird-dog
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.
