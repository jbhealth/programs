How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Box squat – weighted
3 sets10-12 repsDB/Box5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about knee height holding a light weight at the chest, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall through the heels, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the chest up. Sit back under control rather than dropping onto the box, and drive up through the heels.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

Hip external rotation – seated, band
3 sets10 repsBand/ChairLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit tall on a bench or chair with the knees bent at 90 degrees and a band round the working ankle, fixed out to the side of that leg.

Movement: Swing the lower leg in across the other leg against the band, turning at the hip, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change legs.

Focus: Keep the knee still and the thigh on the seat, so the turn comes from the hip. Sit tall and move slowly in both directions. The foot moves in as the thigh turns out.

For your game: Hip rotation strength lets you turn into your trail hip and fire through your lead hip without strain.

Hip internal rotation – seated, band
3 sets10 repsBand/ChairLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit tall on a bench or chair with the knees bent at 90 degrees and a band round the working ankle, fixed to the inside of that leg.

Movement: Swing the lower leg out to the side against the band, turning at the hip, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change legs.

Focus: Keep the knee still and the thigh on the seat, so the turn comes from the hip. Sit tall and move slowly in both directions. The foot moves out as the thigh turns in.

For your game: Turning into your lead hip at impact needs strength at the end of its range, which this builds.

Hip abduction – cable, side step
3 sets10-12 repsCable machine1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a cuff or band low around one ankle attached to a side cable, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step the working leg out to the side against the resistance, then step it back under control.

Focus: Keep the torso tall and the hips level, stepping from the outer hip, not leaning. Keep the steps controlled, and the working knee soft.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

Single leg deadlift – with rotation
3 sets10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight, soft standing knee, the other leg ready to reach behind, core braced.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight while adding a small controlled rotation of the torso, then return to standing tall and square.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hips level, and let the rotation come from the upper body, small and controlled. Move slowly, balance and control matter more than depth.

For your game: A hinge with rotation under control is the closest thing in the gym to holding your posture through the swing.

Anterior tibialis – machine
2 sets12-15 repsTibialis machine7.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the tibialis machine, or on a bench with a tib bar, with your heels resting on the edge and the padded bar across the top of your feet. Let your toes point down to start.

Movement: Pull your toes up towards your shins as far as you can, pause, then lower them slowly back down.

Focus: Keep your knees still and move only at the ankles. Lift and lower slowly through the full range, and keep both feet working evenly.

For your game: Strong shins decelerate the foot and protect the knees over a long round.

Lateral walks – band, diagonal
3 sets10-12 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and outward alternately, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees driving out, not caving. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips working each step.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

Squat – trap bar
How to
2 x 8-10Moderate

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Use the raised handles where the bar has them. Squat down with the chest tall and grip the handles in the middle.

Movement: Drive up through the heels to stand tall, then sit straight down between the handles, the torso staying upright. A squat with the load at your sides, so nothing sits on the back or shoulders.

Focus: Corkscrew the feet into the floor so the knees stay in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and keep the heels down. Sit down rather than back, and take depth only as far as the knees are comfortable.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

Romanian deadlift – staggered stance
How to
2 x 8-1010kg

Start: One foot flat, the other half a step back on its toes. Weight in front of the thighs, soft bend in the front knee.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front leg until the hamstring loads, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. Swap legs halfway.

Focus: Most of the weight sits on the front leg, the back foot is only there for balance. Back flat, hips square, not twisting open.

For your game: Hinging from the hips, not the back, is how you hold your posture and load into the ground in the swing.

Hip abduction – band, standing
How to
2 x 8-10Yellow band

Start: Stand with a band around the ankles or lower legs, holding a support, torso tall.

Movement: Take the working leg out to the side against the band, keeping it straight, then return under control.

Focus: Keep the leg straight and the torso tall, not leaning to lift, and the hips level. Lead with the heel, keep it slow, and feel the outer hip.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

Hamstring curl – Swiss ball, bilateral
How to
2 x 8-10Bodyweight

Start: Lie on your back, heels on a Swiss ball, legs straight, hips lifted so the body is in a straight line, arms flat.

Movement: Curl the ball towards the buttocks by bending the knees, keeping the hips lifted, then roll back out under control.

Focus: Keep the hips lifted and level, not dropping, and the ball steady. Keep the core braced, and control the roll back out.

For your game: Strong hamstrings protect your knees and back over a hilly round.

Anterior tibialis – machine
How to
3 x 107.5kg

Movement: Pull your toes up towards your shins as far as you can, pause, then lower them slowly back down.

Focus: Keep your knees still and move only at the ankles. Lift and lower slowly through the full range, and keep both feet working evenly.

For your game: Strong shins decelerate the foot and protect the knees over a long round.

Single leg toe tap – supported
How to
2 x 20s each sideModerate

Start: Stand tall on one leg, a weight in the hand opposite the standing leg, ribs down, fingertips on a wall or chair back beside you.

Movement: Tap the free toe lightly to the floor in front, to the side, then behind, returning to a tall single-leg stance between each. Swap sides halfway.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the weighted side, hips and shoulders level. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

For your game: Steady single-leg control keeps your hips level through the swing and your weight shift repeatable.

Back extension – machine, paused
How to
3 x 10Bodyweight

Start: Set the hip pad below the hip bones, body straight, hands crossed on the chest or by the temples.

Movement: Lower with control, then raise to a straight line and pause for two seconds, holding still before lowering again.

Focus: Hold the pause at a straight line, not arched past it. Keep the glutes and hamstrings working through the hold and the neck in line with the spine.

For your game: Builds a resilient back so you stay comfortable bending, lifting and walking through 18 holes.

Lateral lunge – goblet
How to
3 x 8-105kg

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step wide to one side, sitting the hips back into the bent leg while the other stays fairly straight, then push back to centre.

Focus: Keep the bent knee over the foot, not caving in, and the chest up with the bell close. Sit into the hip rather than the knee, and push back through the heel.

Deadlift – trap bar, high handles
How to
3 x 8-1030kg

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar with the raised handles facing up, feet hip-width. Hinge down only as far as the handles, grip them in the middle, chest up, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, then sit the hips back and lower the bar under control to the floor. The raised handles shorten the pull, so the start is higher and kinder on the back and hips.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the chest proud at the bottom, not rounding to reach the handles. Push through the heels and finish tall without leaning back.

ISO single leg deadlift – supported
How to
3 x 30s each sideModerate

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Hip abduction machine – slow eccentric
How to
3 x 8-1024.75kg

Start: Sit at the machine, outer thighs against the pads, back supported, feet on the rests.

Movement: Push the thighs apart, then return slowly over three to four seconds under control.

Focus: Push apart smoothly and control the slow return, not letting the pads snap in. Keep the back settled, and feel the outer hips work on the return.

Anti-rotation press – BB
How to
3 x 10-12 each side15kg bar + 5kg

Start: Hold the end of a landmine barbell at your chest with both hands, feet shoulder-width, core braced.

Movement: Press the bar out and slightly across, resisting the arc that wants to rotate you, then bring it back to the chest under control.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, resisting the rotational pull of the bar's arc. Keep the core braced and the back neutral, and press smoothly without twisting the torso.

Hand plank
How to
3 x 30sBodyweight

Start: Hands on the floor directly under your shoulders, arms straight, legs extended back with the toes tucked under.

Movement: Lift the hips to form a straight line and hold that position for the set time, breathing steadily throughout.

Focus: Keep a straight line from head to heels, hips neither sagging nor piking, and the ribs down. Push the floor away through the hands so the shoulder blades stay set rather than sinking between them. Come down when the line breaks or you feel it in the lower back.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes
How to
3 x 30 secBodyweight

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.

For your game: Loosening the band across the lower back lets the hips turn without the spine being dragged into it.

Leg press – wide stance
How to
3 x 1035.75kg

Start: Sit in the leg press, feet wide on the platform with the toes turned out, hips and back into the pad, core braced.

Movement: Lower under control to a comfortable depth, then press back up without locking out hard. The wide stance brings in the inner thigh and glutes.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking out over the toes in line with the turned-out feet, never caving in. Keep the hips flat and control the depth to what feels comfortable.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips, not the arms, the arms just hold the rope. Keep the back flat, and finish standing tall without leaning back or arching.

Anterior tibialis – toe raises off a step
How to
3 x 15Bodyweight

Start: Stand on a step or box with the heels on the surface and the toes hanging out over the edge. Hold a rail or frame for balance.

Movement: Let the toes lower below the level of the step under control, then pull them up towards the shins as high as they will go.
