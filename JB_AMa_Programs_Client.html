How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Lateral lunge – goblet
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step wide to one side, sitting the hips back into the bent leg while the other stays fairly straight, then push back to centre.

Focus: Keep the bent knee over the foot, not caving in, and the chest up with the bell close. Sit into the hip rather than the knee, and push back through the heel.

Deadlift – trap bar, high handles
3 sets8-10 repsTrap bar30kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar with the raised handles facing up, feet hip-width. Hinge down only as far as the handles, grip them in the middle, chest up, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, then sit the hips back and lower the bar under control to the floor. The raised handles shorten the pull, so the start is higher and kinder on the back and hips.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the chest proud at the bottom, not rounding to reach the handles. Push through the heels and finish tall without leaning back.

ISO single leg deadlift – supported
3 sets30s each sideDBModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Hip abduction machine – slow eccentric
3 sets8-10 repsHip abduction machine24.75kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the machine, outer thighs against the pads, back supported, feet on the rests.

Movement: Push the thighs apart, then return slowly over three to four seconds under control.

Focus: Push apart smoothly and control the slow return, not letting the pads snap in. Keep the back settled, and feel the outer hips work on the return.

Anti-rotation press – BB
3 sets10-12 each sideLandmine/Barbell15kg bar + 5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold the end of a landmine barbell at your chest with both hands, feet shoulder-width, core braced.

Movement: Press the bar out and slightly across, resisting the arc that wants to rotate you, then bring it back to the chest under control.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, resisting the rotational pull of the bar's arc. Keep the core braced and the back neutral, and press smoothly without twisting the torso.

Back extension – machine
3 sets10 repsBack extension machine/GHDModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set the pad at the top of your hips so you can bend freely. Cross your arms over your chest or place your hands by your temples, body in a straight line.

Movement: Hinge down at the hips with control, then raise back up until your body is straight. Move smoothly, no jerking.

Focus: Stop at a straight line, don't arch past it into the lower back. Lead the lift with the glutes and hamstrings, and keep the neck in line rather than craning up.

For your game: Builds a resilient back so you stay comfortable bending, lifting and walking through 18 holes.

Pallof press
3 sets10 repsCable/BandRed band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

For your game: Resisting the pull trains your core to control rotation, so your swing stays repeatable.

Hand plank
3 sets30sMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hands on the floor directly under your shoulders, arms straight, legs extended back with the toes tucked under.

Movement: Lift the hips to form a straight line and hold that position for the set time, breathing steadily throughout.

Focus: Keep a straight line from head to heels, hips neither sagging nor piking, and the ribs down. Push the floor away through the hands so the shoulder blades stay set rather than sinking between them. Come down when the line breaks or you feel it in the lower back.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.

For your game: Loosening the band across the lower back lets the hips turn without the spine being dragged into it.

Leg press – wide stance

Start: Sit in the leg press, feet wide on the platform with the toes turned out, hips and back into the pad, core braced.

Movement: Lower under control to a comfortable depth, then press back up without locking out hard. The wide stance brings in the inner thigh and glutes.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking out over the toes in line with the turned-out feet, never caving in. Keep the hips flat and control the depth to what feels comfortable.

Pull-through – cable

Start: Face away from a low cable, rope between your legs, feet shoulder-width. Step forward to take the tension, hands holding the rope, soft knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and let the rope draw between your legs with a flat back, then drive the hips forward to stand tall and squeeze the glutes.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips, not the arms, the arms just hold the rope. Keep the back flat, and finish standing tall without leaning back or arching.

Anterior tibialis – toe raises off a step

Start: Stand on a step or box with the heels on the surface and the toes hanging out over the edge. Hold a rail or frame for balance.

Movement: Let the toes lower below the level of the step under control, then pull them up towards the shins as high as they will go.

Focus: This is about range, not load. The edge lets the foot drop below level, which is where the shin has to work hardest and where most people have never trained. Go as low as the ankle allows before pulling back up, and keep the knees straight throughout.

Cross-body carry

Start: Stand tall with one weight racked at a shoulder and another held at the opposite side, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and steady.

Focus: Resist the offset pull from both weights, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the ribs down. Brace hard, take steady steps, and stay tall.

Dead bug – arms and legs

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent over the hips, arms towards the ceiling, lower back pressed gently into the floor.

Movement: Lower the opposite arm and leg slowly towards the floor, keeping the back flat, then return and swap sides.

Focus: Keep the lower back pressed to the floor as the limbs extend, never letting it arch. Move slowly and controlled, and shorten the reach if the back lifts.

Goblet squat – pause at bottom

Start: Hold a weight at the chest with both hands, elbows tucked, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat, pause for two to three seconds at the bottom holding still, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down through the pause. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and pause only at a depth you control comfortably.

Step-ups – lateral, weighted

Start: Stand side-on to a step holding a weight in each hand, near foot ready to step onto it.

Focus: Keep the stepping knee over the foot, not caving in, and the hips level. Drive through the heel, control the lower, and keep the torso tall rather than leaning.

Lunge matrix

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, a weight held at the chest or hands free for balance.

Movement: Lunge in a set sequence, changing direction each rep (forward, reverse, lateral, angled), lowering under control and pushing back to standing between each.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, never caving in, and the torso tall. Push back to standing through the front heel, and control each direction rather than rushing the sequence.

For your game: Free hip rotation lets you turn fully back and through while keeping your posture.

Anterior tibialis – single leg toe raises

Start: Stand with your back against a wall, heels stepped a little forward, and take one foot off the floor. Keep a hand on something if you need it.

Movement: Lift the toes and forefoot of the standing leg up towards the shin as far as they will go, then lower under control.

Focus: One leg takes everything that two were sharing, so expect a clear difference side to side. Match the weaker side's reps on the stronger one rather than chasing a number. Step the standing foot further from the wall to make it harder.

Figure-4 stretch – seated
