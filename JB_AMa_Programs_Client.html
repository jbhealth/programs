How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Box squat – weighted
3 sets10-12 repsDB/BoxLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in front of a box at about knee height holding a light weight at the chest, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall through the heels, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the chest up. Sit back under control rather than dropping onto the box, and drive up through the heels.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

Chest press – machine, single arm
3 sets10-12 each sideChest press machineModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit in the chest press machine with your back and head against the pad, feet flat and a little wider than usual. Take one handle, level with the middle of your chest, and rest the other hand on your thigh.

Movement: Press the one handle forward until the arm is almost straight, then bring it back slowly until your hand is level with your chest. Finish the set, then change sides.

Focus: Keep both shoulders against the pad and your chest facing forward, not twisting towards the working arm. Brace your middle, and control the return.

For your game: Keeping your shoulders and arms in good condition helps you swing freely and without pain.

Hip abduction – cable, side step
3 sets10-12 repsCable machineModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a cuff or band low around one ankle attached to a side cable, feet together, torso tall.

Movement: Step the working leg out to the side against the resistance, then step it back under control.

Focus: Keep the torso tall and the hips level, stepping from the outer hip, not leaning. Keep the steps controlled, and the working knee soft.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

Lateral walks – band, diagonal
3 sets10-12 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and outward alternately, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees driving out, not caving. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips working each step.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

Anterior tibialis – machine
2 sets12-15 repsTibialis machineLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit at the tibialis machine, or on a bench with a tib bar, with your heels resting on the edge and the padded bar across the top of your feet. Let your toes point down to start.

Movement: Pull your toes up towards your shins as far as you can, pause, then lower them slowly back down.

Focus: Keep your knees still and move only at the ankles. Lift and lower slowly through the full range, and keep both feet working evenly.

For your game: Strong shins decelerate the foot and protect the knees over a long round.

Single leg deadlift – bodyweight
3 sets10-12 each sideBodyweightBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

For your game: Single-leg hip control steadies your base and balance through the swing.

Bear crawl – lateral
3 sets30sMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Start on the hands and knees, then lift the knees just off the floor, keeping the back flat.

Movement: Crawl sideways by moving the opposite hand and foot together, a few steps one way and then back the other way.

Focus: Keep the knees low and the hips level, as if balancing a cup of tea on your back. Small, slow steps work better than big ones.

For your game: A strong, steady trunk gives your arms and legs a stable base to swing from.

Prone hip internal rotation stretch
3 sets30sMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie face down, one knee bent to 90 degrees, the lower leg pointing up, hips flat.

Movement: Let the lower leg fall gently out to the side, rotating the hip inwards, to a comfortable stretch, then return.

Focus: Let the leg fall only to a comfortable stretch, keeping the hips flat on the floor. Move slowly, breathe, and ease off if the hip or knee complains.

For your game: Free hip rotation lets you turn fully back and through while keeping your posture.

Squat – trap bar

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Use the raised handles where the bar has them. Squat down with the chest tall and grip the handles in the middle.

Movement: Drive up through the heels to stand tall, then sit straight down between the handles, the torso staying upright. A squat with the load at your sides, so nothing sits on the back or shoulders.

Focus: Corkscrew the feet into the floor so the knees stay in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and keep the heels down. Sit down rather than back, and take depth only as far as the knees are comfortable.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

Romanian deadlift – staggered stance

Start: One foot flat, the other half a step back on its toes. Weight in front of the thighs, soft bend in the front knee.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front leg until the hamstring loads, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. Swap legs halfway.

Focus: Most of the weight sits on the front leg, the back foot is only there for balance. Back flat, hips square, not twisting open.

For your game: Hinging from the hips, not the back, is how you hold your posture and load into the ground in the swing.

For your game: Steady single-leg control keeps your hips level through the swing and your weight shift repeatable.

Deadlift – trap bar, high handles

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar with the raised handles facing up, feet hip-width. Hinge down only as far as the handles, grip them in the middle, chest up, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, then sit the hips back and lower the bar under control to the floor. The raised handles shorten the pull, so the start is higher and kinder on the back and hips.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the chest proud at the bottom, not rounding to reach the handles. Push through the heels and finish tall without leaning back.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.

For your game: Loosening the band across the lower back lets the hips turn without the spine being dragged into it.
