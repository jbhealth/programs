Program 1 — Rotational Power & Hip Control
WC 1st June 2026
ViPR rotations
2 × 12 each sideViPR

Start: Stand tall holding the ViPR at waist height with both hands.

Focus: Start slowly — the offset load can catch you off guard. Core braced, control each direction of rotation.

Movement: Rotate side to side in a controlled arc. The offset weight creates a unique challenge that a standard weight cannot replicate.

DB lateral lunge
2 × 10 each sideDumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the bent heel to return.

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Curtsey lunge to knee raise
2 × 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Control the landing before the next rep. Feel the glute med and hip flexors working.

Movement: Perform curtsey lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward as you return.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Bird-dog – hold and pulse
2 × 10 each sideMat

Start: Start on all fours with hands under shoulders and knees under hips. Spine neutral.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still throughout both the hold and the pulses. The pulses challenge stability endurance.

Movement: Extend the opposite arm and leg, hold for 5 seconds, then add 3 small pulses before returning.

Corkscrews
2 × 12Mat

Start: Lie on your back with both legs raised to 90° and arms flat on the floor at your sides.

Focus: Movement is driven by your core — not momentum. Keep your upper back and shoulders flat on the floor throughout.

Movement: Rotate your hips and legs in a large controlled circle in one direction, then reverse. Return legs to centre between each direction.

Program 2 — Carry & Core Stability
WC 1st June 2026
ViPR – log roll or carries
2 × 20 stepsViPR

Start: Hold the ViPR horizontally in both hands with a braced core and tall posture.

Focus: Keep your ribs down and spine tall throughout. Feel the offset weight challenging your core stability.

Movement: Carry the ViPR for prescribed distance or time. Vary the carry style — at waist height, on the shoulder, or overhead. Each position creates a different stability challenge.

Dumbbell squat
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

Focus: Keep chest tall and torso upright. Knees track over toes.

Movement: Squat to depth under control. Drive through heels to stand.

Single arm DB row – incline
2 × 12 each sideDumbbell

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Pivot lunges
2 × 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft bend in the knee.

Focus: Land softly on each step and control the pivot back. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Build confidence in each direction before moving to the next.

Movement: Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to the starting position. Repeat for all reps, then move to lateral — spring out to the side and pivot back. Finally rotational — jump and rotate in the air, planting your foot behind and diagonally. Complete all reps in each direction before moving to the next.

Lateral raises – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides with a slight bend in elbows.

Focus: Keep shoulders pressed down — don't shrug. Lead with elbows.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lower slowly under control.

Reverse lunge to knee raise
2 × 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Elbow plank – arm reach
2 × 8 each sideMat

Start: Set up in a forearm plank with core firmly braced.

Focus: Master earlier progressions first. Keep hips level and core braced throughout.

Movement: Slowly reach one arm forward, keeping it low. Hold 3 seconds, return, switch sides.

Bicycle twists
2 × 15 each sideMat

Start: Lie on your back with hands lightly behind your head and knees bent at 90°.

Focus: Rotation comes from your trunk — not from pulling your neck with your hands. Keep the movement controlled, not rushed.

Movement: Extend one leg while rotating your torso toward the bent knee, bringing the opposite elbow toward it. Alternate sides in a controlled cycling motion.

Program 3 — Functional Strength & Balance
WC 1st June 2026
DB thruster
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing inward.

Focus: Leg drive initiates the press — this should feel like one fluid squat-to-press movement.

Movement: Squat to below parallel, drive explosively to standing and press the dumbbells overhead as you rise. Lock out at the top.

Cossack squat
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Wood chop – med ball / DB
2 × 12 each sideDumbbell

Start: Hold a medicine ball or dumbbell with both hands overhead to one side.

Focus: Power comes from your hips and obliques — not just arms.

Movement: Rotate and chop explosively diagonally down toward opposite hip. Return under control.

Balance – single leg free
2 × 30 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Curtsey lunge – bodyweight
2 × 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep front knee pointing forward — don't let it collapse inward. Keep hips square.

Movement: Step back and across behind your standing leg into a lunge. Push through front heel to return.

Shoulder press – seated DB
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Sit tall with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Core braced, lower back neutral. Don't arch your lower back.

Movement: Press both dumbbells directly overhead to full arm extension. Lower to shoulder height under control.

Side plank – with rotation
2 × 10 each sideMat

Start: Set up in a full side plank — elbow under shoulder, feet stacked.

Focus: Rotation comes from your thoracic spine — not your lumbar. Keep your lower back stable throughout.

Movement: Reach your top arm underneath your body, rotating through your upper back. Return to the start position.

V-sit ups
2 × 12Mat

Start: Lie flat on your back with arms straight overhead and legs straight.

Focus: Raise your arms and legs simultaneously — this is a full body core exercise. Control the lowering.

Movement: Simultaneously raise both your arms and legs toward each other, meeting in a V shape at the top. Lower under control.
