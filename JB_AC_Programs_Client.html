Program 1 — Knee Stability & Functional Control
WC 1st June 2026
TRX Reverse Lunge
3 × 10 eachTRX

Start: Hold TRX handles for balance.

Focus: Use the TRX for balance, not power. Progress to freestanding when control is established.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge. Lower rear knee toward floor, return through front heel.

Romanian Deadlift – Single Leg
3 × 8 eachDB

Start: Stand tall holding weight in front of your thighs.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout — feel the stretch develop in your hamstrings.

Movement: Push hips back and lower weight down the front of your legs. Drive hips forward to return.

Balance – Single Leg with Arm Reach
3 × 5 reaches each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Band Pallof Press – Half Kneeling
3 × 10 each sideBand

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Copenhagen Plank – Short Lever
3 × 20–30 sec each sideBench

Start: Set up in side plank with lower shin resting on a bench.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

Movement: Other leg hovers below bench — unsupported. Hold position.

Waiter carry – single arm
3 × 20m each sideKB

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

Leg extension – bilateral
3 × 12Machine

Start: Sit in the leg extension machine, pad just above your ankles.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh at full extension. Use full range.

Movement: Extend both legs to full knee extension. Hold briefly, lower with control.

Terminal knee extension
3 × 15 each legBand

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.
