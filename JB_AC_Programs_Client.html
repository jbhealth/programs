Main Circuit
⌄
Romanian deadlift – BB
3 x 8BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

⌄
Goblet squat – pause at bottom
3 x 8KB/DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell in front of chest.

Movement: Squat down and pause 3 seconds at bottom. Drive through heels to stand.

Focus: At the bottom, keep your chest tall and lower back neutral. Hold the tension.

⌄
Single leg Romanian deadlift
3 x 8 eachDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a slight bend in that knee, holding a weight in front of your thigh, or just bodyweight to begin.

Movement: Hinge from the hip, lowering the weight down the front of your standing leg as your free leg reaches behind you. Drive the hip forward to return tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips level, don't let the lifted side open up. Feel the stretch build in the standing-leg hamstring.

⌄
Terminal knee extension
2 x to burn eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

⌄
Calf raises – single leg
3 x 12 eachStepBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg on the edge of a step, holding a rail or wall for balance.

Movement: Rise as high as possible onto the ball of the foot, then lower under control. Move to the loaded version once 3x15 is easy.

Focus: Lower the heel slowly below the step to a full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthen.

⌄
Copenhagen plank – short lever
2 x 20s eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up in side plank with lower shin resting on a bench.

Movement: Other leg hovers below bench, unsupported. Hold position.

Focus: Keep straight body line head to ankle. Feel adductors and lateral core engaged.

⌄
Nordic hamstring curls
2 x 6MatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Kneel on a mat with your feet anchored securely by a partner or machine.

Movement: Slowly lower your body toward the floor using your hamstrings as the brake. This is the most effective knee injury prevention exercise available.

Focus: Control is everything. Lower as slowly as you can, use your hands to catch yourself at the bottom if needed.

⌄
Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 x 30s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.
