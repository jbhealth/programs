How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Box squat – BB
3 sets6-8 repsBarbell/Box/Rack25kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set a box behind you at about parallel height. Bar on the upper back, feet shoulder-width or wider, toes out, braced hard.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box under control, then drive up through the heels to stand tall. Don't relax fully onto the box.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the shins fairly upright as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the back flat and core braced throughout.

Back extension – machine
3 sets10 repsBack extension machine/GHDModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set the pad at the top of your hips so you can bend freely. Cross your arms over your chest or place your hands by your temples, body in a straight line.

Movement: Hinge down at the hips with control, then raise back up until your body is straight. Move smoothly, no jerking.

Focus: Stop at a straight line, don't arch past it into the lower back. Lead the lift with the glutes and hamstrings, and keep the neck in line rather than craning up.

Good mornings – BB (pause)
3 sets8 repsBarbell/Rack15kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Bar across the upper back, feet hip-width, soft knees, core braced hard.

Movement: Hinge the hips back with a flat back until you feel the hamstrings, pause for two seconds at the bottom, then drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Hold the flat back through the pause, never let it round. Hinge at the hips, keep a soft knee, and only go as deep as your flat back and hamstring flexibility allow.

Pallof press – standing lunge
3 sets10 repsCable/Band2 x yellow band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height in a split stance, one foot forward in a shallow lunge, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to twist you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square over the split stance, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable. The lunge stance narrows your base, so the anti-rotation job gets harder. Keep the ribs down and press smoothly.

Focus: Keep the bent knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the chest up as you sink into the hip. Keep the straight leg's heel down, and go only as deep as your hips and knees comfortably allow.

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.
