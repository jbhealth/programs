Lunge matrix
2 x 10 eachDumbbell2 x 6kg

Start: Stand with good posture, feet hip-width apart.

Focus: Stay upright and controlled in each direction. Move slowly through full range.

Movement: Complete all forward lunges alternating legs, then all lateral lunges alternating legs, then all rotational lunges alternating legs.

ViPR – clean and press
2 x 15ViPR6kg

Start: Stand with the ViPR on the floor, ready to hinge.

Focus: Hip drive initiates the clean — not your arms. Focus on full hip extension at the top of the clean.

Movement: Clean the ViPR from the floor to your shoulder using explosive hip drive. Press overhead to full arm extension. Lower to shoulder, then hinge back to the floor.

Walking lunges
2 x burnBodyweightweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Maintain tall spine throughout. Keep a long stride — don't shuffle.

Movement: Take long step forward, lower into lunge, drive through front heel into next lunge.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell6kg

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Balance – single leg free
4 x 30s eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.

Deep squat with thoracic rotation
2 × 8 eachMatweight

Start: Hold a deep squat position.

Focus: Keep the squat stable while rotating. Follow your hand with your gaze.

Movement: Reach one arm to ceiling, rotating thoracic spine. Hold briefly, switch sides.

Glute bridges – single leg
10 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with foot flat, other leg extended.

Focus: Keep hips level — don't let one side drop.

Movement: Drive through the bent knee heel to push hips up. Hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

90/90 hip flow
2 × 8 eachMatweight

Start: Start in 90/90 sitting position (one leg in front, one to the side).

Focus: Move smoothly and fluidly. Use hands lightly for support only.

Movement: Transition smoothly from one 90/90 position to the other, rotating through hips.

Rotation & Control
ViPR – throw over shoulder
2 × 8 eachViPRweight

Start: Stand tall holding the ViPR at hip height with a stable base.

Focus: Power comes from your hip rotation — not just your arms. Build to full power progressively.

Movement: Rotate explosively and throw the ViPR over one shoulder. Catch and return. This is a high-power rotational exercise.

Pivot lunges
2 × 8 eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand tall on one leg with a soft bend in the knee.

Focus: Land softly on each step and control the pivot back. Keep your knee in line with your hip and ankle throughout. Build confidence in each direction before moving to the next.

Movement: Spring forward onto the opposite foot, pivot back to the starting position. Repeat for all reps, then move to lateral — spring out to the side and pivot back. Finally rotational — jump and rotate in the air, planting your foot behind and diagonally. Complete all reps in each direction before moving to the next.

Shin to opposite shoulder
2 × 8 eachDumbbellweight

Start: Stand tall holding a dumbbell or kettlebell at one shoulder.

Focus: This is a full multi-planar movement — control each phase and feel the full rotation through your spine.

Movement: Squat down and rotate to reach the weight outside the opposite ankle. Drive back up diagonally to the starting position, pressing weight back to shoulder.

Renegade row
2 × 8 eachDumbbellweight

Start: Start in a push-up plank position with a dumbbell in each hand directly under your shoulders.

Focus: Prevent your hips from rotating — that's the whole challenge. Row one side at a time while the rest of the body stays still.

Movement: Row one dumbbell to hip height, keeping your hips level. Place it back down, then row the other side.

Reverse lunge – bodyweight
2 × 10 eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

Step burpee
2 × 8Bodyweightweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Lower-impact version — step rather than jump throughout. No press-up required.

Movement: Step one foot back, then the other to a plank position. Hold briefly. Step feet forward one at a time. Stand.

Dead bug – arms and legs
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Supine spinal rotation
2 × 8 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with knees bent, feet flat on the floor.

Focus: Keep shoulders flat. Breathe steadily into your lower back.

Movement: Let both knees drop to one side, hold the stretch. Return and repeat to other side.

New Kit — Flow & Power
Barbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Barbellweight

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell in a double overhand grip just outside your legs. Stand tall with the bar at hip height.

Focus: This is a hip hinge — keep your knees soft but nearly straight. Feel the hamstring stretch as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body throughout.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch. Drive your hips forward to return to standing, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Goblet squat
2 × 12Kettlebellweight

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

DB chest press
2 × 10Dumbbellweight

Start: Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Use the full range of motion. Control the lowering phase — don't let the dumbbells drop.

Movement: Press both dumbbells upward to full arm extension, allowing them to naturally travel slightly inward at the top. Lower under control.

Med ball rotational throw
2 × 10 eachMedicine ballweight

Start: Stand sideways to a wall holding a medicine ball at hip height.

Focus: Power comes from your hip rotation — not just your arms. Load your hips by rotating away first.

Movement: Rotate away to load, then explosively rotate and throw the ball against the wall. Catch the rebound and repeat.

Lunge and rotate
2 × 10 eachDumbbellweight

Start: Stand tall ready to step into a lunge.

Focus: Rotation comes from your thoracic spine, not arm swinging. Control both movements.

Movement: Step into lunge, at bottom rotate torso toward front leg while holding weight. Return to centre, push back.

KB swings
2 × 15Kettlebellweight

Start: Stand with feet slightly wider than hip-width, kettlebell on the floor just ahead of you.

Focus: Power comes entirely from your hip snap — not your arms. Think explosive hip extension, not shoulder raise.

Movement: Hinge at the hips and hike the kettlebell back between your legs. Explosively snap your hips forward to drive the kettlebell up to shoulder height. Let it fall back and repeat.

Bent-over rows – BB
2 × 10Barbellweight

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge forward to roughly 45° with a flat back. Grip the barbell with an overhand grip.

Focus: Keep your core braced throughout — don't let your lower back round. Squeeze your shoulder blades at the top.

Movement: Pull the barbell toward your lower ribs, driving your elbows back. Lower under control.

Lunge pass – med ball
2 × 10 eachMedicine ballweight

Start: Stand tall holding a medicine ball at chest height.

Focus: Control the rotation during the pass — don't rush it. The pass under your leg is a stability challenge as much as a strength one.

Movement: Step into a lunge and at the bottom pass the ball from hand to hand under your front leg. Stand, return, repeat on the same or alternating leg.
