Main Circuit
⌄
Deadlift – BB conventional
3 x 6BarbellHeavy
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand over the bar with feet hip-width apart and shins close. Grip the bar just outside your legs.

Movement: Brace, grip, push the floor away to stand. Lock out fully at the top.

Focus: Keep your hips and bar rising together, the biggest fault is the hips shooting up while the bar stays low.

⌄
Box squat – goblet
3 x 10KB/DB/BoxModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands, with a box or bench set behind you.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower under control until you lightly touch the box, then drive up through your heels to stand tall.

Focus: Sit back to the box rather than dropping straight down. Stay tall through the chest and keep your tension, don't collapse onto the box.

⌄
Landmine press – single arm
3 x 8 eachBarbell/LandmineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold the end of the barbell in a landmine at shoulder height with one hand.

Movement: Press forward and upward in a diagonal arc to full arm extension. Return under control.

Focus: Resist the rotation created by the single arm throughout. Drive your elbow forward as you press.

⌄
Barbell hip row
3 x 10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Hinge at the hips and pull the bar toward your hip crease. Squeeze at the bottom, return to standing.

Focus: This is a hip hinge movement. Feel your lower lats engaging as you pull.

⌄
Reverse lunge to knee raise
3 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 eachDumbbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
Landmine rotation
3 x 10 eachBarbell/LandmineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the end of the landmine at chest height with both hands.

Movement: Rotate side to side in a controlled arc, letting the bar travel in its natural path. An effective anti-rotation core exercise.

Focus: Resist the rotational pull as the bar swings. Keep your core braced and hips facing forward.

Cardio Finisher
Mountain climbers / Climbers
3 rounds of 30 seconds, steady rhythm, hips low.

Main Circuit
⌄
Hip thrust – KB
3 x 10KB/BenchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench and rest a kettlebell across your hips, feet flat on the floor.

Movement: Drive through both feet to full hip extension until the body is level shoulder to knee, then lower under control.

Focus: Squeeze the glutes hard at the top. Keep the ribs down and the lower back neutral, no arching.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat
3 x 8 eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

⌄
DB incline press
3 x 10DB/Incline benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set a bench to 30–45° incline. Sit with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Movement: Press dumbbells upward and slightly inward to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Focus: Upper chest and anterior delt focus. Use the full range of motion and control the lowering.

⌄
Single arm DB row – incline
3 x 10 eachDB/Incline benchModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement, this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

⌄
Cross-body carry
3 x 20mDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at your side.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance, resisting the pull of the load.

Focus: The single arm load creates both lateral and rotational forces. Resist all compensations, stay tall.

⌄
Balance – single leg with arm reach
3 x 8 eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

⌄
Corkscrews
3 x 8 eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with both legs raised to 90° and arms flat on the floor at your sides.

Movement: Rotate your hips and legs in a large controlled circle in one direction, then reverse. Return legs to centre between each direction.

Focus: Movement is driven by your core, not momentum. Keep your upper back and shoulders flat on the floor throughout.

Main Circuit
⌄
Romanian deadlift – KB
3 x 10KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell in both hands in front of your thighs, feet hip-width, with soft knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the kettlebell down the front of your legs to mid-shin, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. The bell sits a touch closer to the body than a barbell.

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch building before you drive the hips forward to return.

⌄
Goblet squat – pulse
3 x 10KB/DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell at chest height, feet shoulder-width apart.

Movement: Squat to the bottom, pause briefly, then add 3 small pulses before driving back to standing.

Focus: The pulses challenge stability and strength at end range. Keep your posture tall and knees tracking out.

⌄
Arnold press
3 x 10DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing you.

Movement: Press upward while rotating palms away so they face forward at top. Lower, reversing rotation.

Focus: Slow rotation as you press engages all three heads of deltoid throughout.

⌄
Bent-over row – BB overhand
3 x 10BarbellModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge forward to roughly 45° with a flat back and overhand grip on the barbell.

Movement: Pull the bar toward your lower ribs. Squeeze at the top. Return under control.

Focus: Drive your elbows back hard and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top.

⌄
Waiter carry – bilateral
3 x 20mDB/KBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in each hand, both arms locked straight.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining both arms overhead in the waiter position.

Focus: Keep both arms locked straight overhead. Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout.

⌄
90/90 hip flow
2 x 6 each wayMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start in 90/90 sitting position (one leg in front, one to the side).

Movement: Transition smoothly from one 90/90 position to the other, rotating through hips.

Focus: Move smoothly and fluidly. Use hands lightly for support only.

⌄
Side plank – with hip dip
3 x 8 eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up in a full stacked side plank.

Movement: Slowly lower hip toward floor without touching it. Raise back to start position.

Focus: Control the movement, no collapsing at bottom. Brace your obliques.

Cardio Finisher
Squat thrust
3 rounds of 30 seconds, land soft, keep the spine long.
