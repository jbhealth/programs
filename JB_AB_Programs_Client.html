Hip Hinge & Core Control

Main Circuit

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 x 10DB2 x 6kg

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

⌄
KB goblet lateral lunge
10 each sideKB4kg

Start: Stand tall holding a kettlebell at chest height in goblet position.

Focus: The goblet position adds an anterior load that increases core demand throughout. Keep your chest tall.

Movement: Step wide to the side into a lateral lunge, bending that knee while the other stays straight. Drive through the bent heel to return.

⌄
Single arm row – DB
2 x 10 eachDB/Bench8kg

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control — don't let weight drop.

⌄
Lateral raises – slow eccentric
2 x 6-8 eachDB4kg

Start: Stand tall with dumbbells at your sides, shoulders pressed down away from ears.

Focus: The slow eccentric lowering is the therapeutic part — particularly good for supraspinatus health. Keep shoulders down throughout.

Movement: Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height at normal speed. Lower over 4 seconds under full control. Repeat.

⌄
Single leg deadlift – KB
10 eachKB12kg

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell in the opposite hand.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the kettlebell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance. Drive through your heel to return.

⌄
Glute bridges – march
1 set to burnMatBodyweight

Start: Hold a glute bridge at the top with hips fully extended.

Focus: Keep hips absolutely still — no rocking or dropping during the march.

Movement: Alternately lift each foot slightly off the floor in a marching motion. Maintain hip stability.

Mobility, Rotation & Balance
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Deadlift – KB
3 x 10KB16kg

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

⌄
Cossack squat
3 x 8 each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

⌄
Knee drive – med ball
3 x 10 each sideMedicine ball4kg

Start: Stand tall holding a medicine ball at shoulder height on one side.

Focus: Push through the ball of your standing foot to generate the drive. Feel the coordination between your core and lower body.

Movement: Drive the opposite knee powerfully upward while pressing the ball downward to meet it. Lower with control and alternate knees.

⌄
Wood chop – med ball / DB
3 x 10 each sideMedicine ball/Dumbbell4kg

Start: Hold a medicine ball or dumbbell with both hands overhead to one side.

Focus: Power comes from your hips and obliques — not just arms.

Movement: Rotate and chop explosively diagonally down toward opposite hip. Return under control.

⌄
DB lateral lunge
3 x 8 each sideDB2 x 6kg

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the bent heel to return.

⌄
Waiter carry – single arm
2 x to burnDB/KB6kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

⌄
Bird-dog – weighted arm
3 x 8 each sideDBBodyweight

Start: Set up in bird-dog position holding a light dumbbell in extending arm.

Focus: Added weight increases anti-rotation challenge. Slow, controlled movement.

Movement: Extend arm forward and opposite leg behind simultaneously. No momentum.

Full-Body Strength & Stability
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Hip thrust – BB
3 x 10Barbell/Bench6kg

Start: Sit with your upper back against a bench, barbell across your hips and feet flat on the floor.

Focus: The peak glute exercise. Drive to full hip extension at the top and squeeze your glutes hard. Keep your hips level — no side drop.

Movement: Drive through your heels to lift your hips to full extension. Hold briefly, lower under control.

⌄
Dumbbell squat
2 x 10DBModerate

Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

Focus: Keep chest tall and torso upright. Knees track over toes.

Movement: Squat to depth under control. Drive through heels to stand.

⌄
Barbell pull-over
2 x 10Barbell/Bench4kg

Start: Lie on a bench with your upper back supported and feet flat on the floor. Hold the barbell above your chest with a shoulder-width grip.

Focus: Control the lowering phase as you lower the bar behind your head — feel the lat stretch.

Movement: Lower the bar behind your head in a controlled arc until you feel a deep stretch. Pull back over your chest in the same arc.

⌄
Arnold press
3 x 10DBModerate

Start: Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing you.

Focus: Slow rotation as you press engages all three heads of deltoid throughout.

Movement: Press upward while rotating palms away so they face forward at top. Lower, reversing rotation.

⌄
Side bends – KB
3 x 12 each sideKBModerate

Start: Stand tall with a kettlebell in one hand at your side.

Focus: Move slowly with intent — not momentum. Keep body in same plane throughout.

Movement: Bend directly to the weighted side and return. Stand tall at top of each rep.

⌄
Hollow body hold
3 x 30sMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with arms extended overhead and legs straight out.

Focus: Press your entire lower back firmly into the floor. Feel like a hollow banana shape.

Movement: Hold position with core tension. If lower back lifts off, raise your legs higher.
