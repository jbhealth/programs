Control & Strength
Week commencing: 8 June 2026
Main Circuit
Box squats – high box
2 × 10Box–

Start: Stand in front of a high box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees in line with hips and ankles throughout. Use heel drive to stand.

Movement: Sit back onto the box with control, then stand fully using your heels.

Single arm row – cable
2 × 10 eachCable machine–

Start: Stand facing the cable machine, feet shoulder-width apart, slight bend in the knees.

Focus: Drive elbow back and squeeze shoulder blade at the end. Control the return.

Movement: Pull cable handle toward your lower ribs. Resist the cable pulling your shoulder forward as you return.

Balance – single leg assisted
2 × 20s eachWall–

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

Calf raises – bilateral
2 × 12Step–

Start: Stand tall, feet flat on the floor or edge of a step.

Focus: Go through the full range — let heels dip below the step on the way down.

Movement: Rise up onto the balls of your feet as high as possible. Lower slowly through full range.

Glute bridges – bilateral
2 × 12None–

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Brace your core throughout.

Movement: Drive your heels into the floor and push hips toward the ceiling, squeeze glutes, hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

Bird-dog
2 × 8 eachNone–

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

Mobility & Movement
Week commencing: 8 June 2026
Main Circuit
Step-ups – forward low
2 × 8 eachStep box–

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

Straight arm raise – plate
2 × 10Plate–

Start: Hold a plate at arm's length in front of you, thumbs up.

Focus: Keep arms slightly forward of body at 30° — this targets the supraspinatus.

Movement: Raise both arms to shoulder height keeping them straight, then continue all the way overhead to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Lateral band walks
2 × 10 each wayBand–

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Band hip hinge
2 × 10Band–

Start: Stand on a resistance band, loop it around your hips.

Focus: Feel the band resist your hips going back. Squeeze glutes as you stand.

Movement: Hinge forward, drive hips forward to return. Squeeze glutes at top.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCable–

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Dead bug – arms only
2 × 8 eachNone–

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time — this is non-negotiable.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead toward the floor, return, then alternate sides. Never let your lower back arch.
