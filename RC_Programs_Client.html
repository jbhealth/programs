Program 1 — Rotation, Power & Hamstrings
WC 1st June 2026
Single-leg deadlift – DB
2 × 8 eachDumbbell

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand pressed overhead, arm fully extended. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep hips square throughout — the overhead load increases the anti-rotation challenge significantly. Control the movement slowly.

Movement: Hinge at the hip, pushing the free leg behind as counterbalance while maintaining the dumbbell overhead. Lower until your torso is near parallel. Drive the standing hip forward to return.

Cable hip abduction
2 × 12 eachCable

Start: Attach ankle strap to cable machine at lowest setting. Stand side-on to machine.

Focus: Keep your torso upright. Resist leaning away from the cable.

Movement: Step away from machine and lift attached leg out to side against cable.

Med ball rotational throw
2 × 10 eachMedicine Ball

Start: Stand sideways to a wall holding a medicine ball at hip height.

Focus: Power comes from your hip rotation — not just your arms. Load your hips by rotating away first.

Movement: Rotate away to load, then explosively rotate and throw the ball against the wall. Catch the rebound and repeat.

Band monster walk – diagonal
2 × 12 eachResistance Band

Start: Set up as for a band monster walk with band above knees.

Focus: Step at 45° angles, alternating directions. Feel your hip stabilisers working in multiple planes.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and to the side, alternating directions.

Reverse lunge to knee raise
2 × 10 eachBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Control the lunge before driving the knee. Keep your torso tall throughout.

Movement: Perform reverse lunge, then drive rear knee powerfully upward in front. Balance and hip flexor challenge.

Seated cable row
2 × 10Cable

Start: Sit at a cable row station with a close neutral grip attachment

Focus: Drive elbows back, squeeze shoulder blades at the end. Don't lean back to cheat the weight.

Movement: Pull the handle to your lower abdomen, driving elbows back. Return to full arm extension under control.

Nordic hamstring curl
2 × 6Bodyweight

Start: Kneel on a mat with your feet anchored securely by a partner or machine.

Focus: Control is everything. Lower as slowly as you can — use your hands to catch yourself at the bottom if needed.

Movement: Slowly lower your body toward the floor using your hamstrings as the brake. This is the most effective knee injury prevention exercise available.

Hollow body hold
2 × 30 secMat

Start: Lie on your back with arms extended overhead and legs straight out.

Focus: Press your entire lower back firmly into the floor. Feel like a hollow banana shape.

Movement: Hold position with core tension. If lower back lifts off, raise your legs higher.

ISO Copenhagen plank
2 × 20 sec eachMat

Start: Lie on your side with your top foot or shin resting on a bench. Set up a side plank with your bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Build time progressively — start with shin on bench, progress to ankle. Keep your body in a straight line throughout.

Movement: Hold the position for the prescribed time with no hip drop. This isometric exercise loads both the adductors and lateral core.

Ski Erg — Cardio Finisher
3 × 30 sec on / 30 sec offSki Erg

Focus: Full body conditioning. Drive from the hips, not just the arms. Keep consistent pace — don't sprint the first interval.

Movement: Stand on the footplates, grip the handles overhead. Pull down and back explosively, hinging at the hips. Return arms overhead with control and repeat.

Program 2 — Posterior Chain & Knee Stability
WC 1st June 2026
KB deadlift
2 × 10Kettlebell

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 × 10 eachBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Band pallof press – half kneeling
2 × 10 eachResistance Band

Start: Kneel on one knee perpendicular to a band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotational pull of the band. Keep your hips square and torso facing forward.

Movement: Hold band at chest and press straight out. Return to chest.

Single arm DB row – incline
2 × 10 each · slow eccentricDumbbell

Start: Lie face down on an incline bench with one arm hanging freely. Hold a dumbbell in the hanging hand.

Focus: The incline removes all lower back involvement — this is a pure back exercise. Squeeze your shoulder blade at the top.

Movement: Row the dumbbell toward your hip by driving your elbow back. Lower under control.

Step-ups – lateral low
2 × 10 eachStep

Start: Stand to the side of a low step box.

Focus: Keep heel fully in contact with the step — don't let it hang off.

Movement: Step nearest foot up and sideways. Drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower under control.

Lateral raises – slow eccentric
2 × 12Dumbbell

Start: Stand tall with dumbbells at your sides, shoulders pressed down away from ears.

Focus: The slow eccentric lowering is the therapeutic part — particularly good for supraspinatus health. Keep shoulders down throughout.

Movement: Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height at normal speed. Lower over 4 seconds under full control. Repeat.

Back extension machine – bilateral
2 × 10Machine

Start: Set up on the back extension machine with hips on the pad.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Keep your lower back neutral throughout.

Movement: Lower your torso, extend back to neutral.

Bicycle twists
2 × 15 eachMat

Start: Lie on your back with hands lightly behind your head and knees bent at 90°.

Focus: Rotation comes from your trunk — not from pulling your neck with your hands. Keep the movement controlled, not rushed.

Movement: Extend one leg while rotating your torso toward the bent knee, bringing the opposite elbow toward it. Alternate sides in a controlled cycling motion.

Side plank – feet stacked
2 × 30 sec eachMat

Start: Lie on your side with feet stacked, elbow under shoulder.

Focus: Keep hips pressing forward. Don't let them drop or rotate back.

Movement: Lift hips to create a straight line from head to heels. Hold for prescribed time.

Ski Erg — Cardio Finisher
3 × 30 sec on / 30 sec offSki Erg

Focus: Full body conditioning. Drive from the hips, not just the arms. Keep consistent pace — don't sprint the first interval.

Movement: Stand on the footplates, grip the handles overhead. Pull down and back explosively, hinging at the hips. Return arms overhead with control and repeat.
