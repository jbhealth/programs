Back squat – high bar
3 x 10Barbell25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Rest the bar high on the traps, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced hard, standing tall after unracking.

Movement: Sit down with a fairly upright torso, knees tracking forward over the toes, to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees over the toes and the heels down, chest tall throughout. Keep the core braced to protect the back, and control the depth to your comfortable range.

⌄
Deadlift – BB
3 x 6Barbell35kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with feet hip-width, the bar over mid-foot. Hinge down to grip just outside your legs, shins to the bar, chest up, back flat, shoulders slightly ahead of the bar, core braced hard.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, keeping the bar dragging close to your legs, hips and shoulders rising together. Finish standing tall with the glutes, then hinge the hips back and lower the bar under control down the same line.

Focus: The back stays flat from floor to lockout, never rounded. Take the load through the legs and hips and keep the bar close to the body throughout. Don't jerk the bar or lean back at the top, just stand tall.

DB incline press
3 x 8-10DB/Incline bench2 x 6kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on an incline bench, a dumbbell in each hand at upper-chest height, shoulder blades set back, feet planted.

Movement: Press the dumbbells up until the arms are nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set on the bench and the elbows at about 45 degrees, not flared wide. Keep the ribs down, and control the lower to a comfortable shoulder range.

⌄
Barbell hip row
3 x 8-10Barbell20kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back holding a barbell at arms' length, chest up, core braced, hips back.

Movement: Pull the bar towards the hips, driving the elbows back, then lower under control while holding the hinge.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hinge steady throughout, not rising or rounding as you row. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and keep the shoulders down.

⌄
Bulgarian split squat – BB
3 x 8 eachBarbell/Bench10kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Barbell across the upper back, stand a stride in front of a bench with the back foot resting on it, torso tall, core braced.

Movement: Lower by bending the front knee until the thigh is towards parallel, then drive up through the front heel to the start.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the weight through the front leg with the core braced. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the knee stays comfortable.

Banded hip thrust – BB
2 x burnBarbell/Bench/Band10kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit with the upper back against a bench, a barbell across the hips, a band around the thighs, feet planted.

Movement: Drive the hips up until the body is level from knees to shoulders, pushing the knees out against the band, then lower under control.

Focus: Drive through the heels and squeeze the glutes at the top, ribs down, not arching the lower back. Push the knees out against the band, and control the lower.

⌄
Copenhagen plank – short lever
3 x 20s eachBenchBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your side with the top leg's inner knee resting on a bench, forearm down, elbow under the shoulder.

Movement: Lift the hips and the bottom leg so the body forms a straight line, supported by the knee on the bench, and hold.

Focus: Keep the hips lifted and level, not sagging, and the body in one line. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow, and hold only as long as the line holds.

Band pallof press – half kneeling
3 x 8 eachBandRed band
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Half kneel side-on to a band at chest height, inside knee down, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the band straight out, resisting the rotational pull, hold briefly, then return to the chest.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square and the torso tall, resisting any twist towards the band. Keep the core braced and the back neutral, not arching, and press with control.
