Main Circuit
Box squat – BB
4 sets6 repsBarbell/Box/RackHeavy
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set a box behind you at about parallel height. Bar on the upper back, feet shoulder-width or wider, toes out, braced hard.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box under control, then drive up through the heels to stand tall. Don't relax fully onto the box.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the shins fairly upright as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the back flat and core braced throughout.

Hip thrust – BB
3 sets10 repsBarbell/BenchModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench, a padded barbell across the front of your hips. Feet flat, hip-width, shins vertical when the hips are up. Tuck the chin slightly and brace the core.

Movement: Drive through your heels and push the hips up until the thighs are level and the body makes a straight line from knees to shoulders. Squeeze the glutes hard at the top, hold, then lower with control.

Focus: Finish the lift with the glutes at a straight line, don't over-arch the lower back to gain height. Keep the ribs down and the chin tucked so the movement stays in the hips, not the back. Shins stay roughly vertical at the top.

Bent-over row – BB underhand
3 sets10 repsBarbellModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hinge forward with a flat back holding a barbell underhand at arms' length, chest up, hips back, core braced.

Movement: Pull the bar towards the lower abdomen, driving the elbows back close to the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hinge steady, not rounding as you row. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades, and keep the wrists straight.

Step-ups – lateral, weighted
3 sets8 eachAny kit/BoxModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a higher step holding a dumbbell in each hand, near foot ready to step up.

Movement: Place the near foot on the step, drive up through that heel to stand tall, then lower back down with control. Complete the reps, then swap sides.

Focus: Keep the stepping knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the hips level. Drive through the heel and control the lower, not pushing off the trailing foot.

Balance board – single leg
3 sets30s eachBalance boardBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on a balance board on one foot, knee soft, torso tall, near a support.

Movement: Keep the board level and steady on one leg, making constant small adjustments, for the set time.

Focus: Keep the standing knee soft and the hips level, adjusting from the ankle. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and keep support within reach.

Calf raises – loaded single leg
3 sets10 eachKB/StepLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on a step edge, a weight in the hand, holding a support with the other.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Renegade row
3 sets8 eachDB/MatModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up in a push-up position gripping two dumbbells, feet wide for stability, body in a straight line, core braced hard.

Movement: Row one dumbbell to the hip while balancing on the other, lower it, then row the other side.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square, resisting the twist as you row, which is the whole point. Keep the body in a straight line, brace hard, and control each row.

Back squat – tempo

Start: Bar on the upper back, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, braced hard, standing tall after unracking.

Movement: Lower slowly over three to four seconds to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand. Keep tension the whole way down.

Focus: Hold the knees tracking over the toes and the chest up through the slow descent, where control matters most. Keep the heels planted and the back flat, and don't let the knees cave as you fatigue.

Deadlift – BB conventional

Start: Feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot. Bend at the hips and knees to grip just outside the legs, shins near the bar, chest up, flat back, shoulders a touch ahead of the bar, brace the core.

Movement: Drive through the legs and push the floor away, keeping the bar close as the hips and chest rise together, to stand tall. Hinge the hips back and control the bar down the same path.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout and the bar close to the legs. Power comes from the legs and hips, not the lower back. Don't round at the bottom or hyperextend at the top.

Goblet squat – pause at bottom

Start: Hold a weight at the chest with both hands, elbows tucked, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat, pause for two to three seconds at the bottom holding still, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down through the pause. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and pause only at a depth you control comfortably.

Romanian deadlift – BB

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and most of the weight through the front leg. Keep the torso tall, control the descent, and go only as deep as the front knee stays comfortable.
