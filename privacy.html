Privacy Notice

JB Health · Last updated: 2 June 2026

This notice explains what personal information JB Health collects when you complete the intake and screening form, why it is collected, how it is stored, and your rights over it. JB Health is the data controller for this information.

Who we are

  • Controller: JB Health / Baker Enterprises (James Baker)
  • Contact: james@jbhealth.co.uk
  • ICO registration: ZC154832

What we collect

  • Identity & contact: your name, email, phone, and an emergency contact.
  • Training information: your goals, experience, availability, equipment, and (for online clients) your setup.
  • Health information: injury history, current pain, screening (PAR-Q) answers, medication relevant to exercise, and lifestyle details. This is special-category data under UK GDPR and is treated with extra care.

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

  • Your health information is collected on the basis of your explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), given by the tickbox at the end of the intake form, solely to design and adjust your training safely.
  • Your identity, contact and training information is processed to provide the coaching service you have asked for (Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).
  • We do not use your information for marketing or share it for advertising.

How it is stored and kept separate

  • Your name is stored separately from your health information. Your programs are keyed to an internal code and initials only — your full name does not appear in any program file.
  • Your email, phone and emergency contact are kept only in the trainer's own records, not in your program files.
  • Form responses are collected and stored through Tally, which acts as a data processor on our behalf.

Who can see it

Only your trainer. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties, except service providers that help run the service (such as Tally for forms and GitHub for hosting your program pages), or where we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are a client, and afterwards only for as long as our insurer and professional body require us to keep training and screening records. We may ask you to re-confirm your screening and consent each year and after any significant change to your health.

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information, or withdraw your consent to us holding your health data, at any time (this may mean we can no longer program for you).
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how we have handled your data.

To exercise any of these, contact james@jbhealth.co.uk.
