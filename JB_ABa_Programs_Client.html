Golf Foundations: Hips & Core
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
How to run it
Two rounds of the circuit. Move through in order, slow and controlled, full range. Quality over speed, rest whenever you need to.
⌄
Glute bridges – bilateral
2 x 12BandBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Brace your core throughout.

Movement: Drive your heels into the floor and push hips toward the ceiling, squeeze glutes, hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

For your game: Strong glutes give you the hip drive that powers your swing through the ball.

⌄
Hip abduction – clamshell
2 x 12 each sideBandGreen band

Start: Lie on your side, hips stacked, knees bent to about 45°, feet together.

Focus: Don't let hips roll backward — movement is only in the hip joint.

Movement: Open your top knee upward like a clamshell. Pause, close. Repeat. Add a band above knees for more resistance.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

⌄
Goblet squat – pause at bottom
2 x 8KB/DBBodyweight

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell in front of chest.

Focus: At the bottom, keep your chest tall and lower back neutral. Hold the tension.

Movement: Squat down and pause 3 seconds at bottom. Drive through heels to stand.

For your game: Loading into your legs and holding steady is the control you want at the top of your backswing.

⌄
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 x 10DBBodyweight

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

For your game: Hinging from the hips, not the back, is exactly how you hold your posture over the ball.

⌄
Bird-dog
2 x 8 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

For your game: Keeping your spine still while an arm and leg move is the anti-rotation control that protects your back in a full swing.

⌄
Pallof press
2 x 10 each sideCable/BandYellow band

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

For your game: Resisting the pull trains your core to control rotation, so your swing stays repeatable.

⌄
Side plank – knees bent
2 x 20s each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Keep your hips straight — don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

For your game: Side-core strength steadies your trunk and keeps you balanced over the ball.

Cardio Finisher
5-minute run
Easy, flat pace, nice and relaxed. A gentle way to finish. If your knees or ankles feel sore, ease right off or stop.
