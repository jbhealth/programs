How to run it
One round: do each exercise once, in order, and you're done. Rest as you need between them.
Main Circuit
Squat – band
3 sets10 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on a band, feet shoulder-width, holding the band at the shoulders or looped over them, chest up, core braced.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat against the band's tension, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down. Let the band add resistance without pulling you forward, and squat only as deep as your knees stay comfortable.

Romanian deadlift
3 sets10 repsAny kit~5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, holding a weight in front of your thighs, or hands empty, with a soft bend in the knees. Set the shoulders back and brace the core.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front of your legs, keeping it close, until you feel a stretch in the hamstrings. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, never let it round. The movement is a hip hinge, not a squat, and the weight stays close to your legs. Only go as low as you can keep the back flat.

Row – band
3 sets12 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Anchor a band at chest height and face it, an end in each hand, arms straight. Step back until there's tension, knees soft.

Movement: Pull the hands towards the lower ribs, driving the elbows back, then let the arms straighten slowly against the band.

Focus: Stand tall, ribs down, so the lower back doesn't arch to finish the pull. Squeeze the shoulder blades together, and don't let the band snap the arms forward.

Pallof press
3 sets8 each sideCable machine/BandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

Push-up – wall or Smith machine
3 sets10 repsWall/Smith machine/Bench/ChairBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand facing a wall, or set a Smith machine bar at a height you can control and lock it off. Put your hands on the wall or bar at shoulder height and width, arms straight, body in a line leaning in.

Movement: Bend the elbows to bring the chest towards the wall or bar, pause just short of touching, then press back to straight arms, keeping the body in a line.

Focus: Keep the body in a straight line, not sagging at the hips. Keep the elbows tucked and the shoulders down, and control the movement. The more upright you stand, or the higher the bar, the easier it is. Step the feet back, or lower the bar, to make it harder.

Calf raises – bilateral
3 sets12 repsStepBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, balls of the feet on the floor or the edge of a step, holding a support for balance.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heels slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankles tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Box squat – goblet

Start: Stand in front of a box at about parallel height holding a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heels to stand tall, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Touch the box under control rather than dropping, and keep the chest up and the weight close throughout.

ISO split squat – bodyweight

Start: Stand in a split stance, one foot forward and one back, weight balanced, torso tall, hands at the chest.

Movement: Lower straight down until both knees are bent towards right angles and hold that bottom position still for the set time.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and the torso upright through the hold. Keep the weight balanced between both legs, and hold only at a depth the knees tolerate.

Single leg deadlift – bodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, the other leg ready to travel back, standing knee soft, arms ready.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back and the arms lower, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, keeping the hips level and not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Pull-apart – band

Start: Stand tall holding a band in front at shoulder height with straight arms, elbows softly bent, ribs down.

Movement: Pull the band apart out to the sides, squeezing the shoulder blades, then return under control.

Focus: Squeeze the shoulder blades together and keep the shoulders down, not shrugging. Keep a soft elbow bend and the ribs down, and control the return.

Glute bridges – single leg

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with the foot flat, the other leg extended straight out and held off the floor.

Movement: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to push your hips up until the body is level. Hold two seconds, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Push with the glute, not the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

3 x 10 each · Bodyweight
Dead bug – slow with band

Start: Lie on your back holding a band anchored behind your head, knees over the hips, back pressed into the floor.

Movement: Lower the opposite arm and leg very slowly against the band's pull, keeping the back flat, then return and swap.

Focus: Keep the lower back pressed to the floor the whole time, resisting the band trying to pull you into an arch. Move slowly, and shorten the range if the back lifts.

Goblet squat and reach

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat and, at the bottom, reach the weight forward to help you sit upright, then draw it back to the chest and stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Use the reach to stay upright rather than tipping forward, and squat only as deep as the knees allow.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

Romanian deadlift

Start: Stand tall holding a kettlebell in front of the thighs with both hands, feet hip-width, soft knees, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the bell down the front of the legs, keeping it close, until the hamstrings stretch, then drive the hips forward to stand.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the bell close throughout. Hinge at the hips, not the waist, and only lower as far as the flat back allows.

For your game: Hinging from the hips, not the back, is how you hold your posture over the ball.

Single arm row

Start: Hand and knee on a bench, the other foot planted, a dumbbell in the free hand hanging down, back flat and level.

Movement: Pull the dumbbell towards the hip, driving the elbow back and up, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the back flat and level, not twisting to lift. Keep the shoulder down, and control the lower.

For your game: A strong back is resilient to the forces the swing puts through it, and keeps you comfortable getting round the course.

Drop landing – bilateral

Start: Stand on a low box or step, feet hip-width, ready to step off, core braced.

Movement: Step off and land softly on both feet in a quarter-squat, absorbing through the hips and knees, then stand.

Focus: Land softly and quietly, absorbing through the hips and knees, with the knees tracking over the toes, not caving in. Aim for a stable, controlled catch rather than a stiff or collapsing landing.

For your game: Steady balance like this keeps you sure-footed on slopes and uneven lies out on the course.

Balance – single leg with arm reach

Start: Stand on one leg, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to reach.

Movement: Reach the arms (and torso if set) out to the sides or forward, challenging balance, then return to centre.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft as you reach, moving slowly. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and return under control.

For your game: Steady balance like this keeps you sure-footed on slopes and uneven lies out on the course.

Wood chop

Start: Stand tall holding a med ball or dumbbell in both hands, feet hip-width, knees soft, core braced.

Movement: Sweep the weight down and across from above one shoulder to the opposite hip (or the reverse), rotating through the trunk, then return.

Focus: Turn through the trunk with the hips pivoting and knees soft, not twisting the lower back. Keep the ribs down, control the sweep, and don't fling the weight.

For your game: Rotational power like this is exactly what you transfer into clubhead speed through the ball.

Deep squat with thoracic rotation

Start: Sit into a deep squat, feet shoulder-width, heels down, hands together in front of the chest.

Movement: From the bottom of the squat, reach one arm up towards the ceiling, rotating the upper back to follow the hand, then return and repeat the other side.

Focus: Let the rotation come from the upper back, not the lower spine, and keep the hips settled and heels down. Hold support with the free hand if balance is a challenge.

For your game: Free upper-back rotation lets you make a fuller shoulder turn without straining your lower back.

Glute bridges – march

Start: Hold a bridge at the top with the hips fully extended and level, feet hip-width, core braced.

Movement: Without letting the hips drop, lift one foot a few centimetres off the floor, set it down, then the other, in a slow march.

Focus: Keep the hips absolutely still and level, no rocking or dipping as the foot lifts. The glutes and core hold the position throughout.

For your game: Strong legs and glutes give you the ground force that powers your swing.

3 x 8 each · Bodyweight
Bird-dog – hold and pulse

Start: On all fours, back flat and neutral, core braced, ready to extend the opposite arm and leg.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out level with the body, then pulse them through a small range while holding the position, before swapping.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square and the back flat through every pulse, resisting the twist. Keep the pulses small and controlled, and stop if the back starts to sag or rotate.

For your game: Resisting rotation builds the core control that keeps your swing on plane and protects your back.

3 x 8 each · Bodyweight
Band crab walks

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, in a slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and out one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, then continue for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees pushing out, not caving in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips and glutes work.

For your game: Stable hips keep your lower body steady so you don't sway off the ball in the backswing.

3 x 10 each · Light
Balance – single leg free

Start: Stand on one leg without support, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to help balance.

Movement: Hold the single-leg balance freely for the set time, then swap.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, fixing the eyes on a point ahead. Make small ankle adjustments to stay steady, and keep the core engaged.

For your game: Steady balance like this keeps you sure-footed on slopes and uneven lies out on the course.

3 x 20s each · Bodyweight
Anterior tibialis toe raises

Start: Stand with the back against a wall or sit tall, heels down, toes free to lift.

Movement: Raise the toes and fronts of the feet up towards the shins, then lower under control.

Focus: Move only at the ankles, raising the toes as high as you can, and control the lower. Keep it slow and deliberate, feeling the front of the shin work.

For your game: Strong shins decelerate the foot and protect the knees over a long round.

3 x 15 · Bodyweight
Side plank – with hip dip

Start: Hold a side plank, body in a straight line, shoulder over the elbow.

Movement: Lower the hips slowly towards the floor a short way, then lift back up to the straight line, controlled, for the set reps.

Focus: Control the dip and the lift with the side waist, keeping the body in line front to back. Don't let the hips sag at the bottom or the torso rotate, and keep the shoulder stacked.

For your game: Side-core strength steadies your trunk and keeps you balanced over the ball.
