Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.

Suitcase squat

Start: Stand tall with a weight in one hand at the side like a suitcase, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Squat down by sending the hips back and bending the knees, then stand back up, keeping the torso upright.

Focus: Resist leaning towards the weight, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the ribs down. Keep the knees tracking over the feet, and stand tall throughout.

Push-up – incline, T rotation
2 sets8-10 repsWall/Bench/ChairBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up for an incline push-up with the hands on a bench, box or wall and the body in a straight line.

Movement: Lower the chest towards the bench and press back up. At the top, turn the whole body to one side and reach that arm up to the ceiling into a side plank, then return. Alternate sides each rep.

Focus: Keep the body moving as one piece as you turn, with no sagging at the hips. Stack the shoulder over the supporting hand before you reach up.

Glute kickback – band
3 sets8-10 repsBand/AnchorLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Loop a band around your ankles or hold it under the standing foot. Stand tall, hands on a support for balance, slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Push one leg back against the band using the glute, keeping it fairly straight, then return with control.

Focus: Squeeze the glute at the back and keep the movement at the hip. Don't arch the lower back or swing the leg to cheat the range.

Wall slides – single leg
2 sets8-10 each sideWallBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Back flat against a wall, feet a little way out, arms on the wall bent to 90 degrees. Lift one foot.

Movement: Slide the arms slowly up the wall towards straight, keeping contact the whole way, then back down, balance held throughout.

Focus: The arms do the work, the standing leg is the challenge. Back, arms and hands stay on the wall, ribs down, hips level. Feel the balance in the standing foot and ankle.

Cuban press
2 sets8-10 repsDB/BandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a light weight in each hand in front of the thighs, palms facing you.

Movement: Lift the elbows out to the sides to shoulder height, then turn the forearms up so the hands are above the elbows. Press the weights overhead, then reverse each stage slowly back down.

Focus: Use a light weight and move slowly through each stage, keeping the elbows at shoulder height before you turn. Stop short of any pinching at the top of the shoulder.

Lateral walks – band, squat depth
2 sets8-10 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Put a loop band just above the knees or round the ankles, feet hip-width, and sit down into a quarter squat with the chest up.

Movement: Step sideways with the lead foot, then follow with the other foot without letting the band go slack. Take the set number of steps one way, then come back the other way.

Focus: Stay at the same height the whole way, with the knees pressing out over the middle toes and the feet pointing forward. Keep the steps small and the band tight.

Single leg deadlift – supported, hold

Start: Stand on one leg, soft knee, weight held in front of the thigh, fingertips on a chair back or worktop in front of you.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold still.

Focus: Back flat, hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Light fingertip support only, enough to steady you, and take the hand away when you feel steady.

Hand plank – shoulder taps
2 sets8-10 each sideMatBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Set up in a hand plank with your hands under your shoulders and your body in a straight line from head to heels. Set your feet a little wider than your hips.

Movement: Lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder, put it back, then do the same with the other hand. Keep alternating for the set reps.

Focus: Keep your hips still and level as each hand lifts, not rocking side to side or lifting your bottom. Brace your middle, squeeze your glutes, and move slowly.
