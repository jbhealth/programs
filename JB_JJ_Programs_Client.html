Seated row – machine

Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit at seated row machine with slight forward lean.

Focus: Keep torso relatively upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together at end of pull.

Movement: Pull handle toward lower ribs, drive elbows back. Return under control.

⌄
Step-ups – lateral low
2 x 10 each legStep boxBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand to the side of a low step box.

Focus: Keep heel fully in contact with the step — don't let it hang off.

Movement: Step nearest foot up and sideways. Drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower under control.

Balance – single leg with arm reach
2 mins each sideBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

Glute bridges – single leg
2 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with foot flat, other leg extended.

Focus: Keep hips level — don't let one side drop.

Movement: Drive through the bent knee heel to push hips up. Hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

Dead bug – arms and legs
2 x 8 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Main Circuit
⌄
Leg press – partial range
2 x 12Leg press machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up in the leg press machine.

Focus: Limited range of motion — usually top half only. Maintain tension at a specific point.

Movement: Perform a leg press through only the top half of the range. Use when specifically prescribed.

⌄
Cable pull-through – hip hinge
2 x 12Cable machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set up at a cable pull-through station.

Focus: On the way down, feel the hamstring stretch. Control the descent.

Movement: Hip hinge slowly, then drive hips forward smoothly. Reinforces hip hinge mechanics.

⌄
Lat pulldown – close neutral grip
2 x 12Lat pulldown machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Use a close neutral grip attachment.

Focus: Drive your elbows down and squeeze at the bottom. Comfortable shoulder position.

Movement: Pull to chin level. Full extension at the top.

⌄
Chest press machine – bilateral
2 x 12Chest press machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit at chest press machine and grip handles at chest height.

Focus: Feel your chest working. Controlled tempo — don't rush the movement.

Movement: Press to full arm extension and return under control.

⌄
Hip abduction – clamshell
2 x 15 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your side, hips stacked, knees bent to about 45°, feet together.

Focus: Don't let hips roll backward — movement is only in the hip joint.

Movement: Open your top knee upward like a clamshell. Pause, close. Repeat. Add a band above knees for more resistance.

⌄
Bird-dog
2 x 8 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

⌄
Balance – single leg free
2 x 20s eachBodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg with no support.

Focus: Fix your gaze on a still point ahead. Keep a soft bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Stand tall and breathe steadily. Spread your foot wide for stability.
