Lower, Glute & Carry
Goblet squat
2 × 10Kettlebell6kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Romanian deadlift
2 × 12Dumbbell8kg

Start: Stand tall holding weight in front of your thighs.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout — feel the stretch develop in your hamstrings.

Movement: Push hips back and lower weight down the front of your legs. Drive hips forward to return.

Lateral band walks
2 × 10 each wayBandweight

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Calf raises – bilateral
2 × 12Stepweight

Start: Stand tall, feet flat on the floor or edge of a step.

Focus: Go through the full range — let heels dip below the step on the way down.

Movement: Rise up onto the balls of your feet as high as possible. Lower slowly through full range.

Suitcase carry
2 × 30 stepsDumbbellweight

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

Balance – single leg assisted
2 × 20s eachWallweight

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCable4.25kg

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Hip abduction – clamshell
2 × 12 eachBandweight

Start: Lie on your side, hips stacked, knees bent to about 45°, feet together.

Focus: Don't let hips roll backward — movement is only in the hip joint.

Movement: Open your top knee upward like a clamshell. Pause, close. Repeat. Add a band above knees for more resistance.

Glute bridges – march
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Hold a glute bridge at the top with hips fully extended.

Focus: Keep hips absolutely still — no rocking or dropping during the march.

Movement: Alternately lift each foot slightly off the floor in a marching motion. Maintain hip stability.

Push/Pull, Hinge & Core
Box squats – medium box
2 × 10Boxweight

Start: Stand in front of a medium-height box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees tracking over toes. Maintain tension — don't collapse onto the box.

Movement: Sit back and down to the box under control. Drive through heels to stand.

Band good morning
2 × 12BandBlack

Start: Stand on a resistance band with it across your upper traps.

Focus: Control the hip hinge — feel your hamstring stretch on the way down.

Movement: Hinge forward with slight knee bend and flat back until torso approaches parallel. Drive hips forward to return.

Single arm row – DB
2 × 12 eachDumbbell6kg

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control — don't let weight drop.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12Dumbbell1.25kg

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Shoulder press – seated DB
2 × 12Dumbbell3kg

Start: Sit tall with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.

Focus: Core braced, lower back neutral. Don't arch your lower back.

Movement: Press both dumbbells directly overhead to full arm extension. Lower to shoulder height under control.

Step-ups – forward low
2 × 10 eachStepBodyweight

Start: Stand in front of a low step or box.

Focus: Drive through the heel of the elevated foot — don't push off the back foot.

Movement: Step one foot up, drive through that heel to stand fully. Lower with control, repeat.

Waiter carry – single arm
2 × 30 stepsDumbbell3kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright.

Side plank – knees bent
2 × 20-30s eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your side with knees bent, bottom elbow under your shoulder.

Focus: Keep your hips straight — don't let your top hip roll backward. Stay in a straight line.

Movement: Lift your hips to form a straight line from knees to head. Hold for the prescribed time.

Bicycle twists
2 × 12 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands lightly behind your head and knees bent at 90°.

Focus: Rotation comes from your trunk — not from pulling your neck with your hands. Keep the movement controlled, not rushed.

Movement: Extend one leg while rotating your torso toward the bent knee, bringing the opposite elbow toward it. Alternate sides in a controlled cycling motion.
