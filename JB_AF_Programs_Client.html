Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Slow and steady, stretch a little each rep.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, holding a weight in front of your thighs, or hands empty, with a soft bend in the knees. Set the shoulders back and brace the core.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front of your legs, keeping it close, until you feel a stretch in the hamstrings. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, never let it round. The movement is a hip hinge, not a squat, and the weight stays close to your legs. Only go as low as you can keep the back flat.

Chest press – cable
3 sets10 repsCable1.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand at a chest-height cable, a handle in each hand at the chest, staggered stance, shoulder blades set, core braced.

Movement: Press the handles forward together until the arms are nearly straight, then return under control to the chest.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the ribs down, not arching to press. Keep the torso square and still, resisting the cable's pull to twist you. Keep the elbows a little below shoulder height, and control the return.

Squat – TRX

Start: Face the anchor holding the handles lightly at chest height, feet hip to shoulder-width, arms fairly straight.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, using the straps only for light balance, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Use the straps for balance, not to pull yourself up, and go only as deep as your knees stay comfortable.

Pull-through – band

Start: Stand facing away from a low band, the band between the legs held in both hands, hinged forward with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips forward to stand tall, pulling the band through, squeezing the glutes, then hinge back under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips and glutes, not the arms or lower back, keeping the back flat. Stand tall with the ribs down at the top, and control the hinge back.

Uneven carry

Start: Stand tall with a heavier weight in one hand and a lighter one (or none) in the other, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and level.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the heavier side, keeping the shoulders and hips level. Keep the ribs down, brace hard, and take steady even steps.

Single leg deadlift – weighted

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the weight lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, not rounding, and keep the hips level, not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Back extension – machine

Start: Set the pad at the top of your hips so you can bend freely. Cross your arms over your chest or place your hands by your temples, body in a straight line.

Movement: Hinge down at the hips with control, then raise back up until your body is straight. Move smoothly, no jerking.

Focus: Stop at a straight line, don't arch past it into the lower back. Lead the lift with the glutes and hamstrings, and keep the neck in line rather than craning up.
