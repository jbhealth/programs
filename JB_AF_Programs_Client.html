Wall squat – Swiss ball
2 sets8-10 repsSwiss ball/WallBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Remember to corkscrew the feet to protect the knees.

Start: Place a Swiss ball between your lower back and a wall, feet a stride out in front, hip-width.

Movement: Roll down the wall on the ball until the knees bend towards a right angle, then press back up through the heels. The ball supports and guides the movement.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and stacked over the ankles at the bottom. Keep pressure into the ball through the back, and go only as low as the knees stay comfortable.

Overhead press – single arm
2 sets8-10 each sideAny kit4kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Remember to set your knees soft and butt out (not tucked).

Start: Stand tall, a weight at one shoulder, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced against the offset load.

Movement: Press the weight overhead until the arm is straight, then lower back to the shoulder under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and resist leaning away from the weight, keeping the torso upright. Keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and press within a comfortable range.

Hip external rotation – seated, band
3 sets10-12 repsBand/ChairYellow band
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit tall on a bench or chair with the knees bent at 90 degrees and a band round the working ankle, fixed out to the side of that leg.

Movement: Swing the lower leg in across the other leg against the band, turning at the hip, then return slowly. Finish the set, then change legs.

Focus: Keep the knee still and the thigh on the seat, so the turn comes from the hip. Sit tall and move slowly in both directions. The foot moves in as the thigh turns out.

Focus: Drive the movement from the glute and keep the leg fairly straight, not arching the lower back to gain range. Keep the torso tall and the standing knee soft, and control the return.

Side plank – knees bent
2 sets20s each sideMatBodyweight

From James, for you: Don’t do this if it’s going to aggravate the left hip by laying on it.

Start: Lie on your side, forearm on the floor with the elbow under the shoulder, knees bent and stacked, hips off the floor.

Movement: Lift the hips so the body makes a straight line from head to knees and hold for the set time.

Focus: Keep the hips lifted and the body in a straight line, not letting the hips sag towards the floor. Keep the shoulder stacked over the elbow, and come down when the hips start to drop.

Elbow plank – leg lift
3 sets30 secMatBodyweight

Movement: Keeping the hips level, lift one foot a few centimetres off the floor, hold briefly, lower, then the other.

Focus: Keep the hips level and square as the leg lifts, no rotating or sagging. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and keep the lift small so the back stays neutral.

Box squat
How to
3 x 10Bodyweight

Start: Stand in front of a box or chair, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Box height is set to suit, so work to the height given in the notes.

Movement: Push the hips back and sit down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall by driving through the heels. Control the descent all the way down.

Focus: Keep the knees pushed out in line with the middle toes and the chest up as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping onto it, and drive up through the heels.

Romanian deadlift
How to
3 x 106kg

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, holding a weight in front of your thighs, or hands empty, with a soft bend in the knees. Set the shoulders back and brace the core.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front of your legs, keeping it close, until you feel a stretch in the hamstrings. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, never let it round. The movement is a hip hinge, not a squat, and the weight stays close to your legs. Only go as low as you can keep the back flat.

Chest press – cable
How to
3 x 101.5kg

Start: Stand at a chest-height cable, a handle in each hand at the chest, staggered stance, shoulder blades set, core braced.

Movement: Press the handles forward together until the arms are nearly straight, then return under control to the chest.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and the ribs down, not arching to press. Keep the torso square and still, resisting the cable's pull to twist you. Keep the elbows a little below shoulder height, and control the return.

Seated cable row – close neutral grip
How to
3 x 1019.25kg

Start: Sit at the low cable, feet braced, a neutral close handle, arms extended, chest up, back tall.

Movement: Pull the handle to the lower abdomen, elbows driving back close to the body, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Drive the elbows back and squeeze the shoulder blades, keeping the back tall and still. Keep the shoulders down, and control the return without rocking.

Curtsey lunge – bodyweight
How to
3 x 10

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, hands at the chest for balance.

Movement: Step one foot back and across behind the other into a curtsey, lowering under control, then drive back up to standing. Keep the hips fairly square.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving inward, and lower only as far as control and knee comfort allow. Keep the chest up and the hips square rather than twisting.

Hip abduction – laying
How to
3 x 10

Start: Lie on your side, legs straight and stacked, hips square, head supported, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Raise the top leg up to the side, keeping it straight and in line with the body, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the leg in line with the body and the hips stacked, not rolling back. Lead with the heel, keep it slow, and feel the outer hip.

Hip abduction – clamshell
How to
3 x 10

Start: Lie on your side, hips and knees bent, feet together, hips stacked, a band around the thighs if used.

Movement: Keeping the feet together, open the top knee up like a clam, then lower under control.

Focus: Open only as far as the hips stay stacked, not rolling the top hip back. Keep the movement slow, and feel the outer hip and glute work.

Hip abduction machine
How to
3 x 1019.25kg

Start: Sit at the machine, the outer thighs against the pads, back supported, feet on the rests.

Movement: Push the thighs apart against the pads, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Push apart smoothly and control the return, not letting the pads snap the legs together. Keep the back settled and the movement steady, feeling the outer hips.

Squat – TRX
How to
3 x 10-12Bodyweight

Start: Face the anchor holding the handles lightly at chest height, feet hip to shoulder-width, arms fairly straight.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, using the straps only for light balance, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Use the straps for balance, not to pull yourself up, and go only as deep as your knees stay comfortable.

Pull-through – band
How to
2 x 8-109.75kg

Start: Stand facing away from a low band, the band between the legs held in both hands, hinged forward with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips forward to stand tall, pulling the band through, squeezing the glutes, then hinge back under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips and glutes, not the arms or lower back, keeping the back flat. Stand tall with the ribs down at the top, and control the hinge back.

Uneven carry
How to
3 x 20m8kg and 6kg

Start: Stand tall with a heavier weight in one hand and a lighter one (or none) in the other, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Walk forward with controlled steps for the set distance, keeping the torso tall and level.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the heavier side, keeping the shoulders and hips level. Keep the ribs down, brace hard, and take steady even steps.

Balance – single leg assisted
How to
3 x 30s each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with light fingertip support on a wall or rail, torso tall, other foot lifted.

Movement: Hold the single-leg balance with light support for the set time, then swap.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, using only light support. Fix the eyes on a point ahead, keep the core engaged, and stand tall.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes
How to
3 x 30 sec

Start: Lie face down, or sit leaning forward, with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.
