Goblet squat
2 × 10Kettlebell2kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Dumbbell5kg

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

Wall slides
2 × 12Wallweight

Start: Back, head and arms touching wall. Feet few cm away. Elbows at 90°, arms in goalpost.

Focus: Keep entire back and both arms in wall contact throughout — no loss of contact.

Movement: Slide arms up slowly toward overhead while maintaining wall contact. Slide back down with control.

Scapular retraction – seated
2 × 12Bodyweightweight

Start: Sit tall with arms relaxed at your sides or bent at 90°.

Focus: Keep shoulders pressed down — don't shrug. Feel your shoulder blades squeeze together.

Movement: Draw shoulder blades back and together, hold 5 seconds, release slowly. Repeat.

Balance – single leg assisted
2 × 30s eachWallweight

Start: Stand on one leg with one finger lightly touching a wall for light support.

Focus: Balance through your standing foot, not through the wall. Keep the wall touch feather-light.

Movement: Stand on one leg for the prescribed time. Switch sides and repeat.

Lateral band walks
2 × 12 eachBandweight

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.

Glute bridges – bilateral
2 × 12Bandweight

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor.

Focus: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Brace your core throughout.

Movement: Drive your heels into the floor and push hips toward the ceiling, squeeze glutes, hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

Dead bug – arms only
2 × 10 eachMatweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time — this is non-negotiable.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead toward the floor, return, then alternate sides. Never let your lower back arch.

Reverse crunches
2 × 12Matweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.
