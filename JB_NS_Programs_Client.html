How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit
Squat – at sides
3 sets8-10 repsAny kit~2kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a weight in each hand by your sides, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, core braced.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the chest up, then drive through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the heels down throughout. Keep the chest up and the core braced, and go only as deep as your knees and hips stay comfortable.

Push press – DB
3 sets6-8 repsDBModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand at the shoulders, palms facing in, feet hip-width, ribs down, core and glutes braced.

Movement: Dip slightly at the knees, then drive up through the legs to help press the dumbbells overhead, then lower under control.

Focus: Use a short controlled leg dip and drive, keeping the ribs down and not arching the back on the press. Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, press both dumbbells evenly, and control the lower to the shoulders.

Pullover
3 sets10 repsAny kit2kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a bench holding a single dumbbell over the chest with both hands, arms fairly straight, ribs down, feet planted.

Movement: Lower the weight back overhead in an arc to a comfortable shoulder stretch, then pull it back over the chest.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and don't arch the lower back as the arms go overhead. Keep the arms fairly straight, go only to a comfortable stretch, and control the arc.

Chest fly
3 sets10 repsAny kit/Bench/Mat0kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a flat bench or on a mat, a weight in each hand pressed above the chest, elbows softly bent, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Open the arms out to the sides in an arc to a comfortable stretch, then bring them back together above the chest.

Focus: Keep a soft elbow bend held throughout, and go only to a comfortable chest stretch, not forcing the shoulders back. Keep the shoulder blades set, and control the arc.

Pullover – single arm
3 sets10 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on a bench holding a dumbbell over the chest in one hand, arm fairly straight, other hand resting on the chest, ribs down, feet planted.

Movement: Lower the weight back overhead in an arc to a comfortable shoulder stretch, then pull it back over the chest.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and don't let the lower back arch or the body twist as the arm goes overhead. Go only to a comfortable stretch, and control the arc.

Lateral raise – side-lying
3 sets10 repsDB/Mat0kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on one side with the head resting on the bottom arm and the knees bent, holding a light weight in the top hand resting on the thigh.

Movement: Raise the top arm up towards the ceiling until it is just short of vertical, then lower slowly to the thigh. Finish the set, then change sides.

Focus: Keep the arm straight but not locked and the shoulder down away from the ear. Move slowly, and stop short of any pinching.

External rotation – side-lying
3 sets10 repsDB0kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Lie on your side, the top arm's elbow pinned to the ribs and bent to 90 degrees, forearm across the tummy, a light weight in the working hand, or none.

Movement: Rotate the forearm upward, opening the hand towards the ceiling, keeping the elbow pinned, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the elbow pinned to the ribs and move only the forearm. Use a very light weight, keep the shoulder down, and control the slow lower.

Goblet squat
How to
3 x 10-122kg

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Deadlift
How to
3 x 10-124kg

Start: Stand with the weight on the floor between your feet, feet hip to shoulder-width. Push the hips back and hinge to take hold of it with both hands, back flat, chest up, shoulders over the weight.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall, pushing the hips forward and squeezing the glutes at the top. Hinge the hips back to lower the weight under control.

Focus: Keep the back flat from start to finish, never rounded. The power comes from the hips and legs, not the lower back or arms. Keep the weight close to your body on the way up and down.

Single arm row
How to
3 x 10-12 each side2kg and 4kg

Start: Hand and knee on a bench, the other foot planted, a weight in the free hand hanging down, back flat and level.

Movement: Pull the weight towards the hip, driving the elbow back and up, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the back flat and level, not twisting to lift. Keep the shoulder down, and control the lower.

Row – band
How to
3 x 10Black band

Start: Anchor a band at chest height and face it, an end in each hand, arms straight. Step back until there's tension, knees soft.

Movement: Pull the hands towards the lower ribs, driving the elbows back, then let the arms straighten slowly against the band.

Focus: Stand tall, ribs down, so the lower back doesn't arch to finish the pull. Squeeze the shoulder blades together, and don't let the band snap the arms forward.

Pallof press
How to
3 x 8 each sideBlack band

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height, feet shoulder-width, hands holding the handle at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to rotate you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable, this is the whole exercise. Keep the ribs down and the core braced, and press smoothly rather than snatching.

Anterior tibialis toe raises
How to
3 x 12-15Bodyweight

Start: Stand with the back against a wall or sit tall, heels down, toes free to lift.

Movement: Raise the toes and fronts of the feet up towards the shins, then lower under control.

Focus: Move only at the ankles, raising the toes as high as you can, and control the lower. Keep it slow and deliberate, feeling the front of the shin work.

Box squat – weighted
How to
3 x 10-122kg

Start: Stand in front of a box at about knee height holding a light weight at the chest, feet hip to shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down to lightly touch the box, then stand tall through the heels, keeping the weight at the chest.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes and the chest up. Sit back under control rather than dropping onto the box, and drive up through the heels.

Romanian deadlift – staggered stance
How to
3 x 10-125kg

Start: One foot flat, the other half a step back on its toes. Weight in front of the thighs, soft bend in the front knee.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the weight down the front leg until the hamstring loads, then drive the hips forward to stand tall. Swap legs halfway.

Focus: Most of the weight sits on the front leg, the back foot is only there for balance. Back flat, hips square, not twisting open.

Overhead press – seated
How to
3 x 10-122kg

Start: Sit tall on a bench with back support, a weight in each hand at shoulder height, elbows under the wrists, ribs down.

Movement: Press the the weights overhead until the arms are straight, then lower them back to shoulder height under control.

Focus: Keep the ribs down and the back against the support, not arching to press. Keep the shoulders down away from the ears, and lower under control to a comfortable shoulder range.

Front raise – slow eccentric
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Stand tall, a light dumbbell in each hand at the thighs, arms straight, ribs down.

Movement: Raise the arms forward to shoulder height, then lower slowly over three to four seconds under control.

Focus: Keep the shoulders down and the arms straight, then control the slow lower. Use a light weight, raise only to shoulder height, and don't lean back.

Bicep curls
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Stand tall with a weight in each hand at the sides, or standing on a band holding the handles, elbows pinned to the ribs, ribs down, core braced.

Movement: Curl the weights up towards the shoulders, keeping the elbows still, then lower under control.

Focus: Keep the elbows pinned to the sides and don't swing the body to lift. Keep the ribs down, and control the lower rather than dropping.

Anterior tibialis – toe raises off a step
How to
3 x 10-12Bodyweight

Start: Stand on a step or box with the heels on the surface and the toes hanging out over the edge. Hold a rail or frame for balance.

Movement: Let the toes lower below the level of the step under control, then pull them up towards the shins as high as they will go.

Focus: This is about range, not load. The edge lets the foot drop below level, which is where the shin has to work hardest and where most people have never trained. Go as low as the ankle allows before pulling back up, and keep the knees straight throughout.

Wrist flexion – supported
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Sit with the forearm resting along a thigh or bench, palm facing up, wrist and hand free over the edge, holding a light weight.

Movement: Curl the knuckles up towards the forearm, hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly to where the range stays pain-free rather than letting the weight drop the wrist back.

Focus: Move only at the wrist and only through the range that stays comfortable. The forearm never lifts off its support. Start lighter than feels necessary and add range before adding weight.

Terminal knee extension
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Stand with a band around the back of one knee anchored in front, a slight bend in that knee, holding a support.

Movement: Straighten the knee fully against the band, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend slightly under control.

Focus: Focus on fully straightening the knee and squeezing the front of the thigh hard at the top. Keep it slow and controlled, stay tall, and keep the movement pain-free.

Standing external rotation – arm raised
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Stand tall with the upper arm out to the side at shoulder height, elbow bent to 90 degrees, forearm pointing forward, holding a light weight.

Movement: Rotate the forearm up towards the ceiling, keeping the upper arm parallel to the floor, then lower under control to the start.

Focus: Keep the upper arm still and level at shoulder height, and move only the forearm. Go very light, keep the shoulder down away from the ear, and stop short of anything that pinches at the top.

Dorsiflexion – banded
How to
3 x 10-12Light

Start: Sit on the floor, working leg straight. Loop a band round the ball of the foot, anchored ahead of you so it pulls the foot away.

Movement: Pull the toes and forefoot back towards the shin against the band, then let it draw the foot slowly back out.

Focus: Keep the knee still and move only at the ankle. The slow return matters as much as the pull. Start with a light band, this muscle tires fast.

McGill curl-up – slow
How to
3 x 10-12Bodyweight

Movement: Slowly lift the head and shoulders just slightly, hold, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the lower back in its natural arch and brace, lifting only slightly and slowly. Keep the neck neutral, don't pull the chin in, and control every phase.

How to
