Goblet squat
3 sets8 repsAny kitModerate
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees in line with the middle toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

Drop landing – bilateral
3 sets5 repsBox/BenchBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on a low box or step, feet hip-width, ready to step off, core braced.

Movement: Step off and land softly on both feet in a quarter-squat, absorbing through the hips and knees, then stand.

Focus: Land softly and quietly, absorbing through the hips and knees, with the knees in line with the middle toes, not caving in. Aim for a stable, controlled catch rather than a stiff or collapsing landing.

Step-down – single leg
3 sets6 each sideStep boxBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on top of a low step on one leg, the other foot hovering off the edge, arms out for balance.

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, lightly tap, then drive back up to stand tall on the one leg.

Focus: Keep the standing knee tracking over the foot, never caving in, and the hips level throughout. Control the lower down slowly, and keep the movement in the standing leg, not by reaching with the free foot.

Pull-through – band
3 sets10 repsBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand facing away from a low band, the band between the legs held in both hands, hinged forward with a flat back, core braced.

Movement: Drive the hips forward to stand tall, pulling the band through, squeezing the glutes, then hinge back under control.

Focus: Drive the movement from the hips and glutes, not the arms or lower back, keeping the back flat. Stand tall with the ribs down at the top, and control the hinge back.

Dorsiflexion – banded
3 sets10 each sideBandLight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit on the floor, working leg straight. Loop a band round the ball of the foot, anchored ahead of you so it pulls the foot away.

Movement: Pull the toes and forefoot back towards the shin against the band, then let it draw the foot slowly back out.

Focus: Keep the knee still and move only at the ankle. The slow return matters as much as the pull. Start with a light band, this muscle tires fast.

Soleus – isometric hold
3 sets30s each sideChairBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Sit on a chair, feet flat, knees bent to about a right angle. Everything above the ankles relaxed.

Movement: Press the balls of the feet down, lift the heels and hold at about half effort. Build into the hold, and lower under control.

Focus: The gentlest way to load the deep calf, and it suits an ankle that is still being careful. No impact, so it can be done often. Little and often rather than hard.

Wall squat – single leg

Start: Back flat against a wall, feet a little way out. Take one foot off the floor and stand on the other leg.

Movement: Slide down the wall by bending the standing knee to about 45 degrees, hold for a slow count of five, then push back up to standing.

Focus: The wall takes the balance out so all the attention goes on the knee. Keep it in line with the middle toes, and stop short of any depth where it drifts inwards.

Good mornings – band

Start: Stand on the middle of a band, feet hip-width, and loop the other end behind the neck and across the upper back. Soft bend in the knees, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and hinge the torso forward with a flat back until the hamstrings load, then drive the hips forward to stand tall against the band.

Focus: Keep the back flat the whole way. Hinge at the hips rather than the waist, and let the band pull you forward only as far as that flat back allows. The band gets harder as you stand up, so drive the hips through to finish.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking out over the toes and the chest up as you sit back. Touch the box under control rather than dropping onto it, and drive up through the heels.

Squat matrix

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, hands relaxed or lightly clasped at the chest for balance.

Movement: Squat in a set sequence, changing stance or direction each rep (feet forward, wider, angled), sitting the hips back and down, then standing tall between each.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes in every stance, never letting them cave inward. Sit the hips back, keep the chest up, and only go as deep as you can control without knee discomfort.
