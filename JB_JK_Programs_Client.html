Main Circuit
⌄
Box squats – medium box
2 x 10BoxBodyweight

Start: Stand in front of a medium-height box, feet shoulder-width apart.

Focus: Keep knees tracking over toes. Maintain tension — don't collapse onto the box.

Movement: Sit back and down to the box under control. Drive through heels to stand.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
2 x 10 each sideDumbbell2.5kg

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

⌄
Step-ups – lateral medium
2 x 10 each legBoxBodyweight

Start: Stand to the side of a medium height box.

Focus: Drive through heel and stand fully at the top. Control the descent.

Movement: Perform lateral step-up on medium box. Greater height demands more from glute med and quad.

⌄
Single arm row – cable
2 x 12 each sideCable machine8.25kg

Start: Stand facing the cable machine, feet shoulder-width apart, slight bend in the knees.

Focus: Drive elbow back and squeeze shoulder blade at the end. Control the return.

Movement: Pull cable handle toward your lower ribs. Resist the cable pulling your shoulder forward as you return.

⌄
Cable press – stability
2 x 12Cable machine2 x 7kg

Start: Stand facing away from cable machine in staggered stance.

Focus: Resist the cable pulling your trunk back. Brace your core throughout.

Movement: Hold cable at shoulder height and press forward to full arm extension. As much core as pressing.

⌄
Cable hip abduction
2 x 12 each sideCable machine1.5kg

Start: Attach ankle strap to cable machine at lowest setting. Stand side-on to machine.

Focus: Keep your torso upright. Resist leaning away from the cable.

Movement: Step away from machine and lift attached leg out to side against cable.

⌄
Wall slides
2 x 12WallBodyweight

Start: Back, head and arms touching wall. Feet few cm away. Elbows at 90°, arms in goalpost.

Focus: Keep entire back and both arms in wall contact throughout — no loss of contact.

Movement: Slide arms up slowly toward overhead while maintaining wall contact. Slide back down with control.

⌄
Bird-dog
2 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.
