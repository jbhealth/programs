Goblet Squat & Reach (Light)
3 x 10Dumbbell

Start: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart holding a single dumbbell vertically at chest height (goblet style), cradling it with both hands

Focus: Keep your chest upright and core braced

Movement: Squat down as deep as mobility allows, then at the bottom position, reach the dumbbell forward or diagonally across your body (rotational reach). Return the dumbbell to chest and drive through your heels to stand. This combines squat mobility with functional reaching in a gentle, movement-rich pattern

KB Romanian Deadlift (Light)
—Kettlebell

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding a kettlebell in each hand at your sides or one in both hands at chest height

Focus: This is a hip hinge movement, not a knee bend

Movement: Hinge at the hips, pushing your bottom backward while the kettlebells travel down the front of your legs. Feel a stretch in your hamstrings. Drive your hips forward to stand back up, squeezing your glutes at the top. This posterior chain flow is gentle but highly mobile

Pivot Lunge (Rotational)
3 x 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, arms crossed on your chest or hands behind your head

Focus: This is a dynamic stepping and rotational movement

Movement: Step forward with one leg and pivot your torso toward that leg (rotating to that side). Lower into a lunge while rotating. Push through your front heel to return to standing, rotating back to center. Step back and return to the starting position. Alternate legs. This integrates stepping, rotation, and dynamic balance—excellent for movement quality post-holiday

Single-Leg Balance Reach (DB)
3 x 8 each sideDumbbell

Start: Stand on one leg with a soft knee, holding a light dumbbell in one hand

Focus: Balance and controlled movement

Movement: Maintain your single-leg stance while reaching the dumbbell forward, to the side, and backward in controlled motions (or perform a single reach in each direction). Return to center after each reach. This is pure movement quality work—gentle loading with high proprioceptive demand. Complete all reaches on one side, then switch legs

Cable Rotation (Low to High)
3 x 10 each sideCable

Start: Stand perpendicular to a cable machine with the handle set at knee height

Focus: This is a rotational core movement with full-body engagement

Movement: Rotate your torso, bringing the cable handle diagonally across your body and up to the opposite shoulder (high position). Feel the rotation and engagement in your core. Reverse the movement, rotating back to the low position. This is gentle, flowing rotational work with constant tension

Quadruped Shoulder Tap
3 x 10 each sideBodyweight

Start: Assume a quadruped position (hands and knees) with hands directly under shoulders and knees directly under hips

Focus: Maintain a stable, neutral spine throughout

Movement: Maintaining the quadruped position and core stability, lift one arm and tap the opposite shoulder. Return that hand to the floor. Lift the other arm and tap the opposite shoulder. Continue alternating in a controlled manner. This integrates core stability with shoulder mobility and proprioception—excellent post-holiday movement quality work

Reverse Lunge to Knee Raise
3 x 10 eachDumbbell or Bodyweight

Start: Stand tall with feet hip-width apart

Focus: Step backward far enough that your front knee can bend to approximately 90 degrees without excessive forward knee travel

Movement: Step one leg back, lowering into a lunge until your rear knee nearly touches the ground. Drive through your front heel to stand back up. As you stand, raise the rear leg's knee up in front of you (hip flexion). This creates a dynamic stepping pattern and develops balance and control. Alternate legs. This functional movement improves walking mechanics and postural control

Waiter Carry (Single Arm)
2 x 20m eachDumbbell or Kettlebell

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright. Complete the distance on one arm, then switch

Bird-Dog
2 x 8Bodyweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine

Focus: Keep your spine completely still—no rotation or hip drop

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, then return under control. Alternate sides
