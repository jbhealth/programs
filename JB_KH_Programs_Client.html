Compound Strength — Knee & Ankle
Goblet squat
2 x 10Kettlebell6kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

KB Romanian Deadlift (Light)
2 x 10Dumbbell10kg

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding a kettlebell in each hand at your sides or one in both hands at chest height

Focus: This is a hip hinge movement, not a knee bend

Movement: Hinge at the hips, pushing your bottom backward while the kettlebells travel down the front of your legs. Feel a stretch in your hamstrings. Drive your hips forward to stand back up, squeezing your glutes at the top. This posterior chain flow is gentle but highly mobile

Bulgarian split squat
2 x burnBenchweight

Start: Stand with your rear foot elevated on a bench behind you and your front foot far enough forward that your front shin stays vertical.

Focus: Keep your torso upright throughout. Your front knee should not travel excessively forward.

Movement: Lower into a deep single leg squat until your rear knee is just above the floor. Drive through your front heel to return.

Single-Leg Balance Reach (DB)
3 × 8 eachDumbbell2.5kg

Start: Stand on one leg with a soft knee, holding a light dumbbell in one hand

Focus: Balance and controlled movement

Movement: Maintain your single-leg stance while reaching the dumbbell forward, to the side, and backward in controlled motions (or perform a single reach in each direction). Return to center after each reach. This is pure movement quality work—gentle loading with high proprioceptive demand. Complete all reaches on one side, then switch legs

Cable Rotation (Low to High)
3 × 10 eachDumbbell7kg

Start: Stand perpendicular to a cable machine with the handle set at knee height

Focus: This is a rotational core movement with full-body engagement

Movement: Rotate your torso, bringing the cable handle diagonally across your body and up to the opposite shoulder (high position). Feel the rotation and engagement in your core. Reverse the movement, rotating back to the low position. This is gentle, flowing rotational work with constant tension

Back extensions – machine
2 x 10Back extension machineweight

Start: Position yourself in the back extension machine, hips at the edge of the pad with feet secured.

Focus: Don't hyperextend — return to a neutral straight line only.

Movement: Lower torso toward floor, then extend back. Squeeze glutes at top.

Calf raises – loaded single leg
2 × 12 eachKettlebell4kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Reverse crunches
Matweight

Start: Lie on your back with hands at sides or under lower back.

Focus: Reset your core and lower back position each time your feet touch the floor — don't rush into the next rep.

Movement: Draw knees toward your chest, lifting hips slightly off the floor. Lower feet back down to touch, pause and reset, then repeat.

Bird-Dog
2 × 8Dumbbellweight

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine

Focus: Keep your spine completely still—no rotation or hip drop

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, then return under control. Alternate sides
