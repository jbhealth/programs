Start: Hold dumbbells at your sides or at shoulders.

⌄
Romanian deadlift – BB
3 x 8Barbell15kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the barbell at hip height with a shoulder-width grip.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your thighs to mid-shin. Drive your hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Feel the hamstring stretch developing as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body.

⌄
Single arm row – DB
2 x 10DB/Bench7kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control, don't let weight drop.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

⌄
DB single-arm press
3 x 10 eachDumbbell5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

⌄
ViPR rotations
3 x 10 eachViPR6kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall holding the ViPR at waist height with both hands.

Movement: Rotate side to side in a controlled arc. The offset weight creates a unique challenge that a standard weight cannot replicate.

Focus: Start slowly — the offset load can catch you off guard. Core braced, control each direction of rotation.

⌄
Terminal knee extension
2 x 10 eachBandPurple
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Fix a resistance band behind the knee, stand facing the anchor.

Movement: Straighten knee fully against band resistance. Hold 1–2 seconds, bend knee to return.

Focus: Squeeze the front of your thigh hard at full extension. Keep your posture tall.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
3 x 8 eachDumbbell5kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

⌄
Calf raises – loaded single leg
3 x 12 eachKB/StepLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a kettlebell and stand on edge of a step on one leg.

Movement: Rise as high as possible on the calf. Progress load when 3x15 is pain-free.

Focus: Lower your heel slowly below the step to full stretch. Feel the Achilles lengthening.

⌄
Dead bug – arms and legs
2 x 10 eachMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward floor. Use a slow 4-count tempo. Alternate sides.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.
