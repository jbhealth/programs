Rotator Cuff & Scapula
Scapular retraction and depression
2 × 12Bodyweightweight

Start: Sit or stand tall.

Focus: Squeeze and press together — both movements happen at the same time.

Movement: Squeeze shoulder blades together AND press them down simultaneously. Hold 5 seconds, release.

Rotator cuff – side-lying external rotation
2 × 12 each · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Lie on your side, top elbow bent at 90°, tucked at your side.

Focus: Keep elbow fixed — all movement is at the shoulder. Use light weight only.

Movement: Rotate forearm upward away from the floor. Lower with control, repeat.

Side-lying internal rotation
2 × 12 each · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Lie on your side with top arm bent at 90°.

Focus: Use very light weight. Work through full range of motion.

Movement: Rotate forearm downward against gravity. Target the inner shoulder muscle.

Prone scaption
2 × 10 · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Lie face down with arms pointing forward at a 30–45° angle from your body — halfway between straight ahead and out to the sides. Thumbs pointing up.

Focus: Squeeze the muscles between and below your shoulder blades at the top.

Movement: Lift both arms a few centimetres off the floor. Squeeze at the top, lower with control.

Wall slides
2 × 10Wallweight

Start: Back, head and arms touching wall. Feet few cm away. Elbows at 90°, arms in goalpost.

Focus: Keep entire back and both arms in wall contact throughout — no loss of contact.

Movement: Slide arms up slowly toward overhead while maintaining wall contact. Slide back down with control.

Pendulum swings
2 × 30s eachBodyweightweight

Start: Lean forward with one hand on table for support. Let other arm hang completely relaxed.

Focus: Keep arm completely loose — movement comes from body swaying, not arm muscles.

Movement: Gently sway body to create arm swinging — forward/back, side to side, circles. Keep relaxed.

Delts, Arms & Mobility
Scapular clock
2 × 10 eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand or sit with good posture.

Focus: Move smoothly through all positions. Keep the movement controlled and fluid.

Movement: Move your shoulder blade through clockwise and anti-clockwise arcs — up, forward, down, back.

Isometric external rotation
2 × 20s eachWallweight

Start: Stand side-on to a wall, elbow bent at 90°, hand near your ribs.

Focus: Use very light effort. Work only in a pain-free range. No movement happens.

Movement: Press the back of your hand into the wall as if rotating your forearm outward, hold 5 seconds, release.

Lateral raises – slow eccentric
2 × 12 · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Stand tall with dumbbells at your sides, shoulders pressed down away from ears.

Focus: The slow eccentric lowering is the therapeutic part — particularly good for supraspinatus health. Keep shoulders down throughout.

Movement: Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height at normal speed. Lower over 4 seconds under full control. Repeat.

Reverse fly – DB
2 × 12 · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Stand with a slight hip hinge or lie face down.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at top. Control the lowering phase.

Movement: Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height. Lead with slight external rotation of upper arm.

Hammer curls – DB (light)
2 × 12 · lightDumbbellweight

Start: Stand tall with a light dumbbell in each hand, arms by your sides, palms facing in towards your body (neutral grip).

Focus: Keep your elbows tucked to your sides and shoulders relaxed and down — movement comes only from the elbow. The neutral grip keeps strain off the biceps tendon at the shoulder. Stop immediately if you feel any shoulder pain.

Movement: Slowly curl the weights up towards your shoulders keeping palms facing in, then lower under full control. Light weight, smooth and controlled tempo.

Scapular protraction – wall
2 × 12Wallweight

Start: Stand in a push-up position with hands on the wall.

Focus: Push the wall away and round your upper back, moving your shoulder blades apart.

Movement: Protract shoulder blades, hold briefly, allow them to come back. Repeat.

Cross-body shoulder stretch
2 × 30s eachBodyweightweight

Start: Stand or sit tall with good posture. Bring one arm straight across your chest.

Focus: Keep both shoulders down and relaxed — don't let your shoulders hunch up toward your ears.

Movement: Use your opposite hand to gently hold your elbow and pull your arm a bit closer to your body. You should feel a stretch in the back of your shoulder. Breathe steadily and hold for the prescribed time, then switch arms.
