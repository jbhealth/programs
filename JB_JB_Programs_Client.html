Shoulder Rehab & Rotator Cuff

Main Circuit
⌄
Prone Y raise
2 x 12Light DBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down on the floor or bench, thumbs pointing upward.

Movement: Raise both arms into a Y shape, lifting a few centimetres. Hold briefly, lower with control.

Focus: Keep the movement small — quality over height. Lower back stays relaxed.

⌄
Prone T raise
2 x 12Light DBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down, arms out to the sides in a T shape, thumbs up.

Movement: Lift both arms a few centimetres off the surface. Hold briefly, lower slowly.

Focus: Keep your neck long — don't crane upward. Squeeze shoulder blades together at top.

⌄
Side-lying external rotation – progressive load
2 x 12 each sideLight DBLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your side with elbow bent at 90°, tucked at your side.

Movement: Rotate forearm outward. Start with no weight, add light dumbbell progressively each session.

Focus: Build strength safely by progressively increasing weight over weeks.

⌄
Full can exercise
2 x 12 each sideLight DB1.25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with arms at your sides, thumbs pointing up.

Movement: Raise arms to shoulder height. This position reduces impingement risk.

Focus: Arms at 30° forward from body. Thumbs-up is more comfortable than thumbs-down.

⌄
Prone horizontal abduction
2 x 12Light DB1.25kg
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down with arms hanging off a bench or mat edge.

Movement: Raise both arms straight out to the sides to shoulder height. Lower with control.

Focus: Squeeze shoulder blades together at the top. Use controlled, steady movement.

⌄
Wall push-up plus
2 x 12WallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand facing a wall with hands at shoulder width.

Movement: Perform a push-up, then protract your shoulder blades. This is the easiest version for early shoulder rehab.

Focus: At the top of the push-up, push further away from the wall, rounding your upper back.

⌄
Glute bridges – march
2 x 10MatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a glute bridge at the top with hips fully extended.

Movement: Alternately lift each foot slightly off the floor in a marching motion. Maintain hip stability.

Focus: Keep hips absolutely still — no rocking or dropping during the march.

⌄
Dead bug – arms only
2 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°.

Movement: Lower one arm slowly overhead toward the floor, return, then alternate sides. Never let your lower back arch.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor the entire time — this is non-negotiable.

Scapular Control & Core Stability
Log the weight you used on the main lifts. Tap a move for cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Prone W raise
2 x 12Light DBBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie face down with elbows bent and hands near your head, forming a W shape.

Movement: Pull elbows back and down. Hold 2 seconds, lower slowly.

Focus: Use your rhomboids and middle traps — not momentum. Squeeze shoulder blades together.

⌄
Scapular retraction – seated
2 x 12BodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit tall with arms relaxed at your sides or bent at 90°.

Movement: Draw shoulder blades back and together, hold 5 seconds, release slowly. Repeat.

Focus: Keep shoulders pressed down — don't shrug. Feel your shoulder blades squeeze together.

⌄
Scapular depression
2 x 12BodyweightBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit or stand tall with arms relaxed at your sides.

Movement: Press both shoulders down hard, hold 5 seconds, release. Feel the muscles under your shoulder blades working.

Focus: Press shoulders down strongly, away from your ears — like pushing them into your back pockets.

⌄
Isometric shoulder press
3 x 20s each sideWallBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand close to a wall and place your hand flat against it at shoulder height.

Movement: Gently press your hand into the wall, hold 5–10 seconds with no movement, release. Try different angles.

Focus: Stay completely pain-free. Press gently — no movement should happen.

⌄
Bird-dog
2 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Start on all fours with a neutral spine.

Movement: Extend opposite arm forward and opposite leg behind. Hold 3 seconds, return under control.

Focus: Keep your spine completely still — no rotation or hip drop.
