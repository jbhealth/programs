Strength & Joint Stability
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
Goblet squat
2 x 8KB/DB8kg

Start: Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell vertically at chest height with both hands.

Focus: Keep chest tall throughout. Keep elbows inside your knees at the bottom.

Movement: Squat down, pushing knees apart with elbows at the bottom. Stand fully.

⌄
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
3 x 8DB2 x 7kg

Start: Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs and hinge at the hips, pushing them back while lowering the weights dow

Focus: Keep a flat back and feel the hamstring stretch before driving your hips forward to return

Movement: Keep the dumbbells close to your legs throughout. Dumbbells allow a slightly freer range of motion than a barbell

⌄
Single arm row – DB
2 x 8DB/Bench12kg

Start: Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold dumbbell in other hand, arm hanging straight down.

Focus: Drive elbow directly back, squeeze shoulder blade at top.

Movement: Row dumbbell up toward ceiling. Lower under control — don't let weight drop.

⌄
Arnold press
2 x 8DB2 x 5kg

Start: Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing you.

Focus: Slow rotation as you press engages all three heads of deltoid throughout.

Movement: Press upward while rotating palms away so they face forward at top. Lower, reversing rotation.

⌄
Single-leg deadlift – DB
2 x 8 each sideDumbbell7kg

Start: Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand at hip height. Slight bend in the standing knee.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the dumbbell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance until your torso is near parallel. Drive through your heel to return to tall.

⌄
Hollow body hold
3 x 30sMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with arms extended overhead and legs straight out.

Focus: Press your entire lower back firmly into the floor. Feel like a hollow banana shape.

Movement: Hold position with core tension. If lower back lifts off, raise your legs higher.

⌄
Dead bug – arms and legs
3 x 8 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°, light ankle weights on.

Focus: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout. Brace your core.

Movement: Extend opposite arm overhead and opposite leg toward the floor on a slow 4-count. Alternate sides.

⌄
Push-up
2 x maxBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Hands on the floor a little wider than shoulder-width, directly under or just outside your shoulders. Body in a straight line from head to heels, core braced and glutes squeezed. Drop to your knees to regress.

Focus: Hold a straight line head-to-heels the whole time — no sagging hips or hiking them up. Let your elbows travel back at roughly 45° rather than flaring straight out to the sides.

Movement: Lower under control until your chest is just above the floor, keeping the whole body rigid. Press back up to full arm extension without losing that plank line. Breathe in on the way down, out as you press.

Conditioning & Balance
Your programme. Tap a move for coaching cues.
Main Circuit
⌄
KB swings
4 x 12KBModerate

Start: Stand with feet slightly wider than hip-width, kettlebell on the floor just ahead of you.

Focus: Power comes entirely from your hip snap — not your arms. Think explosive hip extension, not shoulder raise.

Movement: Hinge at the hips and hike the kettlebell back between your legs. Explosively snap your hips forward to drive the kettlebell up to shoulder height. Let it fall back and repeat.

⌄
Reverse lunge – bodyweight
3 x 10 each legBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand tall with good posture.

Focus: Keep your front knee over your ankle and torso upright. Control the descent.

Movement: Step one foot back into a lunge, lowering rear knee toward floor. Push through front heel to return.

⌄
Band face pull – seated
3 x 12BandLight

Start: Sit on the floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Focus: Drive elbows wide and back. Squeeze with high elbows at the end.

Movement: Pull band toward your face. Seated position prevents cheating with momentum.

⌄
Push-up – varied grip
3 x 10BodyweightBodyweight

Start: Set up in a push-up position.

Focus: Three grip widths shift the load differently — standard targets chest and triceps, wide emphasises chest, narrow emphasises triceps. Maintain solid form throughout.

Movement: Perform push-ups in three grips within the same set: standard, wide, then narrow. Adjust hand position between sets.

⌄
Glute bridges – single leg
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with foot flat, other leg extended.

Focus: Keep hips level — don't let one side drop.

Movement: Drive through the bent knee heel to push hips up. Hold 2 seconds, lower with control.

⌄
Suitcase carry
3 x 30s each sideDB/KBModerate

Start: Hold heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist lateral pull trying to lean you toward loaded side. Completely upright.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

⌄
Balance – single leg with arm reach
3 x 25s each sideBodyweightBodyweight

Start: Stand on one leg with soft knee bend.

Focus: Control the movement slowly. The arm reach is the challenge, not speed.

Movement: Reach opposite arm in three directions: forward, out to side, diagonally behind. Return between each reach.

⌄
Standing hip abduction – ankle weights
3 x 12 each sideBodyweightModerate

Start: Ankle weights on, stand tall on one leg with the standing knee slightly bent. Hold a wall or chair if needed.

Focus: Keep the standing hip still and torso upright, do not lean to the side. Feel the glute med working.

Movement: Slowly lift the other leg out to the side, then control it back down. Lift and return both slow.
