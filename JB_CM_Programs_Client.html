Program 1 — KB Power & Carry
Week commencing: 1 June 2026
Hip power · Anti-rotation carries · Shoulder stability
2 rounds60 sec rest30 minsKB · Bands · Bodyweight
Main Circuit
2 rounds · 60 sec rest
KB Swings
3 × 12 · Moderate-HeavyKettlebell

Start: Stand with feet slightly wider than hip-width, kettlebell on the floor just ahead of you.

Focus: Power comes entirely from your hip snap — not your arms. Think explosive hip extension, not shoulder lift.

Movement: Hinge at the hips and hike the kettlebell back between your legs. Explosively snap your hips forward, letting the bell float to chest height. Control the descent back into the hinge.

Push-up – Varied Grip
2 × 8 each gripBodyweight

Start: Set up in a push-up position.

Focus: Three grip widths shift the load differently — standard targets chest and triceps, wide emphasises chest, narrow hits triceps hard.

Movement: Perform push-ups in three grips within the same set: standard, wide, then narrow. Maintain a strict plank line throughout.

Waiter Carry – Single Arm
2 × 20m eachKettlebell

Start: Hold a kettlebell overhead in one hand like a waiter carrying a tray, arm locked out.

Focus: Resist trunk rotation and lateral flexion throughout. Keep your arm locked straight overhead.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the overhead position. Keep your torso completely upright — no leaning away from the load.

Balance – Single Leg Eyes Closed
2 × 20 sec eachBodyweight

Start: Establish a controlled single leg stance with eyes open first. Soft knee bend, tall spine.

Focus: Closing your eyes dramatically increases proprioceptive demand by removing the visual cue. Master eyes-open first — don't rush.

Movement: Once you have solid eyes-open control, close your eyes and hold balance. Open if needed to reset. Build time gradually.

Side Plank – With Rotation
2 × 8 each sideBodyweight

Start: Set up in a full side plank — elbow under shoulder, feet stacked.

Focus: Rotation comes from your thoracic spine — not your lumbar. Keep your lower back stable throughout.

Movement: Reach your top arm underneath your body, rotating through your upper back. Return to the start position under control.

Goblet Squat – Pause at Bottom
2 × 8 · ModerateKettlebell

Start: Hold a kettlebell in front of chest.

Focus: At the bottom, keep your chest tall and lower back neutral. Hold the tension — don't collapse into the hips.

Movement: Squat down and pause 3 seconds at bottom. Drive through heels to stand.

Bottoms-Up KB Carry
2 × 15m eachKettlebell

Start: Hold a kettlebell upside down — bell pointing up, handle pointing down — at shoulder height with your elbow bent at 90°.

Focus: Continuous rotator cuff firing required to maintain the inverted position. Start with a light kettlebell — harder than it looks.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the upside-down position at shoulder height. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Cardio Finisher
Ice Skaters
30 sec on · 15 sec off · 4 rounds · ~3 mins. Push off one leg and bound laterally, landing softly on the opposite foot. Stay on the balls of your feet. Build speed as you settle in.
Program 2 — DB Strength & Stability
Week commencing: 1 June 2026
Lateral squat pattern · Anti-lateral flexion · Single leg strength
2 rounds60 sec rest30 minsDB · Bodyweight
Main Circuit
2 rounds · 60 sec rest
DB Lateral Lunge
3 × 10 each · ModerateDumbbell

Start: Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.

Focus: Keep the lunging knee tracking over your foot. Chest tall throughout.

Movement: Take a wide step to one side, bend that knee while keeping the other leg straight. Push through the lunging heel to return to standing.

Single Arm Clean and Press
3 × 6 each · ModerateDumbbell / Kettlebell

Start: Stand with a kettlebell or dumbbell on the floor between your feet.

Focus: Hip drive initiates the clean — not your arms. Explosive hip extension then press overhead in one fluid movement.

Movement: Hinge and clean the weight to shoulder height using explosive hip drive. Press overhead to full arm extension. Lower under control.

Suitcase Carry
2 × 20m eachDumbbell / Kettlebell

Start: Hold a heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand at side.

Focus: Resist the lateral pull trying to lean you toward the loaded side. Stay completely upright — anti-lateral flexion challenge.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance. Switch hands at halfway point.

ISO Romanian Deadlift – Single Leg
2 × 20 sec eachDumbbell

Start: Hinge into a single leg RDL position with your torso roughly parallel to the floor and one leg extended behind.

Focus: Feel the posterior chain endurance demand. Keep your back flat and hips square throughout.

Movement: Hold the position for the prescribed time. Use a light dumbbell in one or both hands. Focus on hamstring tension — not just balance.

Hollow Body Hold
2 × 25 secBodyweight

Start: Lie on your back with arms extended overhead and legs straight out.

Focus: Press your entire lower back firmly into the floor. Feel like a hollow banana shape — the lower back must stay flat.

Movement: Hold position with core tension. If lower back lifts off, raise your legs higher.

Lunge and Rotate
2 × 10 each · Light-ModerateDumbbell

Start: Stand tall ready to step into a lunge.

Focus: Rotation comes from your thoracic spine, not arm swinging. Control both the lunge and the rotation.

Movement: Step into lunge, at bottom rotate torso toward front leg while holding weight. Return to centre, push back to standing. Alternate sides.

ISO Split Squat – Weighted
2 × 20 sec each · LightDumbbell / Kettlebell

Start: Hold dumbbells or a kettlebell and lower into a split squat — both knees at 90°, front shin vertical, rear knee hovering just above the floor.

Focus: Torso upright, front shin vertical, core braced. Hips level. Builds strength endurance in quads and hip flexors simultaneously.

Movement: Hold the bottom position isometrically for the prescribed time. Don't let the rear knee touch the floor.

Cardio Finisher
Mountain Climbers
30 sec on · 15 sec off · 4 rounds · ~3 mins. Full push-up plank, hips level. Drive knees to chest alternating at a controlled-to-fast pace. Build speed as rounds progress.
