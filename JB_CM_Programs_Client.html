Deadlift – KB
2 x 12Kettlebell12kg

Start: Stand with a kettlebell between your feet.

Focus: Keep your back flat throughout. Keep kettlebell close to your legs.

Movement: Push hips back, grip handle. Drive through heels to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.

KB thruster
2 x 10Kettlebell8kg

Start: Hold kettlebells in the rack position at shoulder height.

Focus: Leg drive initiates the press. This should feel like one fluid squat-to-press movement.

Movement: Squat below parallel, drive explosively to standing and press the kettlebells overhead as you rise.

Renegade row
3 × 8 eachDumbbell5kg then 7kg

Start: Start in a push-up plank position with a dumbbell in each hand directly under your shoulders.

Focus: Prevent your hips from rotating — that's the whole challenge. Row one side at a time while the rest of the body stays still.

Movement: Row one dumbbell to hip height, keeping your hips level. Place it back down, then row the other side.

Single leg deadlift – KB
2 × 8 eachKettlebell8kg

Start: Stand on one leg holding a kettlebell in the opposite hand.

Focus: Keep your back flat and hips square throughout. Resist the pelvis rotating toward the free leg.

Movement: Hinge forward at the hip, lowering the kettlebell toward the floor while your rear leg extends behind as a counterbalance. Drive through your heel to return.

Band pallof press – half kneeling
2 × 10 eachBand–

Start: Kneel on one knee perpendicular to a band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotational pull of the band. Keep your hips square and torso facing forward.

Movement: Hold band at chest and press straight out. Return to chest.

Dead bug – with resistance
2 × 10 eachBand2kg each

Start: Set up in dead bug position holding a band taut between hands and opposite knee.

Focus: Maintain band tension throughout. Feel intra-abdominal pressure and bracing.

Movement: Extend opposing arm and leg away from each other. Return and alternate.

Stability, Push/Pull & Carry
Single arm overhead press
3 × 6 eachDumbbell–

Start: Stand tall with one dumbbell or kettlebell at shoulder height. Other arm at your side.

Focus: Resist the trunk rotation and lean created by the single arm load. Core braced, hips level.

Movement: Press the dumbbell overhead to full arm extension with one arm. Anti-rotation is the key stimulus. Lower under control.

Cossack squat
2 × 8 eachBodyweight–

Start: Stand with feet very wide apart, toes pointing slightly out.

Focus: Shift your weight deeply to one side while the other leg remains straight. Feel the deep hip and adductor stretch.

Movement: Bend one knee to squat deeply to that side while the other leg stays straight. Return to centre and alternate sides.

Rack carry – KB
2 × ~30m eachKettlebell–

Start: Hold the kettlebell in a rack position at shoulder height on one side — elbow tucked, forearm vertical.

Focus: Single arm rack position challenges both anti-lateral flexion and rotational stability. Stay tall throughout.

Movement: Walk for the prescribed distance maintaining the rack position. Keep your torso upright.

Band chest press – standing
3 × 12Band–

Start: Anchor a band behind you at chest height. Face away.

Focus: Resist the band pulling your arms back. Feel the resistance increase at full extension.

Movement: Press hands forward from chest height to full arm extension.

Band row – seated on floor
3 × 12Band–

Start: Sit on floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Focus: Drive elbows back and squeeze shoulder blades. No lower body movement.

Movement: Pull band toward lower abdomen. Seated position isolates the back.

Pivots
2 × 10 eachBodyweight–

Start: Stand on one leg with soft bend in knee.

Focus: Keep your standing knee tracking forward and core braced. Control rotation — don't swing.

Movement: Pivot your body 90° left and then 90° right, rotating through hip and ankle.

Corkscrews
2 × 12Mat–

Start: Lie on your back with both legs raised to 90° and arms flat on the floor at your sides.

Focus: Movement is driven by your core — not momentum. Keep your upper back and shoulders flat on the floor throughout.

Movement: Rotate your hips and legs in a large controlled circle in one direction, then reverse. Return legs to centre between each direction.
