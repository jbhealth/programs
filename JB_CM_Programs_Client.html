Goblet squat
3 x 10KB/DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

⌄
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
3 x 10DBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, a dumbbell in each hand resting in front of your thighs, knees softly bent, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the dumbbells down the front of your legs, keeping them close, until you feel the hamstrings stretch. Drive the hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the dumbbells close throughout. Hinge at the hips, don't squat or round the back. Go only as low as your flat back allows.

⌄
DB floor press – single arm
3 x 8 each sideDBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back on the mat, knees bent, feet flat, one dumbbell in one hand at chest height, the upper arm resting lightly on the floor with the elbow at about 45 degrees.

Movement: Press the dumbbell up until the arm is nearly straight, then lower under control until the upper arm rests back on the floor.

Focus: Let the elbow touch the floor gently each rep rather than crashing down, and keep the shoulder blade set. Because it is one side at a time, brace the trunk so it does not twist toward the working arm.

⌄
Single arm row – DB
3 x 10 each sideDBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hand and knee on a bench, the other foot planted, a dumbbell in the free hand hanging down, back flat and level.

Movement: Pull the dumbbell towards the hip, driving the elbow back and up, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the back flat and level, not twisting to lift. Keep the shoulder down, and control the lower.

⌄
Lateral band walks
2 x 12 each sideBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand with a band around the legs (ankles or above the knees), feet hip-width, knees softly bent, torso tall.

Movement: Step sideways one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, for the set steps, then return the other way.

Focus: Keep tension on the band throughout and the feet pointing forward, not letting the knees cave in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips work.

⌄
Bird-dog
3 x 10 each sideMatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat and neutral, core braced.

Movement: Reach the opposite arm and leg out straight until level with the body, hold briefly, then return under control and swap.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square to the floor and the back flat, resisting any twist or sag as the limbs extend. Move slowly, and reach only as far as you keep the neutral spine.

Band deadlift

Start: Stand on the middle of a band, feet hip to shoulder-width, and take a handle or the band in each hand. Hinge down with a flat back, chest up.

Movement: Drive through the floor and stand tall against the band's pull, squeezing the glutes at the top, then hinge the hips back to lower with control.

Focus: Keep the back flat throughout, power from the hips and legs. Control the return, don't let the band snap you back down.

2 x 10 · Black band
Band squat

Start: Stand on a band, feet shoulder-width, holding the band at the shoulders or looped over them, chest up, core braced.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat against the band's tension, then drive up to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down. Let the band add resistance without pulling you forward, and squat only as deep as your knees stay comfortable.

3 x 12 · Black band
Band push-up

Start: Loop a band across the upper back and under the hands, set up in a push-up position, body in a straight line, core braced.

Movement: Lower the chest towards the floor, then press back up against the band's resistance, keeping the line.

Focus: Keep the body straight, not sagging at the hips. Keep the elbows tucked and the shoulders down, and control the press against the added band tension.

2 x 10 · Yellow
Band row – seated on floor

Start: Sit on the floor, legs straight out, band looped around the feet, sitting tall from the hips, chest up.

Movement: Pull the band towards your lower abdomen, driving the elbows back, then return under control.

Focus: Drive the elbows back and squeeze the shoulder blades, not just bending the arms. Keep sitting tall with no rocking, and keep the shoulders down away from the ears.

3 x 12 · Black band
Foot anchored wood chops low to high

Start: Anchor a resistance band under one foot and hold the other end in both hands down by the opposite hip, feet hip-width, knees soft, core braced.

Movement: Sweep the band up and across from the low hip to above the opposite shoulder, rotating through the trunk, then return under control.

Focus: Drive the rotation through the hips and trunk together, ribs down, and control the band back down. Don't yank with the arms or twist the lower back.

2 x 10 each · Black band
Band crab walks

Start: Stand with a band around the legs, feet hip-width, knees softly bent, in a slight squat, torso tall.

Movement: Step diagonally forward and out one foot at a time, keeping tension on the band, then continue for the set steps.

Focus: Keep tension on the band and the knees pushing out, not caving in. Stay low and controlled, and feel the outer hips and glutes work.

3 x 10 each · Black
Dead bug – arms and legs

Start: Lie on your back, knees bent over the hips, arms towards the ceiling, lower back pressed gently into the floor.

Movement: Lower the opposite arm and leg slowly towards the floor, keeping the back flat, then return and swap sides.

Focus: Keep the lower back pressed to the floor as the limbs extend, never letting it arch. Move slowly and controlled, and shorten the reach if the back lifts.
