Main Circuit
⌄
Band deadlift
3 x 10BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on a resistance band and hold both ends at your sides.

Movement: Perform hip hinge, lower toward floor, drive through heels to stand. Band teaches hip hinge well.

Focus: Feel the band resistance increasing as you stand. Drive through your heels.

⌄
Band squat
3 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on a resistance band, hold it at shoulder height.

Movement: Squat to depth, drive back up against band resistance.

Focus: The band gets heavier as you stand, loading the top of the movement.

⌄
Band push-up
3 x 10BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Loop a resistance band across your upper back and under both hands in a push-up position.

Movement: Perform a push-up with the band assisting the press phase. Maintain a strict plank line.

Focus: The band provides assistance on the press phase. Feel the chest driving at the top.

⌄
Band row – seated on floor
3 x 12BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit on floor with legs straight, band looped around your feet.

Movement: Pull band toward lower abdomen. Seated position isolates the back.

Focus: Drive elbows back and squeeze shoulder blades. No lower body movement.

⌄
Band crab walks
3 x 10 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Place a resistance band above your knees in a wide, deep squat stance.

Movement: Step sideways maintaining the deep squat position. This is more demanding than standard lateral walks.

Focus: Keep chest up and push knees firmly outward against the band at all times.

⌄
Dead bug – slow with band
3 x 8 eachBandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie on your back with hips and knees at 90°. Loop a band between both hands and the opposite knee to create tension.

Movement: Slowly extend the opposing arm and leg over 4 seconds while maintaining lower back contact. Return slowly over 4 seconds.

Focus: 4-second tempo both extending and returning. Quality is the priority, this is the most challenging dead bug variation.
