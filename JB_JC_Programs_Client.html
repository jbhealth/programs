How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Main Circuit

Single leg box squat
3 x 8-12 each sideBoxBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg in front of a box or chair, the other leg extended forward off the floor, arms out for balance.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down on the one leg to lightly touch the box, then drive up through the heel to stand.

Focus: Keep the working knee tracking over the toes, never caving in, and the hips level. Touch the box under control, use a higher box to start, and keep the heel down throughout.

⌄
ISO deadlift pull – mid shin
3 x 40sBarbell/RackModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Set the bar against pins or blocks at mid-shin. Take your deadlift stance and grip, chest up, flat back, shoulders over the bar, core braced.

Movement: Pull hard against the immovable bar as if to stand it up, building maximum tension, and hold that effort for the prescribed time.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the chest up through the whole pull, exactly as in a real lift. Drive through the legs and hips, not the lower back, and breathe in short controlled breaths rather than fully holding.

⌄
Chest press – Swiss ball
3 x 8-12Any kit/Swiss ballModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Lie with the upper back on a Swiss ball, hips up in a bridge, a weight in each hand at chest height, shoulder blades set.

Movement: Press the the weights up until the arms are nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the hips up and the core braced to stay stable on the ball, and the shoulder blades set. Keep the elbows at about 45 degrees, and control the lower.

⌄
Curtsey lunge – weighted
3 x 8-12 each sideAny kitModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hold a weight at the chest, feet hip-width, torso tall.

Movement: Step one foot back and across behind the other into a curtsey, lowering under control, then drive back up to standing.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving in, and lower only as far as knee comfort allows. Keep the chest up and the hips square, and drive up through the front heel.

⌄
Knee extension – cable
3 x 8-12Cable machineModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Sit or stand with a cuff around one ankle attached to a low cable behind, the knee slightly bent.

Movement: Straighten the knee against the cable, squeezing the thigh, then let it bend back under control.

Focus: Straighten under control and squeeze the thigh, never kicking the knee straight. Keep the range comfortable and pain-free, and control the return.

⌄
ISO single leg deadlift
3 x 40s each sideDBModerate
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand on one leg, a soft bend in the knee, holding a weight in front of the thigh, the other leg ready to trail behind.

Movement: Hinge at the hip and lower the weight down the standing leg while the free leg lifts behind, reach a flat-backed stretch, then hold that bottom position still.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the hips level, don't let the raised hip open out. Hold steady through the hips and standing glute, and keep the weight close to the leg.

⌄
Pallof press – standing lunge
3 x 8-12 each sideCable/BandLight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band at chest height in a split stance, one foot forward in a shallow lunge, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the handle straight out in front of you, resisting the pull that wants to twist you, hold briefly, then draw it back.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square over the split stance, letting nothing rotate you towards the cable. The lunge stance narrows your base, so the anti-rotation job gets harder. Keep the ribs down and press smoothly.

⌄
Pike – Swiss ball
3 x 8-12Swiss ball/MatBodyweight
Logged weight (if necessary)✓

Start: Hands on the floor, shins or feet on a Swiss ball, body in a straight line, core braced hard.

Movement: Lift the hips towards the ceiling into a pike, rolling the ball in, then lower back to the straight line under control.

Focus: Lift from the core and control the ball, not letting the hips sag on the return. Keep the shoulders steady over the hands, and move smoothly.

Massage gun release – lower back and glutes

Start: Lie on your side or sit leaning forward with the area relaxed. The muscle has to be slack.

Movement: Work the gun slowly over the muscles just above the hips and across the glutes, pausing on tender spots for twenty to thirty seconds.

Focus: Pressure you can breathe through, never pain. Stay off the spine and the bony ridge of the pelvis. Anything sharp, electric or numb down the leg, come off that spot straight away.

Standing long jump – single leg

Start: Stand on one leg, knee soft, arms ready to swing, core braced. Take a moment to settle the foot and find the arch before you go.

Movement: Swing the arms, load the hip, and jump forward off one leg. Land on the same leg in a quarter-squat and hold it still for a second before stepping back to the start.

Focus: The landing is the exercise. Land on the same leg, softly, with the knee tracking over the middle of the foot and the hip absorbing the load rather than the knee. If the knee drops inwards, the jump was too far. Shorten it until every landing is clean, then build distance from there.
