How to run it
Straight sets: do all the sets of each exercise, rest, then move to the next.
Goblet squat – pause at bottom
2 sets8-10 repsAny kit10kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: As deep as you can each time!

Start: Hold a weight at the chest with both hands, elbows tucked, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit down into a squat, pause for two to three seconds at the bottom holding still, then drive up through the heels to stand.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes and the heels down through the pause. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and pause only at a depth you control comfortably.

Deadlift – trap bar
3 sets6-10 repsTrap bar40kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand in the centre of the trap bar, feet hip-width, the handles in line with the middle of your feet. Hinge down and grip the handles in the middle, chest up, back flat, core braced.

Movement: Push the floor away and stand tall, hips and shoulders rising together. Finish standing tall with the glutes, then sit the hips back and lower the bar under control to the floor.

Focus: The back stays flat from floor to lockout, never rounded. Push through the whole foot with the heels down, and stand tall at the top without leaning back.

Push press – BB
2 sets8-10 repsBarbell/Rack17.5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Little squat, big push

Start: Stand tall, a barbell racked at the shoulders, feet hip-width, ribs down, core and glutes braced.

Movement: Dip slightly at the knees, then drive up through the legs to help press the bar overhead, then lower under control.

Focus: Use a short controlled leg dip and drive, keeping the ribs down and not arching the back on the press. Keep the knees tracking over the feet, and control the lower to the shoulders.

Dips – machine assisted
3 sets8-12 repsAssisted dip machine35kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Slowly down, for safety. Have the bars in their ‘narrow’ setting.

Start: Set the assist, grip the dip bars, arms straight, chest up, shoulders down, ribs down.

Movement: Bend the elbows to lower until the upper arms are about parallel, then press back up to straight arms.

Focus: Lower only to a comfortable shoulder depth, not below, to protect the shoulders. Keep the shoulders down away from the ears, the chest up, and press up under control.

Single leg deadlift – weighted
To burn x2 each sideAny kit8kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one leg holding a weight, the other leg ready to travel back, torso tall, standing knee soft.

Movement: Hinge forward from the hip, letting the free leg travel back as the weight lowers, then return to standing.

Focus: Hinge from the hip with a flat back, not rounding, and keep the hips level, not opening out. Keep the standing knee soft, move slowly, and control the return.

Balance board – soft board
3 sets30 secBalance boardBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on the soft board on one foot, knee soft, torso tall, with a support within reach.

Movement: Hold steady on the one leg for the set time, making small constant corrections from the foot and ankle. Step off and swap sides between sets.

Focus: Let the ankle and foot do the correcting rather than the arms. Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, and keep a hand near the support until it settles.

Calf raises – loaded single leg
3 sets8-12 each sideKB/Step5kg
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.

Start: Stand on one foot, the ball of the foot on a step edge, a weight in the hand, holding a support with the other.

Movement: Rise up onto the toes as high as you comfortably can, then lower the heel slowly under control.

Focus: Rise and lower slowly and evenly, not bouncing, through the full comfortable range. Keep the ankle tracking straight, not rolling out, and control the lower.

Broad jump – repeated
3 sets10 repsBodyweightBodyweight
Did something different? Change Sets, Reps or Weight.
From James, for you: Do sideways ones, not forward.

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, knees soft, arms ready, core braced.

Movement: Jump forward and land softly, then immediately load and jump again, for the set reps.

Focus: Land softly with the knees tracking over the toes, absorbing through the hips and knees each time. Keep every landing controlled before the next jump, not rushing into a sloppy rep.

Front squats

Start: Rest the bar across the front of the shoulders, elbows lifted high to make a shelf, hands lightly guiding the bar, feet shoulder-width, core braced hard.

Movement: Sit the hips down with an upright torso, elbows kept high, to a comfortable depth, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the elbows up so the chest stays tall and the bar secure, and the knees tracking over the toes. Keep the heels down and the core braced, and control depth to protect the knees and back.

3 x 6 · 15kg
Romanian deadlift – BB

Start: Stand tall with a barbell held at the front of your thighs, hands just outside your legs, feet hip-width. Set the shoulders back, brace the core hard, and keep a soft bend in the knees.

Movement: Push the hips back and slide the bar down the front of your legs, keeping it in contact, until you feel a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Keep the knees still and the shins vertical. Drive the hips forward to stand tall and finish with the glutes.

Focus: The back stays flat from start to finish, never rounded. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, so the movement happens at the hips while the bar tracks close to the legs. Only lower as far as you can hold a flat back, and don't lean back or over-arch at the top.

2 x 10 · 25kg
Hip thrust – BB single leg

Start: Upper back on a bench, a padded bar across the hips, one foot flat and hip-width, the other leg lifted and held. Chin tucked, core braced.

Movement: Drive through the planted heel to push the hips up level, squeeze the glute hard at the top, hold briefly, then lower with control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Finish at a straight line with the glute, not by arching the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

3 x 10 each · Moderate
Seated cable row – single arm

Start: Sit at the low cable, a single handle in one hand, feet braced, arm extended, chest up, back tall.

Movement: Pull the handle to the side of the abdomen, driving the elbow back, then return slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the torso square rather than rotating to pull. Keep the back tall and the shoulder down.

3 x 12 each · 8.25kg then 13.75kg
Incline press

Start: Lie on an incline bench, a dumbbell in each hand at upper-chest height, shoulder blades set back, feet planted.

Movement: Press the dumbbells up until the arms are nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set on the bench and the elbows at about 45 degrees, not flared wide. Keep the ribs down, and control the lower to a comfortable shoulder range.

3 x 10 · 2 x 6kg
Walking lunges – suitcase

Start: Stand tall with a weight in one hand at your side like a suitcase, feet hip-width, ribs down, core braced, with space ahead to walk into.

Movement: Step forward into a lunge, lowering until both knees bend towards right angles, then drive through the front heel and bring the back foot through into the next lunge, holding the weight steady at your side throughout. Load one side for the set, then swap.

Focus: Resist the pull towards the weighted side, keeping the shoulders and hips level and the torso tall. Keep the front knee over the ankle, brace hard so you don't lean, and step under control.

3 x 10 each · 5kg
Drop landing – bilateral

Start: Stand on a low box or step, feet hip-width, ready to step off, core braced.

Movement: Step off and land softly on both feet in a quarter-squat, absorbing through the hips and knees, then stand.

Focus: Land softly and quietly, absorbing through the hips and knees, with the knees tracking over the toes, not caving in. Aim for a stable, controlled catch rather than a stiff or collapsing landing.

3 x 6 · Bodyweight
Wood chop – cable, high to low

Start: Stand side-on to a high cable, handle in both hands above one shoulder, arms extended, feet planted, core braced.

Movement: Sweep the handle down and across to the opposite hip, rotating through the trunk, then return under control.

Focus: Turn through the trunk with the hips pivoting, not twisting the lower back or knees. Keep the ribs down, drive from the core, and control the return.

3 x 12 each · 9.75kg
Curtsey lunge – bodyweight

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, hands at the chest for balance.

Movement: Step one foot back and across behind the other into a curtsey, lowering under control, then drive back up to standing. Keep the hips fairly square.

Focus: Keep the front knee tracking over the foot, not caving inward, and lower only as far as control and knee comfort allow. Keep the chest up and the hips square rather than twisting.

2 x 10 · Bodyweight
Balance board – single leg

Start: Stand on a balance board on one foot, knee soft, torso tall, near a support.

Movement: Keep the board level and steady on one leg, making constant small adjustments, for the set time.

Focus: Keep the standing knee soft and the hips level, adjusting from the ankle. Fix the eyes on a point, keep the core engaged, and keep support within reach.

3 x 30s each · Bodyweight

Goblet squat

Start: Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest with both hands, elbows tucked down, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

Movement: Sit the hips back and down into a squat, keeping the weight at the chest and the torso upright, then drive up through the heels to stand tall.

Focus: Keep the knees tracking over the toes, never caving in, and the heels flat. Keep the chest up and the weight close, and squat only as deep as you can control comfortably.

3 x 10 · 7kg
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, a dumbbell in each hand resting in front of your thighs, knees softly bent, core braced.

Movement: Push the hips back and lower the dumbbells down the front of your legs, keeping them close, until you feel the hamstrings stretch. Drive the hips forward to return.

Focus: Keep the back flat and the dumbbells close throughout. Hinge at the hips, don't squat or round the back. Go only as low as your flat back allows.

3 x 10 · 2 x 7kg
Single arm row – DB

Start: Hand and knee on a bench, the other foot planted, a dumbbell in the free hand hanging down, back flat and level.

Movement: Pull the dumbbell towards the hip, driving the elbow back and up, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Lead with the elbow and squeeze the shoulder blade, keeping the back flat and level, not twisting to lift. Keep the shoulder down, and control the lower.

3 x 10 each side · 10kg
DB chest press – single arm

Start: Lie on a bench, a dumbbell in one hand at chest height, the other arm out for balance, shoulder blades set, core braced.

Movement: Press the dumbbell up until the arm is nearly straight, then lower under control to a comfortable stretch.

Focus: Keep the shoulder blades set and resist the torso twisting towards the free side. Keep the elbow at about 45 degrees, not flared, and control the lower.

2 x 10 each · Moderate
Reverse lunge – bodyweight

Start: Stand tall, feet hip-width, hands on hips or out for balance.

Movement: Step one foot back and lower until both knees are bent towards right angles, back knee towards the floor, then drive through the front heel to return to standing.

Focus: Keep the front knee over the ankle, not drifting past the toes or caving in, and the torso tall. Lower under control, and push back up through the front heel.

3 x 8 each side · Bodyweight
Band pallof press – half kneeling

Start: Half kneel side-on to a band at chest height, inside knee down, hands at the chest, core braced.

Movement: Press the band straight out, resisting the rotational pull, hold briefly, then return to the chest.

Focus: Keep the hips and shoulders square and the torso tall, resisting any twist towards the band. Keep the core braced and the back neutral, not arching, and press with control.

3 x 10 each side · Red band
Glute bridges – single leg

Start: Lie on your back, one knee bent with the foot flat, the other leg extended straight out and held off the floor.

Movement: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to push your hips up until the body is level. Hold two seconds, then lower slowly under control.

Focus: Keep the hips level, don't let the free side drop or twist. Push with the glute, not the lower back, and keep the ribs down.

3 x 10 each side · Bodyweight
Balance – single leg free

Start: Stand on one leg without support, torso tall, other foot lifted, arms ready to help balance.

Movement: Hold the single-leg balance freely for the set time, then swap.

Focus: Keep the hips level and the standing knee soft, fixing the eyes on a point ahead. Make small ankle adjustments to stay steady, and keep the core engaged.

3 x 30s each side · Bodyweight
Step-down – single leg

Start: Stand on top of a low step on one leg, the other foot hovering off the edge, arms out for balance.

Movement: Bend the standing knee to lower the free foot slowly towards the floor, lightly tap, then drive back up to stand tall on the one leg.

Focus: Keep the standing knee tracking over the foot, never caving in, and the hips level throughout. Control the lower down slowly, and keep the movement in the standing leg, not by reaching with the free foot.

3 x 8 each side · Bodyweight
