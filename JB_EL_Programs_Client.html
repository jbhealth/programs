Compound Barbell Strength
Barbell back squat
2 × 8Barbellweight

Start: Position the bar across your upper traps (high bar) or rear delts (low bar). Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Unrack and take two steps back.

Focus: Keep your chest up and core braced throughout. Track knees over toes — push them out as you descend. Depth to parallel or below if mobility allows.

Movement: Break at the hips and knees simultaneously, descending under control. Drive through your heels to stand, extending hips and knees together. Lock out at the top without hyperextending.

Barbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10Barbellweight

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell in a double overhand grip just outside your legs. Stand tall with the bar at hip height.

Focus: This is a hip hinge — keep your knees soft but nearly straight. Feel the hamstring stretch as you lower. Keep the bar close to your body throughout.

Movement: Push your hips back and lower the bar down the front of your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch. Drive your hips forward to return to standing, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Bent-over rows – BB
2 × 10Barbell25kg

Start: Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge forward to roughly 45° with a flat back. Grip the barbell with an overhand grip.

Focus: Keep your core braced throughout — don't let your lower back round. Squeeze your shoulder blades at the top.

Movement: Pull the barbell toward your lower ribs, driving your elbows back. Lower under control.

DB single-arm press
2 × 10 eachDumbbell8kg

Start: Stand or sit with a dumbbell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing forward. Brace your core to resist the lateral pull of the uneven load.

Focus: Resist any lean to the weighted side — the anti-lateral flexion challenge is part of the exercise. Keep your torso upright throughout.

Movement: Press the dumbbell directly overhead until your arm is fully extended. Lower with control back to shoulder height. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Farmer's carries
2 × 20mKettlebell2 x 14kg

Start: Stand tall holding a heavy kettlebell or dumbbell in each hand at your sides.

Focus: Torso perfectly upright, shoulders back, core braced. Take deliberate, controlled steps.

Movement: Walk for prescribed distance or time. Keep everything tight — this is a full body stability and strength exercise.

Pallof press
2 × 10 eachCable7kg

Start: Stand side-on to a cable or band anchor at chest height.

Focus: Resist the rotation — the cable wants to pull you. Stay facing forward.

Movement: Hold cable at chest, press directly out to full arm extension. Return to chest, repeat.

Cable abductor – side step
To burn eachCable machine1.25kg

Start: Attach a cable ankle cuff to one ankle. Stand with the cable on the outside of the working leg.

Focus: Keep your standing leg slightly bent and torso upright. This is a functional abductor exercise with constant tension.

Movement: Step sideways, abducting the working leg against the cable resistance. Return under control.

Lateral band walks
2 × burnBandweight

Start: Place resistance band above knees, stand in a slight squat.

Focus: Push knees out against the band. Don't stand upright between steps.

Movement: Step sideways in small, controlled steps, staying in the squat position.
